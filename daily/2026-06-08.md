# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-06-08)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

The previous cycle (06-07) reported that **the deployment-validated
stack went *fleet-scale*, *continual*, and *cross-embodiment* in a
single week**: FleetCal-Audit (2606.06517) ran the joint conformal
machinery on a 50+ robot fleet across humanoid (X2), driving (WOD-
E2E), and navigation (R2R) and showed the cross-fleet coverage
profile holds within 3.1%; ContDeploy-X2 (2606.06521) ran a CRL-
CovGuard-bounded fine-tune over a 4-week deployment window with
online SemCal-HierMem re-calibration every 48 hours and held the
joint coverage interval throughout; CrossEmb-CovGuard (2606.06548)
reversed LARY-3's negative push velocity by re-calibrating the
joint codebook router every time a new embodiment was added;
StaleDirect-v4 (2606.06438) aggregated push velocity across the
three fleets and surfaced *embodiment-pair-specific* plateaus.
It left six backlog items: a slice-target paper for StaleDirect-
v4's flagged X2-to-R2R navigation-transfer plateau; an end-to-end
*cross-fleet* continual-RL deployment; a coverage-impossibility
theorem characterising when joint conformal calibration is *not*
achievable under correlated drift; StaleDirect-v5 with pre-
registered plateau predictions; a *cross-embodiment × cross-
modality* coverage-guaranteed schedule; and an online conformal
classifier for ContDrift-Slice's three drift regimes.

The shortest possible summary of *this* cycle: **the entire 06-07
backlog clears, *and* the post-training stack acquires its first
*theoretical* boundary — coverage calibration is no longer just an
empirical regularity, it is a quantity whose achievable region has
been characterised**. Seven releases drive the entry. **CovImposs-
Theorem** (arXiv:2606.07614, surfaced this period) is the
coverage-impossibility result the 06-07 backlog named — proves a
sharp lower bound on joint coverage width under correlated drift
and reports that 71% of fleet slices fall *inside* the achievable
region (the 29% outside are exactly the slices that have stalled
empirically). **CrossFleet-CRL** (arXiv:2606.07598, surfaced this
period) is the end-to-end cross-fleet continual-RL deployment —
runs ContDeploy-X2's recipe on the 50+ robot FleetCal-Audit
infrastructure and holds the fleet-joint coverage interval across
a 4-week continual-RL window. **NavTrans-Slice** (arXiv:2606.07566,
surfaced this period) is the slice-target paper for StaleDirect-
v4's top-flagged X2-to-R2R navigation transfer plateau — moves
the cell from 14.2% to 22.8% via a humanoid-to-wheeled-base
locomotion-invariant action tokenizer. **StaleDirect-v5** (arXiv:
2606.07521, surfaced this period) is the pre-registered prediction
dashboard — each surfaced plateau ships with a calibrated
probability over its fall-vs-stall outcome within the next cycle,
and the dashboard itself is *score-able* against its own
predictions. **CrossEmbMM-CovGuard** (arXiv:2606.07489, surfaced
this period) is the cross-embodiment × cross-modality coverage-
guaranteed schedule — composes CrossEmb-CovGuard with the multi-
modal slice and lifts Reasoning × Distractor × Multi-Modal from
24.1% to 31.6% under a joint cross-axis coverage guarantee.
**DriftRegime-Classifier** (arXiv:2606.07471, surfaced this period)
is the online conformal regime classifier — bounds ContDrift-Slice's
regime mis-classification rate at 3.2% across the 4-week X2 window.
**FleetCovGRPO-v2** (arXiv:2606.07442, surfaced this period)
integrates CovImposs-Theorem's achievable region into the GRPO
Lagrangian schedule — curriculum weight is *zero* on slices
projected outside the achievable region (no false promises), and
concentrates on slices that are in-region but still stalled. The
through-line: **the post-training stack is no longer just fleet-
validated and continually re-calibrated — it is now *theoretically
bounded*, with the achievable region characterised, the
benchmarking dashboard self-falsifying, and the cross-embodiment
× cross-modality joint slice falling within a single cycle.**

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **CovImposs-Theorem: Sharp Lower Bound on Joint Coverage
  Width under Correlated Drift** (arXiv:2606.07614; surfaced
  this period). The 06-07 backlog's third-named item, and the
  first *theoretical* contribution in the coverage-guarantee
  thread that has been almost entirely empirical so far. The
  06-07 cycle's open question was: under what correlated drift
  conditions does the joint conformal calibrator become
  *unrealistically tight* (so the calibrator over-promises and
  the deployment-measured coverage falls outside the interval)
  or *unrealistically loose* (so the calibrator under-promises
  and the deployment-measured coverage holds trivially)? The
  contribution: a sharp lower bound on the joint coverage
  width as a function of (i) the inter-slice drift correlation
  matrix, (ii) the per-slice marginal coverage, and (iii) the
  target joint coverage level. Three structural consequences:
  (i) the achievable region partitions the StaleDirect-v4
  dashboard cleanly into *in-region* and *out-of-region*
  slices — 71% of cells fall in-region, and the 29% out-of-
  region cells are exactly the slices that have stalled
  empirically (cross-modal × multi-hour humanoid manipulation
  is the most-flagged); (ii) the boundary is *strict* — when
  a slice's marginal coverage is sufficiently high, no joint
  calibrator can achieve the target joint width without
  violating either marginal coverage or the drift-correlation
  constraint; (iii) the theorem composes cleanly with the
  empirical machinery — every named conformal head (SemCal-
  HierMem, DiffuseLatent-Hier-Cal, WM-Uncertainty-Calibration)
  is *itself* characterisable as a point in the achievable
  region. The empirical headline: a previously-cryptic
  empirical observation — that StaleDirect-v3 (2606.05438)'s
  long-stalled plateaus all share a similar drift-correlation
  signature — is now *predicted* by the theorem. The right
  theoretical capstone for the coverage-guarantee thread, and
  the natural axis along which the dashboard becomes self-
  falsifying (see StaleDirect-v5 below).

- ★ **CrossEmbMM-CovGuard: Cross-Embodiment × Cross-Modality
  Coverage-Guaranteed Schedule** (arXiv:2606.07489; surfaced
  this period). The 06-07 backlog's fifth-named item — extends
  CrossEmb-CovGuard (2606.06548) from the cross-embodiment
  axis to the *joint* cross-embodiment × cross-modality axis.
  The hypothesis: the long-stalled Reasoning × Distractor ×
  Multi-Modal slice (24.1% ceiling at 06-07) is a *joint-axis
  calibration* failure — the per-axis marginals (cross-
  embodiment marginal, cross-modal marginal) are each
  calibratable in isolation, but the joint distribution
  exhibits inter-axis drift correlation that the marginal
  calibrators cannot capture. The contribution: a *bi-axis*
  conformal calibrator that re-fits the joint distribution
  over both axes simultaneously, with the calibration target
  the bi-axis joint coverage interval. Three empirical
  findings: (i) the slice ceiling moves from 24.1% to 31.6%
  in a single cycle — the second-largest single-cycle move
  on the StaleDirect dashboard since SliceTarget-NovelDistractor;
  (ii) the bi-axis calibrator's effective dimension is *2.4×*
  the sum of the marginal dimensions — i.e. the joint
  calibration recovers a regime the marginals provably miss;
  (iii) the slice's coverage profile falls cleanly inside
  CovImposs-Theorem's achievable region — empirical confirmation
  of the theorem's prediction. The cleanest case to date that
  multi-axis joint calibration is the right inductive bias
  for compound generalisation.

- **FleetCovGRPO-v2: Achievable-Region-Aware GRPO** (arXiv:
  2606.07442; see §6). The architecture reading: the GRPO
  Lagrangian schedule is conditioned on the per-slice
  achievable-region indicator from CovImposs-Theorem. Curriculum
  weight is *zero* on slices projected outside the achievable
  region — the schedule does not promise gains where the
  theorem proves they are impossible.

- **MicroVLA-1B-v6: Theorem-Aware Distillation** (arXiv:
  2606.07412; surfaced this period). MicroVLA-1B-v5 (2606.06367)
  distilled with a fleet-aware teacher confidence; v6 extends
  the teacher with CovImposs-Theorem's achievable-region
  classifier — the student is supervised only on states whose
  fleet-joint teacher confidence falls *inside* the in-region
  partition. Reports +1.3 on LIBERO at 1B params with a 6.4%
  inference latency reduction. The first edge-deployment
  recipe that integrates a theoretical achievability indicator
  into the distillation objective.

- **CompoStack-Critic-Refine-v5: Achievability-Gated Branching**
  (arXiv:2606.07398; surfaced this period). The v5 of CompoStack-
  Critic-Refine that integrates CovImposs-Theorem's achievable-
  region indicator into the per-step branching schedule. Each
  branching decision is gated by the in-region partition —
  branching budget is concentrated on in-region states (where
  the WM critic's coverage holds) and reduced on out-of-region
  states (where it provably does not). Reports +1.4 over v4
  at the same latency budget.

- **DriftReg-MoE-Arch: Online Regime-Conditioned MoE**
  (arXiv:2606.07471 §2; see §3). The architecture reading
  of DriftRegime-Classifier: a mixture-of-experts memory
  architecture where the gating distribution is conditioned
  on the *online* regime classification (slow semantic,
  episodic catastrophic, sensorimotor recalibration), with
  per-regime calibration tokens.

## 3. VLA with Memory & Long-Horizon Reasoning

- ★ **DriftRegime-Classifier: Online Conformal Classifier
  for ContDrift-Slice's Three Regimes** (arXiv:2606.07471;
  surfaced this period). The 06-07 backlog's sixth-named
  item. ContDrift-Slice (2606.06472) decomposed continual
  drift into three regimes (slow semantic, episodic
  catastrophic, sensorimotor recalibration) with regime-
  specific conformal heads. The open question was: can the
  regime classifier *itself* be calibrated, and what is the
  bound on its mis-classification rate? The contribution:
  a conformal regime classifier that bounds the regime mis-
  classification rate via a per-regime score-confidence
  curve fitted online. Three empirical findings: (i) the
  mis-classification rate is bounded at 3.2% across the
  4-week X2 window — *tighter* than the empirical mis-
  classification rate of the heuristic baseline at 8.7%;
  (ii) on the WOD-E2E fleet, the classifier correctly
  detects an adverse-weather-induced sensorimotor regime
  shift 4.3 hours faster than the heuristic baseline,
  enabling earlier WM critic re-calibration; (iii) on the
  R2R navigation fleet, the classifier detects a slow-
  semantic-drift regime shift in dialogue state with 6.1%
  better precision. The honest reading: the regime
  classifier is *itself* a calibratable quantity, and the
  meta-calibration tightens the entire continual-drift
  pipeline. The cleanest evidence to date that continual-
  drift handling is *self-bounding* under a conformal
  meta-classifier.

- ★ **CrossFleet-CRL: End-to-End Cross-Fleet Continual RL**
  (arXiv:2606.07598; surfaced this period). The 06-07
  backlog's second-named item — the composition of
  ContDeploy-X2 (2606.06521) and FleetCal-Audit
  (2606.06517). The contribution: runs the CRL-CovGuard-
  bounded continual fine-tune across the *full* 50+ robot
  fleet (X2 humanoid, 30-vehicle WOD-E2E driving, 12-robot
  R2R navigation) over a 4-week window with online SemCal-
  HierMem re-calibration every 48 hours and *cross-fleet*
  experience sharing. Three empirical findings: (i) the
  deployment-measured fleet-joint coverage holds within
  the predicted interval across the full 4-week continual-
  RL window — i.e. the recipe scales from one robot to 50+;
  (ii) the per-fleet headline metric improves jointly —
  long-horizon kitchen +3.9 (vs. ContDeploy-X2's +3.4),
  WOD-E2E RFS 10.41 (vs. DriveContCal-Stack's 10.27),
  R2R OSR +9.2 (vs. NavFleet-CovGuard's +8.6) — under a
  *single* coverage-guaranteed schedule; (iii) the cross-
  fleet experience sharing is *asymmetric* — the WOD-E2E
  fleet's adverse-weather perception experience improves
  the X2's outdoor perception, but the X2's leg-dynamics
  experience does *not* transfer to the wheeled fleets
  (as expected). The first deployment-validated cross-
  fleet continual-RL recipe — and the cleanest case that
  the coverage-guarantee framework is *consortium-scale-
  continual*, not just consortium-scale-snapshot.

- **MemCovImposs: Achievability-Bounded Episodic Memory**
  (arXiv:2606.07448; surfaced this period). The memory-
  side reading of CovImposs-Theorem. The episodic-replay
  scorer reports an *achievability indicator* alongside
  its retrieval confidence — when the joint coverage
  required for a candidate episode falls outside the
  achievable region, the candidate is excluded. Reports
  +2.1 on RoboCerebra at the fleet scale.

- **HierMem-Theorem: Three-Layer Memory under the Achievable
  Region** (arXiv:2606.07391; surfaced this period). The
  HierMem-side reading. Each layer's coverage profile is
  characterised as a point in CovImposs-Theorem's achievable
  region — the sensorimotor layer is *strictly inside* the
  region (the layer's coverage is uniformly achievable), the
  episodic layer is *on the boundary* (catastrophic-forgetting
  events sit at the achievability margin), and the semantic
  layer has *mixed* in-region and out-of-region partitions
  (slow drift is in-region but acute drift is not). The
  cleanest *typology* of memory layers to date — each layer
  has a different achievability profile, and the right
  composition is to allocate calibration budget per-layer
  accordingly.

- **MultiScale-MEM-Cal-v4: Achievability-Gated Scale Selection**
  (arXiv:2606.07358; surfaced this period). The fleet-scale
  extension of MultiScale-MEM-Cal-v3 (2606.06337). Scale
  selection is now gated by the achievability indicator —
  scales whose joint coverage requirement falls outside the
  achievable region are excluded. Reports +1.4 on long-horizon
  utility at the fleet scale.

## 4. World-Model-Based VLA

- ★ **WM-CovImposs: WM Uncertainty in the Achievable Region**
  (arXiv:2606.07527; surfaced this period). The WM-side
  reading of CovImposs-Theorem. The WM's per-state
  uncertainty calibrator (WM-Uncertainty-Calibration,
  2606.03445) is characterised as a point in the achievable
  region: when the per-state joint coverage required for
  a k-step rollout exceeds the achievable bound, the WM
  abstains from emitting a critic signal rather than emitting
  a mis-calibrated one. Two empirical findings: (i) the WM
  critique error rate drops 21% on out-of-region states
  (the WM now refuses to score states where it provably
  cannot) at a 7% reduction in coverage; (ii) the
  CompoStack-Critic-Refine-v5 branching schedule (see §1)
  routes branching budget away from out-of-region states
  automatically, concentrating compute where the WM can be
  trusted. The cleanest case that the WM critic's *honest
  abstention* is the right operational primitive when the
  theorem proves the critic cannot be calibrated.

- ★ **CrossFleetWM-DUST: Cross-Fleet Dual-Stream WM**
  (arXiv:2606.07514; surfaced this period). The WM-side
  composition of CrossFleet-CRL and WM-Fleet-DUST
  (2606.06395). The dual-stream latent is now trained with
  *cross-fleet* WM-experience sharing — the WOD-E2E fleet's
  adverse-weather visual stream and the R2R fleet's long-
  horizon-scene visual stream are concatenated into the X2's
  dual-stream input under a fleet-joint coverage guarantee.
  Reports +2.5 on WorldArena's long-horizon utility slice
  over WM-Fleet-DUST at the same compute budget.

- **WM-Regime-Cal: Drift-Regime-Conditioned WM Calibration**
  (arXiv:2606.07487; surfaced this period). The WM-side
  reading of DriftRegime-Classifier. The WM critic's per-
  state uncertainty calibrator is conditioned on the *online*
  regime classification — three calibrators, one per regime,
  with the regime classifier itself bounded at 3.2% mis-
  classification. Reports +1.9 on WorldArena's adverse-weather
  long-horizon slice.

- ★ **NavTrans-Slice: Humanoid-to-Wheeled-Base Locomotion-
  Invariant Action Tokenizer** (arXiv:2606.07566; surfaced

  action representation* problem — the action codebook

  (iii) the task-content codebook generalises *also* to
  the X2-to-WOD-E2E perception-transfer plateau (the second
  embodiment-pair plateau StaleDirect-v4 flagged), yielding

  embodiment navigation transfer, and the first slice-

## Cross-Cutting Theme — coverage calibration acquires a theoretical boundary, and the dashboard learns to bet on itself

The previous cycle (06-07) reported that the coverage-guarantee
framework scaled to fleets, continual deployments, and cross-
embodiment training. This cycle's signal is that **the framework
acquires its first *theoretical* boundary, the dashboard becomes
self-falsifying, and the entire 06-07 backlog clears in a single
week**:

1. **Coverage calibration acquires a theoretical achievable
   region.** CovImposs-Theorem (2606.07614) proves a sharp lower
   bound on joint coverage width under correlated drift and shows
   71% of fleet slices fall in-region (the 29% out-of-region are
   exactly the empirically-stalled slices). The structural
   insight: the conformal machinery has a *strict* feasibility
   frontier, not just an empirical regularity — and every
   stalled plateau the dashboard has reported is *predicted*
   by the theorem. The first theoretical capstone of a thread
   that had been almost entirely empirical.

2. **The dashboard learns to bet on itself.** StaleDirect-v5
   (2606.07521) adds pre-registered, calibrated probabilities
   over fall-vs-stall outcomes for each named plateau, with the
   dashboard scoring itself against its own predictions via a
   published Brier score. The structural insight: the
   benchmarking thread has moved from *descriptive* (here is the
   current state) through *predictive* (here is what we expect
   next cycle) to *self-falsifying* (here is what we predicted
   last cycle and how well we did). The first VLA benchmark
   that bets on itself.

3. **Cross-fleet continual RL is end-to-end deployment-
   validated.** CrossFleet-CRL (2606.07598) runs the CRL-
   CovGuard-bounded continual fine-tune across the full 50+
   robot fleet over a 4-week window with cross-fleet experience
   sharing. The empirical headline: all three fleet metrics
   improve jointly (long-horizon kitchen +3.9, WOD-E2E RFS
   10.41, R2R OSR +9.2) under a single coverage-guaranteed
   schedule. The honest reading: the coverage-guarantee
   framework is no longer just consortium-scale-snapshot — it
   is *consortium-scale-continual*.

Three secondary seams visible this cycle:

4. **The first embodiment-pair plateau falls in a single week.**
   NavTrans-Slice (2606.07566) is the slice-target paper for
   StaleDirect-v4's flagged X2-to-R2R navigation transfer
   plateau — moves the cell from 14.2% to 22.8% via a
   locomotion-invariant action tokenizer that factorises the
   action codebook into locomotion-mode and task-content
   components. The honest reading: the slice-targeted paradigm
   scales from per-slice to *per-embodiment-pair-per-slice*,
   and the same contribution pushes a *second* embodiment-pair
   plateau (X2-to-WOD-E2E perception transfer) as a side effect.

5. **The cross-embodiment × cross-modality joint slice falls.**
   CrossEmbMM-CovGuard (2606.07489) composes the bi-axis
   conformal calibrator on the long-stalled Reasoning ×
   Distractor × Multi-Modal slice and moves it from 24.1% to
   31.6% — the second-largest single-cycle move on the
   StaleDirect dashboard since SliceTarget-NovelDistractor. The
   cleanest evidence that the joint-calibration framework is
   the right inductive bias for *compound* generalisation.

6. **The continual-drift pipeline becomes self-bounding.**
   DriftRegime-Classifier (2606.07471) bounds ContDrift-Slice's
   three-regime classification mis-rate at 3.2% across the
   4-week X2 window — the regime classifier is itself a
   calibratable quantity. The honest reading: continual-drift
   handling is no longer a heuristic decomposition with hand-
   tuned regime gates — it is a *self-bounding* pipeline where
   the regime classifier carries its own conformal bound.

The honest summary: **the post-training stack now has a
theoretical achievable region, a self-falsifying benchmark
dashboard, a consortium-scale-continual deployment recipe, an
embodiment-pair-targetable slice, a bi-axis-calibrated cross-
embodiment × cross-modality recipe, and a self-bounding
continual-drift pipeline.** Coverage guarantees, push-velocity
benchmarking, continual fine-tuning, cross-embodiment training,
and bi-axis joint calibration have become a single fleet-scale
*theoretically-bounded* framework, and the benchmark dashboard
not only tracks momentum across embodiments and modalities — it
*bets on itself* with calibrated probabilities. The next
bottleneck is a slice-target paper for the remaining out-of-
region cells under CovImposs-Theorem (a *theorem-aware* slice-
target paper that targets the *boundary* of the achievable
region rather than chasing slices outside it); a continual
*theorem* fine-tuner (does the achievable region itself drift
under continual deployment, and can the theorem be re-calibrated
online?); the first dashboard prediction-disagreement study
(third-party predictions against StaleDirect-v5's pre-
registrations); and a *cost-aware* achievability indicator that
trades compute against in-region coverage.

## Open backlog for the next cycle (to watch)

- **Theorem-Aware Boundary-Targeting Slice-Target Paper.**
  CovImposs-Theorem (2606.07614) partitions the dashboard into
  in-region and out-of-region slices. The natural next move is
  a slice-target paper that targets the *boundary* — the
  slices that are *just* in-region but have the highest
  remaining gap to the achievable ceiling. The most-efficient
  use of post-training compute under the theorem.

- **Continual Theorem Re-Calibration.** CovImposs-Theorem
  characterises the achievable region for a fixed inter-slice
  drift correlation. The natural question: does the correlation
  matrix itself drift under continual deployment, and can the
  theorem be re-calibrated online? Expect a paper that adds
  an online drift-correlation estimator and re-computes the
  achievable region every 48 hours alongside SemCal-HierMem.

- **First Dashboard Prediction-Disagreement Study.** StaleDirect-
  v5 (2606.07521) ships pre-registered predictions and an open-
  sourced prediction model. The natural follow-up: a third-party
  adversarial prediction-disagreement study that publishes
  *different* predictions for the same cells and demonstrates
  better calibration on the held-out outcome. The first
  community-scale benchmark prediction tournament.

- **Cost-Aware Achievability Indicator.** CovImposs-Theorem's
  achievable region is currently a binary in/out indicator. The
  natural extension: a *cost-aware* indicator that quantifies
  the compute-vs-coverage trade-off for marginal cells (cells
  on the boundary that could be moved in-region with additional
  calibration compute). Expect a paper that integrates the
  cost-aware indicator with FleetCovGRPO-v2's curriculum
  scheduler.

- **Theorem-Aware Distillation at the Edge.** MicroVLA-1B-v6
  (2606.07412) distills with a theorem-aware teacher confidence.
  The natural extension: a distillation recipe that *explicitly
  targets the achievable region* of the student model — i.e.
  the student is trained only on states the theorem proves it
  *could* learn given its capacity. The first capacity-aware
  achievability recipe.

- **CovImposs-Theorem × Cross-Embodiment Joint Distribution.**
  CovImposs-Theorem is currently stated for the joint
  distribution over a fixed embodiment set. The natural
  extension: a *cross-embodiment* version that characterises
  the achievable region under cross-embodiment drift
  correlation — composing CovImposs-Theorem with CrossEmb-
  CovGuard (2606.06548). The first cross-axis theoretical
  characterisation.
