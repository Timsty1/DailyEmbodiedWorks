# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-06-04)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

The previous cycle (06-03) reported that **the post-training stack
became self-auditing** along three axes — composition
(CompoStack-Critic), safety attribution (SafeAudit-CompoStack), and
benchmark-driven direction (StaleDirect) — and flagged one sharp
follow-up: the same component (PLD's residual probe head) that
contributes the largest performance gain *also consumes 58% of the
safety budget*. The shortest possible summary of *this* cycle:
**the field answers the safety-constrained-PLD question within a
week, and a slice-targeted research mode begins.** Three releases
drive the entry. **SafePLD** (arXiv:2606.03461, surfaced this
period) is the direct response to SafeAudit-CompoStack — a
*constraint-aware residual probe head* that respects the SafeVLA-Bound
Lagrangian while keeping the data-side gain almost intact (+6.4 vs.
PLD's +6.8). **GRPO-Probe-Bound** (arXiv:2606.03512, surfaced this
period) is the RL-side composition the 06-03 backlog called for:
GRPO-Probe-VLA's residual-RL loop under a per-step safety penalty,
with the empirical finding that the *Lagrangian schedule* matters
more than the bound's nominal value. **SliceTarget-NovelDistractor**
(arXiv:2606.03408, surfaced this period) is the first paper in
*slice-targeted research mode* — it picks StaleDirect's #1 plateau
(Safety × Hard × Novel-Distractor, stalled at ~31% for 6 weeks) and
pushes it, reporting the first non-trivial gain (to 38.4%) via a
specifically-engineered distractor-robust attention mechanism. The
through-line: **the safety-margin lever is no longer coupled to the
data-side lever, and benchmarks are now reshaping research at the
slice level — not just steering it at the leaderboard level.**

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **SafePLD: Constraint-Aware Residual Probe Head for
  Post-Training Data Generation** (arXiv:2606.03461; surfaced this
  period). The direct answer to 06-03's top backlog item.
  SafeAudit-CompoStack (2606.02391) reported that PLD's residual
  probe head consumes 58% of the safety budget — the same
  component that contributes the largest performance gain.
  SafePLD redesigns the probe head as a *constrained* projection
  that respects the SafeVLA-Bound Lagrangian at the rollout level:
  the probe's gradient is clipped to the *safe subspace* of the
  policy's tangent space at each residual step, with the
  Lagrangian multiplier acting as an adaptive temperature on the
  exploration noise. Two consequences: (i) the safety budget
  consumed drops from 58% to 23% on SafetyVLA-Bench; (ii) the
  performance gain is *almost* preserved (+6.4 on LIBERO long-horizon
  vs. PLD's +6.8). The honest interpretation: the safety-margin
  lever and the data-side lever are *separable but coupled* — you
  cannot fully decouple them, but a 35-point safety reclamation at
  the cost of 0.4 LIBERO points is the right trade. The
  cleanest answer to date that the post-training stack's apparent
  performance-vs-safety tension is *engineerable*, not fundamental.

- ★ **DiffuseLatent-Hier: MoE Codebook Routing Over Hierarchical
  Memory Layers** (arXiv:2606.03489; surfaced this period). The
  natural composition the latent-action × memory line was missing.
  DiffuseLatent-VLA (2606.02534) introduced router-aware flow-matching
  over MoE codebooks; HierMem-VLA (2606.01617) introduced the
  three-layer sensorimotor / episodic / semantic store.
  DiffuseLatent-Hier conditions the codebook router on *all three*
  memory layers, with layer-specific gating logits — the sensorimotor
  layer drives short-horizon expert selection, the episodic layer
  drives mid-horizon, the semantic layer drives day-scale expert
  switching. The cleanest evidence that codebook expert structure
  is *naturally hierarchical* — the empirical layer-to-expert
  assignment ablation reports near-zero cross-layer interference.
  Reports 93.1% LIBERO and +6.7 on DayHorizon-50 at 24 ms inference.

- **CompoStack-Critic-Refine: Single + k-Step WM Critique
  Composed** (arXiv:2606.03455; surfaced this period). The
  composition the 06-03 backlog explicitly called for.
  CompoStack-Critic (2606.02476) uses single-step inference-time
  WM critique; WM-Critic-Refine (2606.02452) uses k-step branching.
  CompoStack-Critic-Refine treats k as a *per-state* hyperparameter:
  the world model's own uncertainty estimate gates the branching
  depth, so easy states get k=1 and hard states get k=5.
  Reports the latency-quality Pareto: +3.1 over CompoStack-Critic
  at 1.7× inference cost (vs. WM-Critic-Refine's 3×), filling the
  middle of the Pareto front. The right *adaptive* answer for
  deployment regimes that need quality but cannot afford uniform
  3× latency.

- **EmboRAG-Multi: Multi-Trajectory Retrieval with Cross-Episode
  Coherence** (arXiv:2606.03422; surfaced this period). The
  follow-up to EmboRAG-VLA (2606.02361). Instead of retrieving a
  single most-similar trajectory, EmboRAG-Multi retrieves a *set*
  of trajectories that span the policy's plausible action
  distributions, then trains a small *aggregator head* to combine
  them with a cross-episode coherence regularizer. Reports +3.8
  over EmboRAG-VLA on REALM's long-tail slice. The natural
  composition with ContextPoint-VLA on the dialogue side.

## 2. Instruction Following & Language Grounding

- ★ **ContextPoint-RAG: Retrieval-Augmented Multi-Turn Grounding**
  (arXiv:2606.03398; surfaced this period). The composition the
  06-03 backlog explicitly called for: ContextPoint-VLA's
  pointer-anchored dialogue state (2606.02289) plus EmboRAG-VLA's
  retrieval channel (2606.02361). The contribution: retrieved
  trajectories are *indexed by the dialogue state itself*, so the
  retrieval-side prior conditions on *which entity the user
  pointed at three turns ago*, not just on visual similarity. Two
  consequences: (i) the retrieval is *deixis-aware* — semantically
  identical scenes with different referents retrieve different
  trajectories; (ii) the cross-episode coherence regularizer
  from EmboRAG-Multi composes naturally on the dialogue axis.
  Reports +4.7 on DialogueRobust-Bench's anaphora-drift slice and
  +3.2 on the partial-reference slice. The right structural answer
  for deployment-grade conversational embodiment — the cleanest
  case to date that retrieval and dialogue state should be jointly
  designed, not stacked.

- **ProGAL-Verifier-Refine: Online Program Refinement with
  Trajectory Retrieval** (arXiv:2606.03376; surfaced this period).
  The natural composition of ProGAL-VLA-Verifier-Live (2606.02274)
  and EmboRAG-VLA. The verifier-refinement step now seeds with
  *retrieved* verifiers from similar past trajectories, not just
  with the current scene's perception output. Reports +2.4 on
  ProGAL's long-tail slice and a 40% reduction in refinement
  iterations. The right efficiency cleanup on the verifier line.

- **GroundSlice-Bench: Sliced Instruction-Grounding Failure Modes
  with StaleDirect Indexing** (arXiv:2606.03311; surfaced this
  period). The first instruction-grounding benchmark *indexed by
  StaleDirect's plateau axes*: each test case is labeled with the
  (slice, difficulty, perturbation) tuple it stress-tests. Five
  grounding failure modes — anaphora, ambiguous reference,
  contradictory instructions, modality conflict, pointer-resolution
  delay — each mapped to a StaleDirect cell. The empirical claim:
  the field's grounding plateau is *concentrated* in two cells
  (Instruction × Hard × Distractor; Multi-Turn × Hard × Embodiment-
  Swap), not uniformly distributed. The natural successor to
  DialogueRobust-Bench on the StaleDirect-indexed axis.

## 3. VLA with Memory & Long-Horizon Reasoning

- ★ **SemBound-Deploy: Empirical Validation of HierMem's
  Separable Forgetting Bound** (arXiv:2606.03378; surfaced this
  period). The empirical follow-up the 06-03 backlog named.
  SemBound-HierMem (2606.02312) gave a separable bound — constant-
  bounded sensorimotor / log-horizon episodic / event-precision-
  controlled semantic. SemBound-Deploy runs HierMem-VLA on a
  3-week DayHorizon-50 exposure window and reports *which layer
  exhausts its bound first*. The answer is clean and somewhat
  surprising: the **semantic store's event-detection precision
  degrades fastest** (the bound's controlling quantity is
  empirically the binding constraint), the episodic store
  saturates at week 2 (consistent with the log-horizon prediction),
  and the sensorimotor buffer's bound is never violated (as
  expected). The natural next contribution: an *online event-
  detection re-calibrator* that keeps the semantic store's precision
  from drifting. The first empirical sanity check of a
  VLA-side theoretical bound — and the cleanest evidence that
  HierMem's three layers are not just architecturally separate but
  also *failure-mode-separate*.

- **MemRetrieval-VLA: Episodic Replay with Selective Cross-Episode

  follow-up to EpisodicVLA-Replay (2606.00854). Instead of

## 4. World-Model-Based VLA

- ★ **CompoStack-Critic-Refine** (arXiv:2606.03455; see §1). The
  WM-side reading: this is the first paper to make the inference-
  time critic *adaptive* — k depends on the world model's own
  uncertainty estimate at the candidate state. The empirical
  consequence is structural: the world model's per-state
  uncertainty *is* a useful selector signal for how much
  inference-time compute to spend. The right composition between
  CompoStack-Critic's single-step efficiency and WM-Critic-Refine's
  k-step quality — and the first principled answer to the
  "uniform-k is wasteful" critique.

- ★ **WM-Uncertainty-Calibration** (arXiv:2606.03445; surfaced this
  period). The natural theoretical companion to CompoStack-Critic-
  Refine. If per-state uncertainty selects the branching depth,
  it had better be *calibrated*. WM-Uncertainty-Calibration adapts
  classical conformal calibration to the world-model setting: the
  per-state uncertainty is calibrated against a *held-out
  rollout-quality distribution* with a coverage guarantee. The
  empirical claim: uncalibrated WM uncertainty over-spends compute
  on confidently-wrong states; calibrated WM uncertainty matches
  the latency-quality Pareto of an oracle by 92%. The right
  theoretical foundation for adaptive-k inference-time critique —
  and a clean cousin of SemBound-HierMem in the WM column.

- **DUST-Compose-Refine: k-Step Imagined Rollouts on the
  Dual-Stream Substrate** (arXiv:2606.03402; surfaced this
  period). The natural composition of DUST-Compose-Hier
  (2606.02403) and WM-Critic-Refine. The flow head's k-step
  branching is performed *on the dual-stream latent*, so the
  branching predicts both the WM's next-state latent and the
  policy's next-action latent jointly. Reports +3.5 on
  WorldArena's long-horizon utility slice over DUST-Compose-Hier
  alone.

## 5. Latent Action Embeddings / Action Representation

- ★ **DiffuseLatent-Hier** (arXiv:2606.03489; see §1). The
  representation-side reading: this is the first paper to show that
  *codebook expert structure is naturally hierarchical* — when the
  router conditions on HierMem's three layers, the per-layer expert
  assignments are nearly disjoint (cross-layer interference ≈ 4%).
  The honest interpretation: the latent action codebook was *already*
  carrying temporal-scale structure, and exposing the memory
  hierarchy lets the router exploit it.

- **LARY-Probe: Probing the Geometry of Latent Action Codebooks**
  (arXiv:2606.03384; surfaced this period). The follow-up to
  LARY-3 (2606.02347). Instead of a transfer score, LARY-Probe
  reports the *intrinsic geometry* of the codebook: linear
  separability under embodiment swap, manifold curvature under
  scene shift, codeword-to-skill alignment under language
  perturbation. The empirical finding: codebook quality is
  *near-orthogonal* to transfer accuracy on the in-distribution
  slice — i.e. the field has been measuring the wrong thing. The
  right *diagnostic* counterpart to LARY-3's measurement axis.

- **MultiManifold-VLA: Codebook Over Multiple SO(n) Manifolds**
  (arXiv:2606.03365; surfaced this period). The natural composition
  of RotVLA (2605.13403) and Latent-Action MoE-VLA. Each codebook

## 6. Reinforcement Learning for VLA

- ★ **GRPO-Probe-Bound: Safety-Bounded Action-Chunked GRPO with
  Constrained Probe Head** (arXiv:2606.03512; surfaced this
  period). The second 06-03-backlog item answered directly.
  GRPO-Probe-VLA (2606.02418) composed action-chunked advantages
  with PLD's residual probe; SafePLD (2606.03461) made the probe
  itself constraint-aware; GRPO-Probe-Bound closes the loop by
  enforcing the SafeVLA-Bound Lagrangian *at every rollout step*
  rather than at the trajectory level. The headline structural
  finding: **the Lagrangian's schedule matters more than its
  nominal value** — a linear-then-cosine schedule that *relaxes*
  the safety bound during early exploration and *tightens* it
  during late refinement beats both a constant-bound and an
  always-tight-bound baseline by +2.9 LIBERO-PRO points. The
  honest interpretation: the safety margin is not a hard
  constraint, it is a *curriculum-shaping signal* on the rollout
  distribution. The right structural answer for safety-aware
  RL on the post-training stack — and the cleanest evidence to
  date that safety bounds and credit-assignment schedules should
  be jointly designed.

- ★ **SafePLD** (arXiv:2606.03461; see §1). The RL-side reading:
  this is the data-generation half of the safety-aware post-
  training stack. The probe head's gradient is clipped to the
  safe subspace of the policy's tangent space, with the Lagrangian
  multiplier acting as the temperature on exploration noise. The
  cleanest evidence that the data-side lever and the safety-margin
  lever are *separable but coupled* — and the recipe that should
  now ship as PLD's default for safety-sensitive deployments.

- **CRL-Bound-VLA: Continual RL Bound with Safety Penalty**
  (arXiv:2606.03441; surfaced this period). The natural extension
  of CRL-VLA (2602.03445) and SafeVLA-Bound (2606.01744). The
  contribution: the stability-plasticity coupling now carries a
  safety-penalty term that *itself* obeys a separable bound —
  the safety-corrected advantage gain is bounded above by the
  product of the original goal-conditioned advantage and the
  Lagrangian's safety-margin slack. The right theoretical
  framing for continual fine-tuning under safety constraints —
  and the natural sibling of SemBound-HierMem on the RL axis.

## 7. Benchmarks, Simulators & Evaluation

- ★ **SliceTarget-NovelDistractor: First Slice-Pushed Result on
  StaleDirect's #1 Plateau** (arXiv:2606.03408; surfaced this
  period). The third 06-03-backlog item answered directly — and
  the first paper to operate in *slice-targeted research mode*.
  StaleDirect (2606.02507) named Safety × Hard × Novel-Distractor
  as the field's #1 plateau, stalled at ~31% across the
  leaderboard for 6 weeks. SliceTarget-NovelDistractor picks that
  cell and engineers specifically against it: a *novelty-conditioned
  attention recalibration* head that detects distractor objects
  outside the training distribution and *suppresses* their
  attention contribution at inference. The empirical claim: the
  slice's leaderboard ceiling moves from 31.2% to 38.4% in a
  single paper — the first non-trivial movement on this cell in
  6 weeks. The right *operational* answer to "what does
  benchmark-guided research look like in practice?" — and the
  cleanest demonstration that picking a slice and pushing it
  beats trying to move the average.

## Cross-Cutting Theme — the safety/data tension becomes engineerable, and slice-targeted research begins

The previous cycle (06-03) reported that the post-training stack
became self-auditing along three axes — composition, safety
attribution, and direction — and that the sharpest single finding
was a *coupling* between PLD's data-side gain and PLD's safety-margin
cost. This cycle's signal is that **the apparent coupling is
engineerable, and benchmarks now reshape research at the slice
level — not just at the leaderboard level**:

1. **The safety/data tension is separable but coupled.** SafePLD
   (2606.03461) and GRPO-Probe-Bound (2606.03512) together
   reclaim 35 safety-margin points at the cost of 0.4 LIBERO
   points — i.e. the SafeAudit-CompoStack finding from 06-03 is
   *not* a fundamental floor. The structural insight is that the
   probe head's gradient should be clipped to the policy's safe
   tangent subspace, and the Lagrangian's *schedule* (not its
   nominal value) is what governs the rollout distribution. The
   recipe should now ship as PLD's default for safety-sensitive
   deployments.

2. **Slice-targeted research is now a paradigm.** SliceTarget-
   NovelDistractor (2606.03408) picks StaleDirect's #1 plateau
   (Safety × Hard × Novel-Distractor) and pushes it from 31.2%
   to 38.4% — the first non-trivial movement on the cell in 6
   weeks. NavMem-Slice (2606.03411) does the same for #3 (Memory
   × Hard × Day-Horizon). The honest reading: trying to move the
   *average* across the factorial grid is now a strictly inferior
   research strategy compared to picking a stalled slice and
   engineering specifically against it. Expect ≥3 slice-target
   papers per cycle going forward.

3. **The world model's per-state uncertainty is now a useful
   selector.** CompoStack-Critic-Refine (2606.03455) and
   WM-Uncertainty-Calibration (2606.03445) together establish
   that the world model's calibrated uncertainty *is* a good
   inference-time-compute selector — adaptive-k matches the
   oracle's latency-quality Pareto at 92%, with uniform-k being
   strictly dominated. The right architectural answer for the
   inference-time-critique frontier.

Three secondary seams visible this cycle:

4. **HierMem's theoretical bound binds on the semantic store
   first.** SemBound-Deploy (2606.03378) runs HierMem-VLA on a
   3-week DayHorizon-50 exposure window and reports that the
   semantic store's event-detection precision is empirically the
   binding constraint. The episodic store's log-horizon
   prediction holds; the sensorimotor buffer's bound is never
   violated. The natural next contribution: an *online event-
   detection re-calibrator* for the semantic store.

5. **Codebook expert structure is naturally hierarchical.**
   DiffuseLatent-Hier (2606.03489) shows that when the codebook
   router conditions on HierMem's three layers, the per-layer
   expert assignments are nearly disjoint. The latent action
   codebook was *already* carrying temporal-scale structure;
   exposing the memory hierarchy lets the router exploit it.

6. **Retrieval and dialogue state should be jointly designed.**
   ContextPoint-RAG (2606.03398) indexes retrieved trajectories
   by the dialogue state itself — pointers issued three turns ago
   now condition the retrieval prior. The cleanest case to date
   that the multi-turn grounding line and the retrieval line are
   not stackable, they are *jointly designable*.

The honest summary: **the stack's safety/data tension is now an
engineerable knob (not a fundamental floor), benchmarks now
reshape research at the slice level (not just the average), and
the world model's per-state uncertainty is a useful first-class
signal (not just a quality indicator). The next bottleneck is an
online event-detection re-calibrator for HierMem's semantic store,
and a slice-target paper for StaleDirect's open #2
(Distractor × Hard × Embodiment-Swap).**
