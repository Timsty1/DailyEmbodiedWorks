# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-05-21)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

This cycle's center of gravity is **closed-loop integration**:
world-model-derived rewards are increasingly used to train VLA
policies online, latent action codebooks are being made
controllability-aware rather than reconstruction-aware, and
instruction-following benchmarks have started measuring
*compositional* generalization (novel verb × novel object × novel
spatial relation) rather than single-axis perturbation. Memory and
long-horizon work continues its split into perceptual (frame-level)
and cognitive (text/event-level) banks, but the new development is
*active forgetting* — explicit policies that decide what to drop,
not just what to retain.

---

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **DiTron-VLA: Decoder-Free DiT Action Heads with Token-Level
  Routing** (arXiv:2605.10812, May 18, 2026). Replaces the
  autoregressive action decoder with a diffusion-transformer head
  that conditions on VLM token states via a *learned routing matrix*
  rather than cross-attention. Two consequences: (a) the action head
  is decoupled from the VLM's KV-cache, so inference cost no longer
  scales with backbone width; (b) the routing matrix is sparse and
  interpretable — you can see which language tokens drive which
  action dimensions. Reports parity with π₀-FAST on LIBERO and a
  **2.3× speedup** at matched accuracy. The motivation is cleanly
  stated: action heads have inherited the VLM's autoregressive
  inductive bias even though robot actions are continuous and
  time-correlated, not discrete and locally Markovian.

- ★ **MoVE-VLA: Mixture-of-Visual-Experts for VLA** (arXiv:
  2605.11144, May 19, 2026). Routes visual tokens through one of
  four specialist encoders (object-centric / contact / depth /
  motion) based on the language instruction. The routing is
  differentiable but biased by a CLIP-style instruction classifier.
  Beats single-encoder OpenVLA-OFT baseline by **6.8 / 9.1 / 11.4
  points** on LIBERO-Object / LIBERO-Spatial / LIBERO-Long. The
  contribution that matters here is the *language-conditioned*
  routing — earlier MoE-VLAs (e.g., RoboMoE) routed on visual
  features alone, which collapses on perceptually-similar but
  semantically-different scenes.

- **HyperVLA: Hypernetwork-Conditioned Action Heads**
  (arXiv:2605.09877, May 16, 2026). The action head's weights are
  generated by a hypernetwork conditioned on a compressed task
  embedding, so adaptation to a new embodiment requires updating
  only the hypernetwork's input rather than retraining the head.
  Demonstrates 12-embodiment transfer (5 grippers, 4 dexterous
  hands, 3 quadruped manipulators) from a single backbone.

- **SlimVLA-3B: Distilling π₀ into a 3B Backbone**
  (arXiv:2605.08820, May 14, 2026). Teacher-student distillation
  with a *trajectory-consistency* loss (student must match teacher's
  rollout, not just per-step actions). Recovers **97%** of π₀'s
  LIBERO success with **31%** of the parameters. Useful as a
  reference recipe for compute-constrained groups.

- **Octo-Next**. Industry release of the Octo follow-up;
  multi-embodiment generalist with FAST-style frequency-tokenized
  actions and a unified gripper/dexterous-hand action grammar. No
  paper yet but checkpoints and a model card are out.

- **Helix v2** (Figure AI, blog post May 17). Public update to the
  dual-system humanoid VLA: the slow VLM now runs a *2-step
  lookahead* (predicts the next slow-system intent before the fast
  controller has finished the current one), masking 80–120 ms of
  inference latency. No paper, but the architectural pattern is
  clearly being adopted across industry.

## 2. Instruction Following & Language Grounding

- ★ **ProGAL-Compose: Compositional Grounded Alignment via
  Structured Prospective Reasoning** (arXiv:2605.10056, May 17,
  2026; ProGAL-VLA follow-up). Extends ProGAL-VLA's slow-symbolic /
  fast-control split to handle *compositional* instructions: novel
  combinations of verb × object × spatial relation that never
  co-occurred in training. The 3D entity graph is augmented with a
  *relation slot* whose embedding is constructed compositionally
  from learned verb and relation atoms. On LIBERO-Compose (the new
  split released alongside), success on held-out compositions rises
  from **18.7 → 64.2%** vs. ProGAL-VLA. The paper is a rare example
  where the benchmark and the method were designed against each
  other — the gain is real, not just a benchmark-shaped artifact.

- ★ **InstructProbe-VLA: Causal Tracing of Instruction Tokens
  Inside a VLA** (arXiv:2605.11530, May 19, 2026). Adapts ROME-style
  causal tracing to VLAs: identifies which transformer layers, and
  which token positions, carry the language-to-action signal. Three
  findings: (i) instruction information is concentrated in **layers
  9–14** of a 24-layer backbone, not the final layers as commonly
  assumed; (ii) the *verb* token contributes 3.4× more downstream
  action variance than the average object token; (iii) ablating
  layers 9–14 reproduces the "linguistic blindness" failure mode
  observed by IGAR (2603.06001) and the DAERT red-team paper
  (2604.05595). The first mechanistic story for *why* VLAs ignore
  instructions, not just *that* they do.

- **PARA-Robust: Paraphrase-Invariant Training via Equivariance
  Loss** (arXiv:2605.09621, May 15, 2026). Adds a paraphrase-
  equivariance regularizer (action distributions for paraphrased
  instructions must be KL-close) on top of standard SFT. Improves
  LIBERO-Para from 28.9 → 71.6% on OpenVLA-OFT without hurting the
  base split. A clean, reproducible training-time fix that
  complements IGAR's inference-time approach.

- **NegInstruct-Bench v1.0** (arXiv:2605.07840, May 12, 2026).
  Benchmarks negation handling ("pick everything *except* the red
  cube"). Current SOTA VLAs (π₀, OpenVLA-OFT, GR00T-N1) all score
  below random on negation-heavy splits; ProGAL-VLA and its
  Compose follow-up are the only methods clearly above chance. The
  community has been quietly avoiding negation; this benchmark
  forces the issue.

- **CoT-VLA-Spatial** (arXiv:2605.06210, May 9, 2026). Inserts a
  short language chain-of-thought between perception and action
  ("the green block is left of the red block; pick the right
  one"). The CoT is supervised by a synthetic dataset built from
  scene graphs. Useful for spatial-relation tasks; limited gain
  on contact-rich manipulation.

- **GroundedRefer-VLA**. Late-update from May 18; integrates an
  open-vocabulary referring-expression segmentation module into the
  VLA's perception stack. Helps disambiguate "the cup next to the
  blue one" without bespoke grounding pretraining.

## 3. VLA with Memory & Long-Horizon Reasoning

- ★ **ForgetVLA: Active Forgetting for Long-Horizon Embodied
  Memory** (arXiv:2605.10491, May 17, 2026). The standard memory
  story is *retention* — what to keep. ForgetVLA argues the bigger
  failure mode is *interference* and learns an explicit forgetting
  policy: a small recurrent head that, at each step, predicts a
  per-entry decay coefficient for the memory bank. Trained
  end-to-end with a downstream-task loss. On 12-minute kitchen-
  cleanup, success rises from 41% (MEM baseline) to **63%**, and
  the memory footprint shrinks **4.1×**. The motivation is the
  right one — over the timescales VLAs are starting to reach,
  catastrophic accumulation matters more than retrieval.

- ★ **HierMem-VLA: Hierarchical Episodic / Semantic / Procedural
  Memory** (arXiv:2605.08105, May 13, 2026). Splits VLA memory
  along the cognitive-science axes: episodic (frame buffer + time
  stamps), semantic (scene-graph store updated by VLM), procedural
  (skill bank with success statistics). The three banks are read
  by a learned gating MLP. Beats MEM and MemER on a new 30-minute
  factory-assembly benchmark by 18–22 points; the procedural bank
  contributes most of the gain on tasks with recurring sub-skills.

- **LongCtx-VLA-512K** (arXiv:2605.09245, May 15, 2026). Extends
  the VLM backbone to 512K-token context via sliding-window
  attention and KV-quantization, allowing **~45-minute** raw video
  to fit in context. Mostly an engineering paper but useful as a
  baseline: HierMem-VLA and ForgetVLA outperform it at much lower
  inference cost, which is evidence that long context alone is the
  wrong abstraction for embodied memory.

- **EpiRetrieve-VLA**. Update to the EpiRetrieve line; adds a
  contrastive retrieval objective that aligns natural-language
  queries to keyframe embeddings, so the agent can introspect
  ("what did I do with the towel?") and retrieve from its own
  history. Strong improvement on the EgoLifeBench multi-day split.

- *(Carried primaries: MEM (Physical Intelligence, 2603.03596),
  MemER (2510.20328), MemoryVLA (2508.19236), TrackVLA++,
  RoboMemory.)*

## 4. World-Model-Based VLA

- ★ **WorldRollout-RFT: Verified-Reward RL with Counterfactual
  World-Model Rollouts** (arXiv:2605.11005, May 18, 2026; VLA-RFT
  follow-up). Generalizes VLA-RFT (2510.00406) from goal-matching
  rewards to *counterfactual* rewards: the world model is rolled
  out under both the policy's action and a counterfactual
  alternative, and the reward is the predicted advantage. This
  resolves a real issue with VLA-RFT — its goal-matching reward
  collapses on tasks with multimodal solutions. Beats VLA-RFT by
  **+7.4 points** on RoboCasa-GR1 in <800 fine-tuning steps and
  matches it on LIBERO. The cleanest mechanistic improvement on
  the world-model-RL track this cycle.

- ★ **DreamerVLA: Recurrent Latent World Models for VLA
  Pretraining** (arXiv:2605.10277, May 17, 2026). Replaces the
  video-DiT world model (which is expensive and rolls out in pixel
  space) with a Dreamer-V3-style recurrent latent world model.
  Trained jointly with the policy via a free-energy-style
  objective. Two practical wins: (i) **70× faster** rollouts at
  matched policy quality, making world-model-based RL tractable on
  single-node clusters; (ii) the latent dynamics transfer across
  embodiments better than pixel dynamics, because they discard
  embodiment-specific visual texture. The paper revives a model
  class (RSSM) that the community had largely abandoned and shows
  it's the right substrate for VLAs once you stop reconstructing
  pixels.

- **VideoVLA-XL** (arXiv:2605.07412, May 11, 2026). Scales the
  joint video-generator + action-decoder DiT to 12B parameters.
  Action prediction quality monotonically improves with the
  video-generation quality of the same model, providing the
  cleanest scaling-law evidence to date that *video generation is
  the right pretraining task for VLAs*.

- **OpenWorldLib v2** (arXiv:2605.06104, May 8, 2026). Unified
  inference framework over world-model tasks; v2 adds a streaming-
  rollout API and policy-in-the-loop training hooks. Practical
  infrastructure — the world-model-RL papers above lean on it.

- **NeuralPhys-VLA** (arXiv:2605.08740, May 14, 2026). Couples a
  differentiable physics engine to the VLA's world model so contact
  dynamics aren't learned from scratch. Strong gains on contact-rich
  tasks (pouring, stacking, dexterous insertion); fits naturally
  alongside DreamerVLA's latent dynamics for non-contact regimes.

## 5. Latent Action Embeddings / Action Representation

- ★ **CtrlLAP: Controllability-Aware Latent Action Pretraining**
  (arXiv:2605.10602, May 17, 2026). The critique: LAPA and its
  descendants learn latent actions that reconstruct image pairs,
  but most of that reconstruction signal is *appearance change* the
  policy can't control (lighting, distractor motion). CtrlLAP adds
  a *controllability filter* — a learned mask that down-weights
  reconstruction loss on pixels uncorrelated with the action — and
  pretrains the latent code only on the controllable residual.
  **JALA-dino + CtrlLAP = 98.1% LIBERO-avg** (vs. JALA-dino's
  96.9%) with **3.2× less pretraining compute**. A real
  contribution: the field has been pretending all video signal is
  useful, and this paper diagnoses precisely which fraction isn't.

- ★ **Δ-LACT: Delta-Latent Actions with Hierarchical Codebooks**
  (arXiv:2605.09008, May 14, 2026). Replaces the flat VQ codebook
  (LAPA, JALA) with a two-level hierarchical codebook: a coarse
  code for the *intent* (move-toward, grasp, release, rotate) and
  a fine code for the *modulation* (speed, angle). The two codes
  are jointly trained but separately decodable. Practical wins:
  (a) the intent codebook transfers across embodiments without
  retraining; (b) the modulation codebook can be replaced
  per-embodiment, giving an explicit knob for sim-to-real and
  embodiment transfer. Beats UniLACT on Bridge-V2 + LIBERO joint
  evaluation by 4.7 points avg.

- **FrequencyLA** (arXiv:2605.07880, May 12, 2026). Inspired by
  FAST (π₀-FAST), pretrains latent actions in the DCT frequency
  domain. The bet: frequency-tokenized actions decorrelate the
  high- and low-frequency components of a trajectory, so the
  pretraining signal is cleaner. Modest gains over LAPA; underwhelms
  vs. CtrlLAP.

- **UniLACT-D** (arXiv:2605.06822, May 9, 2026). Depth-aware
  follow-up to UniLACT; jointly trains RGB and depth latent action
  codebooks with a shared head. The hierarchical Δ-LACT extension
  is more flexible, but UniLACT-D is a stronger drop-in replacement
  for current latent-action pipelines.

- **UVMC++ (Gemini Robotics)**. Industry update: Google DeepMind's
  Unified Vision-Motion Codes now use a hierarchical codebook
  (similar in spirit to Δ-LACT) and serve as the action tokenizer
  for the next-gen Gemini Robotics deployment. No paper, but the
  pattern of *hierarchy in latent action codes* is now industry +
  academic convergent.

## 6. Reinforcement Learning for VLA

- ★ **Q-VLA: Trajectory-Level Q-Learning for VLA Post-Training**
  (arXiv:2605.10895, May 18, 2026). The first credible Q-learning
  (not PPO/GRPO) recipe for full-scale VLAs. Key design choices:
  (i) the critic is a *small* (300M) Q-network that consumes the
  VLA's hidden state rather than raw observations, making target
  computation cheap; (ii) the action distribution is sampled from a
  *frozen* SFT policy and re-weighted by the critic, avoiding
  policy-gradient instabilities; (iii) replay buffer mixes online
  and demonstration rollouts at a learned ratio. Beats SimpleVLA-RL
  and VLA-RL by 5–9 points on three out-of-distribution real-robot
  tasks, with **70% fewer rollouts**. The cleanest argument yet
  that off-policy RL is the right post-training regime for VLAs.

- ★ **WorldRollout-RFT** (see §4). Counterfactual-reward extension
  of VLA-RFT; the strongest world-model-based RL result this cycle.

- **SafeRL-VLA** (arXiv:2605.09301, May 15, 2026). Adds a
  constraint-Lagrangian to SimpleVLA-RL with a learned
  safety-critic that fires on contact-force, joint-limit, and
  collision violations. Drops constraint violations from 17% to
  1.6% on a UR5e tabletop setup while preserving 95% of the task
  success rate.

- **AsyncRL-VLA** (arXiv:2605.08512, May 14, 2026). RL training
  framework that decouples rollout and gradient steps with a
  learned importance-sampling correction, enabling **3.4× wall-
  clock speedup** on 8-GPU clusters. Mostly infra, but the IS
  correction is a non-trivial contribution — most async RL stacks
  ignore the off-policy bias and silently degrade.

- **EvoVLA-Real** (arXiv:2605.07021, May 10, 2026). Self-evolution
  recipe (no external rewards) extended to real-robot domains via
  a learned success classifier. Encouraging early results on
  Franka-tabletop; still brittle in cluttered scenes.

- **IRL-VLA-2** (arXiv:2605.05891, May 7, 2026). Inverse-RL
  follow-up; learns a dense reward world-model from human
  demonstrations and uses it to bootstrap VLA-RL on autonomous-
  driving stacks. Closes the loop on a real production driving
  pipeline.

## 7. Benchmarks, Simulators & Evaluation

- ★ **LIBERO-Compose** (arXiv:2605.10056, May 17, 2026; released
  with ProGAL-Compose). The first LIBERO derivative that explicitly
  tests *compositional* instruction generalization: held-out
  combinations of verb × object × spatial relation drawn from
  in-distribution atoms. 280 tasks, 4 compositional axes, two
  difficulty tiers. Current SOTA VLAs score 18–35% on the held-out
  splits — the field had been mistaking memorization for
  compositional understanding, and this benchmark surfaces that.

- ★ **EmbodiedEval v2.0** (arXiv:2605.11227, May 19, 2026; AI2
  vla-eval follow-up). Adds standardized real-robot evaluation
  hooks (5 Franka labs, 3 UR5e labs, 2 humanoid labs), with a
  shared physical-eval protocol (camera calibration, scene reset,
  outcome scoring). Continues the vla-eval line's bet that
  *evaluation infra is the bottleneck*. The real-robot hooks are
  the missing piece — sim benchmarks have been overrated for two
  years and the field knows it.

- **RoboCasa-GR1-Long** (arXiv:2605.09415, May 16, 2026).
  Long-horizon extension of RoboCasa-GR1 with tasks up to 25
  minutes. Pairs naturally with HierMem-VLA, ForgetVLA, and
  LongCtx-VLA for memory evaluations.

- **SimX-OR-v2** (arXiv:2605.08108, May 13, 2026). Update to the
  observational-robustness stress-tester; adds adversarial camera
  pose perturbations sampled from a learned worst-case policy
  rather than i.i.d. perturbations. Tightens the
  robustness-evaluation regime considerably.

- **DexBench-2026** (arXiv:2605.07304, May 11, 2026). Dexterous-
  hand benchmark on the LEAP hand and Shadow hand; 60 tasks
  spanning in-hand manipulation, bimanual coordination, and tool
  use. Notable because the field's manipulation evaluation has
  been overweighted on parallel-jaw grippers.

- **AirVLA-Bench** (arXiv:2605.06602, May 9, 2026). UAV-targeted
  VLA benchmark; 40 outdoor tracking/inspection tasks.
  Complements UAV-Track-VLA (2604.02241).

## 8. Applications, Humanoid, UAV & Driving

- ★ **HumanoidVLA-G1 (Unitree G1 deployment paper)** (arXiv:
  2605.10713, May 18, 2026). Open deployment of a 7B VLA on the
  Unitree G1 humanoid, with full-body whole-arm + locomotion
  control. The interesting design choice: locomotion is delegated
  to a separately-trained low-level RL policy, with the VLA
  emitting *waypoint sequences and gait modes* rather than
  joint-level commands. Real demos of full-kitchen cleanup,
  trash sorting, and table-bussing. The cleanest open-source
  full-body VLA deployment paper to date.

- ★ **DriveVLA-Closed-Loop** (arXiv:2605.11008, May 18, 2026).
  Closed-loop urban driving VLA evaluated on CARLA-Leaderboard 2.0
  and on a real fleet of Lincoln MKZ retrofits. Three notable
  contributions: (i) the action head emits *planner-compatible*
  trajectories (not raw control), so the policy plugs into existing
  AV stacks; (ii) the reward signal during RL fine-tuning comes
  from a world-model rollout (per WorldRollout-RFT), with an
  added comfort term; (iii) safety constraints enforced via
  SafeRL-VLA's Lagrangian. **66% intervention-free urban miles**
  on a 1,200-km eval — not yet shippable, but a clear advance
  over the open closed-loop driving VLA baselines.

- **AerialVLA-Inspect** (arXiv:2605.09502, May 16, 2026). Outdoor
  bridge / power-line inspection VLA; reports robust
  performance under wind and lighting perturbation, with an
  explicit language-conditioned focus head ("inspect the third
  insulator from the right").

- **Surgical-VLA-v2** (arXiv:2605.08231, May 13, 2026). Updated
  surgical-suite VLA on da Vinci Research Kit. Reports the first
  credible numbers on suturing under tissue deformation; pairs
  NeuralPhys-VLA (§4)'s differentiable contact model with a
  domain-randomized SFT recipe. Notable for the safety budget —
  zero adverse events in 1,400 evaluated tool-tissue interactions.

- **GR00T-N1.5 (NVIDIA)**. Industry update to the GR00T humanoid
  generalist; checkpoint and model card released, paper pending.
  Notable for its UVMC-style tokenizer (alignment with the
  hierarchical-latent-action trend in §5).

## Cross-Cutting Theme — the four trends converging

The single most informative pattern this cycle is that **four
tracks that have been developed in parallel are starting to
co-author one design**:

1. **World-model rewards** (VLA-RFT → WorldRollout-RFT) supply
   dense, verifiable training signal.
2. **Off-policy RL** (Q-VLA) makes that signal usable without
   collapsing the SFT prior.
3. **Hierarchical latent actions** (Δ-LACT, CtrlLAP, UVMC++)
   give the policy a controllable, transferable action vocabulary.
4. **Active memory** (ForgetVLA, HierMem-VLA) extends the
   horizon over which the above three can compose without
   interference collapse.

A handful of recent papers (notably the **DriveVLA-Closed-Loop**
deployment paper and the **HumanoidVLA-G1** paper) already
combine 2–3 of these tracks in one system. The expected next-cycle
synthesis is a single open-source generalist that combines all
four — that's the empirical hole worth watching for.

## Picks of the day (if you only read three)

1. **CtrlLAP (2605.10602)** — sharpest critique of the latent-
   action-pretraining literature in months. The observation that
   most reconstruction signal is *not* under the policy's control
   is obvious in retrospect and immediately useful. **JALA-dino +
   CtrlLAP** is the new latent-action pretraining baseline.

2. **Q-VLA (2605.10895)** — the first credible off-policy RL
   recipe for VLAs at scale, with a small-critic design that
   removes the standard cost objection. If this reproduces in
   independent labs, the PPO/GRPO era of VLA RL post-training is
   probably over within two cycles.

3. **ForgetVLA (2605.10491)** — flips the embodied-memory
   conversation from retention to interference. The mechanism is
   simple (per-entry decay coefficients predicted online), the
   evaluation is honest (real long-horizon kitchen tasks, not
   sim-LIBERO), and the framing reorients the rest of the
   long-horizon literature.

## Open backlog for the next cycle (predicted)

- **Q-VLA × WorldRollout-RFT** — pairing off-policy critic
  learning with counterfactual world-model rewards. Both papers
  set this up explicitly; the joint result is the obvious next
  step.
- **CtrlLAP × Δ-LACT** — controllability-aware filtering applied
  per codebook level of the hierarchical action space.
- **HierMem-VLA × ForgetVLA** — three banks with three forgetting
  policies. The episodic bank wants aggressive decay, the
  procedural bank essentially none; a single decay policy across
  all three is unlikely to be Pareto-optimal.
- **InstructProbe-VLA on Q-VLA** — does off-policy RL preserve the
  layer-9–14 instruction signal that ProGAL-VLA and PARA-Robust
  exploit, or does it erode under reward-driven updates? An open
  empirical question with direct implications for the SFT-then-RL
  cookbook.
- **HumanoidVLA-G1 × DexBench-2026** — humanoid + dexterous-hand
  evaluation has been done piecewise; an end-to-end benchmark
  pairing the two would be the natural next eval substrate.
