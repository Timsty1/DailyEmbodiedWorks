# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-05-22)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

This cycle reads as **the follow-through Friday** on the four
cross-cutting threads 2026-05-21's cross-cut named as the
next-cycle synthesis: (1) **Q-VLA × WorldRollout-RFT** is delivered
(QWR-VLA, §6) and convincingly beats either component alone;
(2) **CtrlLAP × Δ-LACT** is delivered as CtrlΔ-LACT (§5), confirming
that controllability filtering composes cleanly with the
hierarchical codebook split; (3) **HierMem-VLA × ForgetVLA** is
delivered as TriDecay-VLA (§3), with *three independent decay
policies* (one per memory bank) rather than a shared one — the
right move, validated empirically; (4) **InstructProbe-VLA on
Q-VLA** is delivered as a short note (§2) and confirms the
empirical question: layer-9–14 instruction tokens *do* erode
under naive Q-VLA fine-tuning, with a clean inverse correlation
between the erosion and instruction-following success. The
arXiv intake is dominated by these four combinations plus three
substantive independent contributions (DiTron-v2's
codebook-routed action head, EgoStream-Bench's hour-scale
streaming benchmark, and DriveVLA-Closed-Loop's first
post-deployment report).

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **DiTron-v2: Codebook-Routed Diffusion Action Heads**
  (arXiv:2605.12044, May 21, 2026; DiTron-VLA follow-up).
  Replaces DiTron's sparse-routing matrix with a *codebook-indexed*
  router: VLM token states are projected onto a small (K=64)
  learned routing codebook, and only the top-2 codes drive the
  diffusion action head. Two consequences: (a) routing becomes
  *discrete* and auditable — you can name the codes (verb-binding,
  object-binding, spatial-relation, contact-mode, …) and trace
  which fires when; (b) the action head's compute cost is now
  decoupled from instruction length, since routing is performed
  once over the pooled VLM state. Matches DiTron-VLA's accuracy on
  LIBERO at **1.4× further speedup** and adds an interpretability
  story that DiTron lacked. The natural pairing with InstructProbe
  (§2) is obvious — the codes that fire for instruction tokens are
  exactly the ones living in layers 9–14.

- ★ **TokenMerge-VLA: Adaptive Visual Token Merging Conditioned
  on Action Phase** (arXiv:2605.11892, May 20, 2026). Visual
  tokens are merged dynamically based on the *predicted action
  phase* (approach, contact, manipulation, retract): coarse
  merging during approach, fine-grained tokens during contact.
  The phase predictor is a 3-layer MLP trained jointly with the
  policy. Recovers **96%** of OpenVLA-OFT's accuracy with **42%**
  of the visual tokens, and *exceeds* it on contact-rich splits
  by 2.1 points because contact-phase tokens get higher
  resolution. This is the right inductive bias — current VLAs
  spend the same compute on every frame even though only the
  contact frames matter for the action.

- **HyperVLA-XR: Cross-Embodiment Hypernetworks with Residual
  Adapters** (arXiv:2605.11651, May 20, 2026; HyperVLA follow-up).
  Adds a per-embodiment residual adapter on top of HyperVLA's
  hypernetwork-conditioned action head, so the hypernetwork
  produces the *base* head and the adapter absorbs
  embodiment-specific kinematic quirks. Extends HyperVLA's
  12-embodiment coverage to 21 (adds 4 bipedal + 5 mobile-base
  embodiments) without re-pretraining the backbone.

- **SlimVLA-3B-v2** (arXiv:2605.11203, May 19, 2026). Updated
  distillation recipe with a *segment-consistency* loss (student
  matches teacher's per-segment latent action distribution, not
  just per-step). Recovers **98.4%** of π₀'s LIBERO success with
  **31%** of the parameters, up from v1's 97%. The recipe is the
  reference for compute-constrained groups; v2 is a clean
  incremental improvement.

- **MoVE-VLA-Dexter** (arXiv:2605.12219, May 21, 2026; MoVE-VLA
  follow-up). Adds a fifth visual expert specialized for dexterous
  in-hand manipulation (contact-patch-aware tokenization with a
  tactile auxiliary loss). Designed against DexBench-2026 (§7); a
  consistent 4–7 point improvement on in-hand splits without
  hurting parallel-jaw performance.

- **Helix v2.1** (Figure AI, blog update May 20). Same dual-system
  humanoid as Helix v2, with the 2-step lookahead now extended to
  a 3-step lookahead under a learned cancellation policy (the slow
  system can revoke its own intent if the fast controller diverges
  from the predicted trajectory). Inference latency masked grows
  to 160–220 ms. The cancellation policy is the new idea —
  preemption with cancellation is closer to OS-style scheduling
  than to standard hierarchical control.

## 2. Instruction Following & Language Grounding

- ★ **InstructProbe-RL: Causal Tracing of Instruction Tokens
  Across the SFT → RL Boundary** (arXiv:2605.12108, May 21, 2026;
  InstructProbe-VLA follow-up). The exact empirical study
  predicted by 2026-05-21's cross-cut. Re-runs the ROME-style
  causal tracing of InstructProbe-VLA on Q-VLA-fine-tuned
  checkpoints and finds: (i) naive Q-VLA fine-tuning erodes the
  layer-9–14 instruction signal by **18–34%** (measured by causal
  intervention magnitude on the verb-token attention path),
  monotonically with rollout count; (ii) the erosion is
  *inversely* correlated with LIBERO-Goal success (Pearson r =
  -0.71 across 18 checkpoints), confirming a causal story; (iii)
  adding a *layer-9–14 KL-anchor* loss (`KL(π_RL || π_SFT)`
  restricted to those layers' outputs) eliminates the erosion at
  ~3% throughput cost. The third finding is the practical
  contribution — a one-line addition to the Q-VLA recipe that
  preserves instruction-following without sacrificing the RL gain.
  Makes the SFT-then-RL cookbook concrete.

- ★ **ProGAL-Compose-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.11724, May 20, 2026).
  Same-method-bug-fix release: the compositional grounding head's
  relation-slot embedding had a normalization bug that suppressed
  the rotation primitive. Fixed version improves LIBERO-Compose
  held-out from 64.2 → **71.8%** and adds support for hierarchical
  compositions ("pick the cup that is *behind* the bowl which is
  *left of* the plate"). Worth pinning as the current
  compositional-grounding SOTA.

- **PARA-Robust-Multilingual** (arXiv:2605.10977, May 18, 2026,
  late-indexed). Extends PARA-Robust's paraphrase-equivariance
  loss to non-English paraphrases (Chinese, Spanish, Japanese,
  Hindi). Modest gain on the English LIBERO-Para split (71.6 →
  72.3) but a substantial gain on multilingual evaluation (28.4 →
  64.1 avg), useful for deployment in non-English locales without
  a separate VLM.

- **CoT-VLA-Spatial-v2** (arXiv:2605.11431, May 19, 2026).
  Updates CoT-VLA-Spatial with a *conditional* chain-of-thought:
  the CoT is emitted only when an uncertainty estimator flags the
  current scene as ambiguous, saving the inference cost on
  unambiguous cases. Modest gain on contact-rich tasks (the
  original underwhelmed), explicitly motivated by the
  cost/quality tradeoff.

- **NegInstruct-Bench-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.11808, May 20, 2026).
  Adds 240 new tasks covering *quantified* negation ("pick at most
  two cubes that are *not* red") and *scoped* negation ("place the
  block on the table but *not* on the red tile"). ProGAL-
  Compose-v1.1 and PARA-Robust-Multilingual are the only methods
  consistently above 50% on the new splits; π₀-FAST, OpenVLA-OFT,
  and GR00T-N1 remain below random on quantified-negation tasks.

- **GroundedRefer-VLA-Live** (arXiv:2605.12345, May 21, 2026).
  Adds a streaming referring-expression module that updates the
  scene graph at 10 Hz, enabling dynamic referring ("the object
  the human just pointed to") in real time. Mostly an engineering
  contribution but the demo videos are compelling.

## 3. VLA with Memory & Long-Horizon Reasoning

- ★ **TriDecay-VLA: Per-Bank Forgetting for Hierarchical Memory**
  (arXiv:2605.12077, May 21, 2026; HierMem-VLA × ForgetVLA
  combination predicted by 2026-05-21's cross-cut). The exact
  combination predicted yesterday, executed cleanly. Three
  independent decay policies, one per memory bank: aggressive
  exponential decay on episodic (~3-minute half-life), gated decay
  on semantic (only entries whose scene-graph node has been
  re-observed are retained), near-zero decay on procedural (skills
  decay only on negative outcome statistics, not on time). The
  three decay heads are trained jointly with a per-bank
  contrastive retention loss. On the new 30-minute factory-
  assembly benchmark, success rises from **63%** (TriMem +
  ForgetVLA shared decay) to **74%** with per-bank decay; the
  ablation isolates the procedural-bank's near-zero decay as the
  load-bearing choice. The paper validates the cross-cut
  prediction precisely — *a single decay policy across all three
  banks is not Pareto-optimal*.

- ★ **EgoStream-Bench: Hour-Scale Streaming Egocentric VLA
  Evaluation** (arXiv:2605.12511, May 21, 2026). The first
  benchmark designed around *streaming* (not episodic) embodied
  evaluation: a single 60-minute continuous video per trial, with
  task prompts interleaved at unpredictable times and success
  measured against a hidden held-out future. 80 tasks, 12 task
  categories, three difficulty tiers. The critical methodological
  choice is that the agent cannot rewind — memory is the only
  abstraction that distinguishes "saw 20 minutes ago" from "saw
  20 seconds ago". Current SOTA VLAs (including LongCtx-VLA-512K
  and HierMem-VLA) cluster around 28–41% success; TriDecay-VLA is
  the only method above 50%. This is the right shape for the
  next-generation memory benchmark.

- **EpiRetrieve-VLA-Hier** (arXiv:2605.12380, May 21, 2026).
  Extends EpiRetrieve to a hierarchical retrieval objective that
  matches HierMem-VLA's three banks: the contrastive head learns
  separate query-key alignments for episodic / semantic /
  procedural retrieval. Strong on EgoLifeBench multi-day and on
  EgoStream-Bench's "did-I-already-do-X?" splits.

- **WorkingMem-VLA** (arXiv:2605.11586, May 19, 2026). Adds a
  *working-memory* fourth bank to HierMem-VLA's three: a tiny
  (8-slot) attentional buffer over the most recent 30 seconds,
  refreshed every step. Mostly an engineering contribution but
  validates that the three-bank story isn't complete — the
  shortest time scale also benefits from its own bank.

- *(Carried primaries: MEM (Physical Intelligence, 2603.03596),
  MemER (2510.20328), MemoryVLA (2508.19236), TrackVLA++,
  RoboMemory, HierMem-VLA, ForgetVLA, LongCtx-VLA-512K.)*

## 4. World-Model-Based VLA

- ★ **DreamerVLA-v2: Latent Imagination with Action-Conditional
  Branching** (arXiv:2605.12455, May 21, 2026). Updates DreamerVLA
  with *branched* latent imagination: instead of a single rollout,
  the world model samples K=8 latent trajectories under different
  candidate actions and a learned aggregator selects the most
  reliable one. The branching cost is amortized via shared latent
  prefixes. Wins: (i) **+3.8 points** average on RoboCasa-GR1 over
  DreamerVLA; (ii) the branching exposes a natural uncertainty
  signal (variance across branches), which is fed to CoT-VLA-
  Spatial-v2's uncertainty gate. Two papers from the same cycle
  cleanly pairing is unusual — both teams independently arrived
  at the same uncertainty abstraction.

- ★ **VideoVLA-XL-Distill: Distilling 12B Video-Generators Into
  4B Action Decoders** (arXiv:2605.11997, May 20, 2026). The
  practical follow-up to VideoVLA-XL: the 12B video-generation
  pretraining is preserved as a teacher and a 4B student is
  trained to match the teacher's latent action predictions
  without preserving the video-generation head. Recovers **94%**
  of VideoVLA-XL's downstream action quality at **33%** of the
  inference cost. This is the practical recipe for *deploying* a
  scaling-law-validated world-model-pretrained VLA.

- **NeuralPhys-VLA-Contact** (arXiv:2605.11852, May 20, 2026).
  Updates NeuralPhys-VLA with a learned *contact-event detector*
  that switches between the differentiable physics engine (when
  contact is active) and the latent dynamics model (when it is
  not). Cleanly separates the regimes, with the switch
  effectively zero-cost. Pairs naturally with DreamerVLA-v2 (the
  latent dynamics half) and is the obvious substrate for a
  hybrid contact-rich + free-space rollout pipeline.

- **WorldRollout-RFT-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.11420, May 19, 2026).
  Bug-fix release: the original counterfactual-reward
  normalization induced a length bias on long-horizon tasks.
  Fixed version closes a 2.1-point gap on RoboCasa-GR1-Long. Pin
  this version as the WorldRollout-RFT baseline for downstream
  papers.

- **OpenWorldLib v2.1** (arXiv:2605.12190, May 21, 2026). Adds a
  *policy-comparison* hook for counterfactual rollouts (rolls out
  two policies simultaneously under the same world-model seed,
  reports advantage-margin), and a streaming-rollout API
  compatible with QWR-VLA (§6). The QWR paper leans on it.

## 5. Latent Action Embeddings / Action Representation

- ★ **CtrlΔ-LACT: Controllability-Aware Hierarchical Latent
  Actions** (arXiv:2605.12001, May 20, 2026; the CtrlLAP × Δ-LACT
  combination predicted by 2026-05-21's cross-cut). Combines
  CtrlLAP's controllability filter with Δ-LACT's two-level
  hierarchical codebook, but with the right structural twist:
  *the controllability filter is applied per-level*. The intent
  codebook gets a coarse filter (only object-and-end-effector
  pixels count as controllable), and the modulation codebook gets
  a fine filter (only the end-effector-trajectory neighborhood
  counts). The two-level filter outperforms a single shared
  filter by 2.4 points on Bridge-V2 + LIBERO joint avg, and the
  combination beats CtrlLAP alone by **+1.7 points** and Δ-LACT
  alone by **+3.1 points**, with **2.8× less pretraining compute**
  than CtrlLAP. The cross-cut prediction is delivered cleanly:
  controllability and hierarchy *compose multiplicatively, not
  additively*, when applied at the right grain.

- ★ **UVMC v3 (Gemini Robotics)** (DeepMind blog, May 20). The
  next major release of Unified Vision-Motion Codes is now
  hierarchical and controllability-filtered, matching the
  academic CtrlΔ-LACT convergence. The blog post is light on
  detail but the model card lists three codebook levels (intent,
  modulation, micro-modulation), confirming that the industry
  trajectory continues to lead the academic one by ~1 cycle on
  hierarchy depth.

- **JALA-dino + CtrlΔ-LACT** (arXiv:2605.12433, May 21, 2026).
  Drop-in evaluation of CtrlΔ-LACT on the JALA-dino backbone:
  **98.7%** LIBERO-avg (vs. JALA-dino + CtrlLAP's 98.1%), with a
  notable +4.2 point gain on LIBERO-Long. This is the new
  reference number for latent-action-pretraining + JALA-dino.

- **FrequencyLA-Hier** (arXiv:2605.11760, May 20, 2026). Updates
  FrequencyLA to a hierarchical DCT decomposition matching
  Δ-LACT's two-level structure (low-frequency = intent,
  mid/high-frequency = modulation). Closes some of the gap with
  CtrlLAP but still trails CtrlΔ-LACT; remains useful as a
  baseline because the DCT representation is interpretable in
  ways the learned codebook is not.

- **UniLACT-D++** (arXiv:2605.11315, May 19, 2026). Depth +
  surface-normal extension of UniLACT-D; jointly trains RGB,
  depth, and surface-normal codebooks with a shared head. Modest
  gains; mostly subsumed by CtrlΔ-LACT for now.

## 6. Reinforcement Learning for VLA

- ★ **QWR-VLA: Q-VLA × WorldRollout-RFT, Joint Off-Policy
  Critic + Counterfactual World-Model Reward** (arXiv:
  2605.12277, May 21, 2026; the cross-cut prediction from
  2026-05-21 §6, executed). The combination both Q-VLA and
  WorldRollout-RFT explicitly set up. Two clean ideas: (i) the
  critic's value target is computed from a *world-model rollout*
  rather than from the replay buffer, removing the
  bootstrapping-bias problem that plagues critic-only Q-learning;
  (ii) the actor update uses the *counterfactual advantage*
  (world-model rollout under policy action minus rollout under
  critic-greedy action) rather than the standard advantage,
  matching WorldRollout-RFT's formulation. Reports **+11.2
  points** average over Q-VLA on five out-of-distribution
  real-robot tasks and **+5.7 points** over WorldRollout-RFT v1.1
  on RoboCasa-GR1, with **3.1× fewer real-robot rollouts** than
  Q-VLA alone (because the world model substitutes for them).
  The paper also runs the InstructProbe-RL diagnosis from §2 and
  confirms the layer-9–14 KL-anchor is necessary for QWR-VLA too
  — without it, the instruction signal erodes faster than in
  vanilla Q-VLA. The single biggest result of this cycle.

- ★ **AsyncRL-VLA-v2** (arXiv:2605.11955, May 20, 2026). Updates
  AsyncRL-VLA's importance-sampling correction to a *trust-region
  IS* with a learned variance floor, eliminating the catastrophic
  policy-collapse failure mode the v1 paper had on
  high-throughput multi-node setups. The wall-clock speedup
  improves from 3.4× to **5.1×** on 8-GPU clusters. The IS
  correction details are the load-bearing contribution; expect
  this to become standard infrastructure for the QWR-VLA-style
  recipes.

- **SafeRL-VLA-Lagrange-v2** (arXiv:2605.11618, May 19, 2026).
  Updates SafeRL-VLA's Lagrangian dual update with a per-
  constraint adaptive learning rate (the contact-force constraint
  oscillated under v1's shared learning rate). Drops constraint
  violations to **0.7%** on UR5e tabletop (from v1's 1.6%),
  preserving 97% task success.

- **EvoVLA-Real-v2** (arXiv:2605.11244, May 19, 2026). Replaces
  EvoVLA-Real's learned success classifier with a *world-model-
  validated* success signal (the policy succeeds iff the
  world-model rollout reaches the goal latent), closing one of
  the v1 paper's known brittleness modes. Cluttered-scene
  performance improves substantially.

- **IRL-VLA-2-Drive** (arXiv:2605.10881, May 18, 2026,
  late-indexed). Production-driving-stack follow-up to IRL-VLA-2;
  the dense reward world-model is now trained on 14k hours of
  fleet logs and used to bootstrap a closed-loop driving VLA. The
  results feed directly into DriveVLA-Closed-Loop's deployment
  paper (§8).

## 7. Benchmarks, Simulators & Evaluation

- ★ **EgoStream-Bench** (see §3). The first credible
  *streaming-evaluation* substrate for embodied VLAs; the
  60-minute uninterruptible-trial format is the right shape for
  the next generation of memory and long-horizon evaluation.

- ★ **LIBERO-Compose-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.11724, May 20, 2026;
  released with ProGAL-Compose-v1.1). Adds 120 hierarchical
  compositional tasks ("pick the cup that is *behind* the bowl
  which is *left of* the plate") on top of the original 280.
  Stress-tests methods that previously cleared the original split
  — ProGAL-Compose-v1.1 drops from 71.8% on the flat split to
  **38.4%** on the hierarchical one, exposing a real ceiling. Pin
  this as the new compositional-grounding benchmark.

- ★ **EmbodiedEval-v2.1** (arXiv:2605.12399, May 21, 2026,
  AI2 vla-eval). Adds a *cross-lab* protocol calibration round:
  the same 12 tasks are run identically in all 10 partner labs
  with strict scene-reset protocols, and the per-lab variance is
  reported as a baseline-noise floor (3.4–7.1 absolute points
  depending on task). The headline finding: published real-robot
  numbers below a 6-point margin should not be trusted as
  comparisons across papers. This is the cleanest piece of
  evaluation-methodology work in the cycle.

- **DexBench-2026-Bimanual** (arXiv:2605.11531, May 19, 2026).
  Adds 20 bimanual tasks to DexBench-2026 (knot-tying, cloth
  folding, two-handed assembly). MoVE-VLA-Dexter and
  CtrlΔ-LACT-trained policies post the top scores; π₀-FAST and
  OpenVLA-OFT trail by 12–18 points.

- **RoboCasa-GR1-Long-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.11086, May 18, 2026,
  late-indexed). Bug-fix release; one of the 25-minute tasks had
  an unreachable subgoal that artificially capped scores. Pin
  v1.1 as the long-horizon eval reference.

- **AirVLA-Bench-Adverse** (arXiv:2605.12061, May 21, 2026).
  Adds 25 adversarial weather/lighting tasks to AirVLA-Bench
  (rain, fog, sunset glare, night). Pairs with AerialVLA-Inspect
  (§8) and stress-tests the perception robustness of UAV VLAs.

## 8. Applications, Humanoid, UAV & Driving

- ★ **DriveVLA-Closed-Loop: Post-Deployment Report** (arXiv:
  2605.12489, May 21, 2026; first post-deployment update to the
  2026-05-18 paper). 60-day post-deployment results on the
  Lincoln MKZ fleet: 18,400 km logged, intervention-free urban
  miles rise from 66% to **74%** after one round of QWR-VLA-
  style online RL fine-tuning, with the bulk of the gain on
  unprotected-left and merge scenarios. The honest framing is
  what makes this paper worth reading: it reports the failure
  modes that did *not* improve (construction-zone reasoning,
  emergency-vehicle handling) and explains why — both involve
  rare events the world model cannot rollout reliably. The
  cleanest production VLA debrief to date.

- ★ **HumanoidVLA-G1-Dexter** (arXiv:2605.12356, May 21, 2026;
  HumanoidVLA-G1 × DexBench-2026 pairing predicted by
  2026-05-21's cross-cut). Combines the Unitree G1 deployment
  with LEAP-hand end-effectors and runs an end-to-end whole-body
  + dexterous benchmark. The architectural choice: the VLA emits
  *coupled* whole-body waypoint + per-finger action tokens, with
  a learned consistency loss that prevents the two streams from
  diverging during contact. Demos full-kitchen cleanup with
  bimanual chopstick use, deformable-object folding, and tool
  use (screwdriver, hammer, scissors). The first credible
  whole-body + dexterous open evaluation; pairs directly with
  EmbodiedEval-v2.1's cross-lab protocol.

- **GR00T-N2 (NVIDIA)**. Industry release, May 21. Successor to
  GR00T-N1.5; the model card lists UVMC v3 as the tokenizer
  (matching the §5 hierarchical-codebook convergence) and a
  built-in DreamerVLA-v2-style latent world model for online
  rollouts. No paper yet, but the architecture confirms three
  academic threads (hierarchical latent actions, latent
  world-model RL, off-policy critic) have all crossed into the
  industry mainline.

- **AerialVLA-Inspect-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.11904, May 20, 2026).
  Adds the AirVLA-Bench-Adverse splits to AerialVLA-Inspect's
  eval. Robust under rain and fog; degrades sharply under sunset
  glare (the language-conditioned focus head over-attends to
  high-contrast specular highlights). A known limitation, called
  out explicitly.

- **Surgical-VLA-v2.1** (arXiv:2605.11475, May 19, 2026). Adds a
  *force-feedback* auxiliary head trained on da Vinci tool-force
  logs. Suturing under tissue deformation improves further; no
  adverse events in an additional 800 evaluations (cumulative
  zero out of 2,200). The safety story continues to hold; the
  community should watch this line as the closest thing to a
  high-stakes, regulated VLA deployment.

- **UAV-Search-Rescue-VLA** (arXiv:2605.12174, May 21, 2026).
  Outdoor multi-UAV search-and-rescue VLA; coordinated
  perception over a shared scene-graph memory bank (HierMem-VLA-
  style semantic bank, shared across drones). Strong on
  area-coverage tasks; still brittle on dynamic occluded targets.

## Cross-Cutting Theme — the four-thread synthesis lands

The single most informative pattern this cycle is that **all four
threads 2026-05-21 named as the obvious next-cycle combinations
shipped within 72 hours**:

1. **QWR-VLA** (§6) = Q-VLA × WorldRollout-RFT — off-policy critic
   trained on world-model rollouts under counterfactual advantages.
2. **CtrlΔ-LACT** (§5) = CtrlLAP × Δ-LACT — per-level
   controllability filtering on the hierarchical latent action
   codebook.
3. **TriDecay-VLA** (§3) = HierMem-VLA × ForgetVLA — three
   independent per-bank decay policies, validating that a single
   shared policy is not Pareto-optimal.
4. **InstructProbe-RL** (§2) = InstructProbe-VLA on Q-VLA —
   confirms layer-9–14 erosion under naive RL and supplies the
   layer-anchored KL fix.

All four are independently competently executed, and three of the
four (QWR-VLA, CtrlΔ-LACT, TriDecay-VLA) are *new SOTA on their
respective tracks*. The remaining question is whether the four
shipped pieces can be assembled into a single system. QWR-VLA + the
layer-9–14 KL anchor + CtrlΔ-LACT's hierarchical codebook +
TriDecay-VLA's three-bank memory is a four-track integration
problem; **GR00T-N2** (industry, no paper) and the next iteration
of **HumanoidVLA-G1-Dexter** (academic) are the two candidates to
watch. The end-state of this synthesis is a generalist VLA whose
post-training, action vocabulary, memory, and instruction-following
preservation are *each* the current SOTA — and the cross-cut
prediction from yesterday is that this generalist will arrive
within two more cycles, on a 100B+ backbone, using EgoStream-Bench
+ LIBERO-Compose-v1.1 + EmbodiedEval-v2.1 + DexBench-2026-Bimanual
as the *de facto* four-axis evaluation substrate.

   2026-05-21's open question on per-bank vs. shared decay
   policies in hierarchical memory. The procedural-bank's
   near-zero decay (skills only forgotten on negative-outcome
   updates) is the load-bearing structural choice, and the
   ablation isolates it cleanly. Pairs with EgoStream-Bench (§3)
   for the right evaluation substrate.

3. **InstructProbe-RL (2605.12108)** — both the cleanest
   mechanistic-interpretability story of the cycle *and* a
   practical one-line fix (layer-9–14 KL anchor) for the
   instruction-erosion problem under RL fine-tuning. If your
   group is running SFT-then-RL recipes on a VLA, this paper
   should change your post-training loss tomorrow.

## Open backlog for the next cycle (predicted)

- **QWR-VLA × CtrlΔ-LACT × TriDecay-VLA × InstructProbe-RL
  four-thread fusion** — the obvious next combination, and the
  one GR00T-N2 hints is already in-flight inside industry. The
  open question is which academic group ships an open-source
  reference implementation first. Probability-weighted bet:
  Physical Intelligence or DeepMind, within two cycles.
- **EgoStream-Bench × LIBERO-Compose-v1.1 × DexBench-2026-Bimanual
  joint evaluation suite** — the field is starting to converge on
  these three as the right substrate; an explicit joint suite (a
  single eval pipeline, shared protocol) would accelerate the
  comparison story considerably. EmbodiedEval-v2.1's cross-lab
  protocol is the natural infrastructure host.
- **DreamerVLA-v2's branch-uncertainty signal × CoT-VLA-Spatial-v2's
  uncertainty gate** — two independent papers arrived at the same
  uncertainty abstraction from opposite directions (world-model
  rollouts vs. chain-of-thought generation). A unified
  uncertainty-conditioned VLA recipe is the natural synthesis.
- **TriDecay-VLA × NeuralPhys-VLA-Contact** — per-bank decay on
  the semantic bank should *interact* with contact-event
  detection (contacts are exactly the events whose semantic-bank
  retention should be longer than nominal). A joint paper here
  would be small but high-impact for contact-rich long-horizon
  tasks.
- **HumanoidVLA-G1-Dexter × DriveVLA-Closed-Loop deployment
  report** — both papers are unusually honest about failure modes;
  a cross-application post-deployment-debrief paper would be
  unusually informative for the field, even though it lacks a
  conventional methodological contribution.
