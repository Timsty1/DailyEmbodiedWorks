## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **CrossGraph-Auto-Theorem: Closed-Form Automorphism Group of
  the Cell-Class Graph Across Embodiment Subgraphs**
  (arXiv:2605.10014, May 14, 2026, MIT + Stanford + DeepMind +
  Berkeley + Tsinghua + AI2 + CMU + Wayve). The theorem-side
  iteration that resolves 05-13's third open question — "is the
  manipulation–driving isomorphism a coincidence, or a closed-form
  artefact of the cell-class graph?" — in the strongest possible
  form. Three load-bearing structural moves: (i) the automorphism
  group Aut(G) of the 7-node cell-class graph is enumerated under
  the binding-head joint consumption relation, yielding a
  Klein-four group V₄ with three non-trivial generators (the
  trajectory ↔ spatial-relation swap, the identity ↔ primitive-pair
  swap, and their composition) — the *first explicit derivation*
  of the cell-class graph's symmetry group; (ii) each embodiment
  subgraph (manipulation, driving, humanoid, UAV) is shown to be
  obtained from a single canonical graph G₀ by a *V₄-valued
  edge-weight perturbation* δ(E) parameterized by the embodiment's
  effective degrees of freedom (manipulator-DoF, vehicle-DoF,
  whole-body-DoF, aerial-DoF respectively), with δ(E) a *closed-
  form linear functional* of the embodiment's reachable-pose
  manifold dimension; (iii) the theorem proves a *cross-subgraph
  transfer lemma*: for any two embodiment subgraphs G_A, G_B
  with V₄-related edge weights, the path-length-3 γ-vector
  transfers by δ(E_A) − δ(E_B) without re-fitting, with the
  transfer error bounded by ‖δ(E_A) − δ(E_B)‖₁ · 0.62 (the
  path-length-3 graph-attenuation constant). Three empirical
  findings: (a) the V₄ symmetry is empirically validated on the
  three measured subgraphs (manipulation, driving, humanoid) with
  the closed-form edge-weight perturbation matching the empirical
  perturbation within ±0.0009 absolute on each of the 21
  path-length-1 edges — the tightest cross-subgraph empirical
  agreement the cycle has reported; (b) the transfer-lemma error
  bound is empirically validated on the manipulation → driving
  transfer at 0.61 ± 0.02 absolute (against the closed-form 0.62
  prediction), and on the manipulation → humanoid transfer at
  0.63 ± 0.02 — the closed-form 0.62 attenuation constant is now
  the cross-subgraph load-bearing constant as well; (c) the
  theorem *predicts* a fourth subgraph (UAV) with δ_UAV computed
  closed-form from the aerial-DoF without any empirical fitting,
  which UAVFourth-VLA (§8) validates within 24 hours. The honest
  read: the cell-class graph's symmetry structure is now derived
  in closed form, the cross-subgraph transfer is no longer an
  empirical coincidence but a *Klein-four-symmetry artefact* of
  the graph topology, and the field has a *closed-form prescription

  effective DoF count. Reads as the *cross-subgraph-symmetry paper*
  of the cycle and the strongest theorem-side iteration since
  PathThree-ε-Theorem (05-13 §1).
