## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **PathThree-ε-Theorem: Path-Length-3 Closed-Form Correction
  for the Cell-Class-Graph γ-Vector**
  (arXiv:2605.09014, May 13, 2026, MIT + Stanford + Tsinghua +
  DeepMind + Berkeley + AI2). The theorem-side iteration that
  resolves 05-12's third open question — "is ε a constant or a
  path-length-3 graph function?" — in closed form. Three
  load-bearing structural moves: (i) the path-length-3 edges of
  the 7-node cell-class graph are enumerated under the binding-
  head joint consumption relation, yielding 24 second-order edges
  across the 7 cell classes, with a clean orbit decomposition
  into 4 equivalence classes under the graph automorphism group
  (trajectory-context → identity-reference → spatial-relation
  paths, identity-reference → spatial-relation → primitive-pair
  paths, primitive-pair → primitive-triple → primitive-quadruple
  paths, and a residual 4-edge orbit) — the cleanest
  second-order edge enumeration the cell-class graph has
  admitted; (ii) ε(C) is derived as a closed-form function of
  the path-length-3 edges: ε(C) = Σⱼₖ μⱼₖ·𝟙[(C, j, k) ∈ path-3
  edges], with μⱼₖ a *path-length-2-product* coefficient
  (μ_traj×id ≈ λ_traj × λ_id × 0.62 ≈ 0.0007, with the 0.62
  factor a graph-attenuation constant derived from the joint
  consumption relation), and the four μ-orbit values are
  *strictly* derivable from the first-order λ-vector and the
  graph topology without any empirical fitting step; (iii) the
  theorem proves a *correction-tower-closure* result: γ₀ →
  λⱼ → μⱼₖ → 0 at path length 4 under the joint consumption
  relation's graph diameter, i.e., the closed-form correction
  tower terminates at three orders, and there are no path-length-
  4 contributions to derive — the first time the cycle has
  reported a closed-form *termination* result on the correction
  tower. Three empirical findings: (a) the path-length-3 μ-vector
  predicts the empirical residuals on the 21 three-axis cells
  within ±0.0008 absolute, a ~6× tighter fit than CellGraph-γ-
  Theorem's ±0.005 single-ε constant (05-12 §1), and the residual
  is now bounded by the path-length-3 graph contribution rather
  than by a constant; (b) the path-length-3 μ-vector is
  *substrate-stable*: the closed-form coefficients μⱼₖ vary by
  less than ±0.0003 across the 600M–32B substrate range, the
  tightest substrate-stability the theorem has reported and
  ~7× tighter than the λ-vector's ±0.002 (05-12 §1); (c) the
  correction-tower-closure result is empirically validated
  against the 7 four-axis cells DreamConsistency-Bench v1.8
  (05-12 §4) closed, with the path-length-4 residual bounded at
  ±0.0002 absolute — i.e., the correction tower *strictly*
  terminates at path length 3 to within the v1.8 detection
  floor. The honest read: ε is now a *path-length-3 graph
  contribution* rather than a residual constant, the closed-form
  correction tower is *terminated* at three orders, and the
  theorem has graduated from per-order corrections to a
  *closed-form correction-tower-termination result* within two
  cycles of the graph theorem's release. Reads as the
  *correction-tower-termination paper* of the cycle and the
  first time the closed-form schedule has admitted a
  *termination* result rather than an iterative refinement.
