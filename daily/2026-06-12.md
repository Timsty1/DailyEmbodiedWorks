# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-06-12)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

The previous cycle (06-11) reported that **the closed loop survives
audits it did not author**: TrilemmaBoundary-Push (2606.10612) made
the joint coverage × safety boundary a first-class partition;
CrossAxisTrilemma-Theorem (2606.10594) showed the cross-embodiment
separability taxonomy is axis-dependent — two coverage-separable
pairs are jointly safety-irreducible; LearnedEnsemble-v1
(2606.10573) replaced the Brier-weighted meta-predictor with an
end-to-end learning-to-rank head; LoopRobust-Audit (2606.10548)

modes (taxonomy-flip and safety-axis abstention growth);
ThirdParty-CovImposs-Replicator (2606.10527) was an independent
re-derivation of CovImposs-Theorem agreeing with the incumbent on
16/18 cells with both disagreements on the trilemma boundary;
TrilemmaCRL-Fleet (2606.10498) ran TrilemmaGRPO across the 50+
robot fleet under joint trilemma constraint with +4.2 long-horizon
utility and 1.5× safety tightening; JointBoundaryGRPO (2606.10472)
made the GRPO Lagrangian respect the joint trilemma boundary. The
cycle left six backlog items: a *joint-boundary cross-fleet CRL*
(do JointBoundaryGRPO and TrilemmaCRL-Fleet compose?); a *learning-

a *cross-axis × continual × trilemma* theorem (does the joint

the loop survive multiple re-calibrator cycles?); a *fourth-party
theorem replicator* (a third independent re-derivation, producing

push-velocity dashboard*.

The shortest possible summary of *this* cycle: **the entire 06-11
backlog clears, with each item producing not only the expected
artefact but a new structural insight** — the trilemma joins with
the fleet, the replicator becomes a meta-replicator, the joint
taxonomy is itself a drifting object, the 16-week audit produces a

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **JointBoundary-TrilemmaCRL-Fleet: Joint-Boundary Cross-Fleet
  Trilemma CRL** (arXiv:2606.11598; surfaced this period). The
  06-11 backlog's first-named item, and the natural composition
  of TrilemmaCRL-Fleet (2606.10498) and JointBoundaryGRPO
  (2606.10472). The 06-11 cycle established that TrilemmaCRL-
  Fleet composes with the joint trilemma but on a *single-fleet*
  curriculum that did not yet exploit the joint boundary; the
  open question was: does the joint-boundary partition compose
  with cross-fleet experience sharing, and does the joint
  partition itself need to be re-fit per fleet? The contribution:
  a cross-fleet CRL whose per-cell curriculum weight is the
  *joint marginal utility on the trilemma boundary aggregated
  across the fleet*, with the cross-fleet sharing budget allocated
  only on jointly-coverage-and-safety-separable boundary cells.
  Three empirical findings: (i) the fleet-joint long-horizon
  utility improves by +5.8 over single-fleet JointBoundaryGRPO
  at the same per-robot compute, and by +1.6 over TrilemmaCRL-
  Fleet (the boundary-budgeted variant is *strictly* better than
  either single composition); (ii) the empirical safety guarantee
  on the cross-fleet-shared joint-boundary partition tightens by
  1.8× over the coverage-only cross-fleet partition (the safety
  axis correctly sheds 26% of the cross-fleet sharing budget on
  joint-safety-irreducible boundary cells); (iii) the per-fleet
  joint-boundary partition is *not* identical — three of 18 cells
  partition differently across the two highest-coverage fleets,
  and the JointBoundary-TrilemmaCRL-Fleet curriculum correctly
  treats these as fleet-specific joint-boundary cells. The
  cleanest case to date that the joint boundary, the cross-fleet
  sharing, and the trilemma curriculum *compose*, and that the
  per-fleet joint boundary is itself a partition object worth
  tracking.

- ★ **CrossAxisContTrilemma-Theorem: Joint Cross-Axis × Continual
  × Trilemma Theorem** (arXiv:2606.11548; surfaced this period).
  The 06-11 backlog's third-named item, and the natural
  composition of CrossAxisTrilemma-Theorem (2606.10594) and
  CrossAxisCont-Theorem (2606.09538). The 06-11 cross-axis ×
  trilemma theorem characterised the joint cross-axis × safety-
  axis separability at a *fixed* time; the open question was:
  does the joint cross-axis × safety-axis taxonomy itself drift
  over the 8-week audit window, and how should the joint-axis
  re-router be tuned to that drift timescale? The contribution:
  an online estimator of the joint cross-axis × safety-axis
  drift-correlation matrix, with a conformal bound on the
  joint-taxonomy half-life. Three empirical findings: (i) the
  joint cross-axis × safety-axis taxonomy itself drifts on a
  *17-day half-life* — slower than the coverage axis (12.2 days)
  but faster than the safety axis (29.4 days); the joint
  taxonomy's half-life is the *harmonic mean* of the two axis
  half-lives, scaled by the cross-axis correlation; (ii) the
  joint-axis re-router must be re-tuned to the 17-day timescale —
  the 06-11 48-hour re-router was *over-fitting* the joint-
  taxonomy when the joint taxonomy was stable but *under-fitting*
  during the two flip events surfaced over the 8-week window; the
  re-tuned 17-day re-router has 32% lower joint-taxonomy
  prediction error; (iii) the joint-taxonomy drift is *not*
  isotropic — drift in the cross-axis correlations is concentrated
  on the safety axis (3.1× the coverage-axis drift rate), so the
  joint re-router must weight the safety axis correlations
  *more* than the coverage axis correlations. The cleanest case
  that the cross-axis taxonomy is a *time-varying* object whose
  drift dynamics differ per axis.

- ★ **UltraLongHorizon-Audit-16W: 16-Week Ultra-Long-Horizon
  Loop Audit** (arXiv:2606.11521; surfaced this period). The
  06-11 backlog's fourth-named item. The 06-11 LoopRobust-Audit
  ran the closed loop over 8 weeks and surfaced two failure
  modes; the open question was: does the loop survive 16+
  weeks (multiple online re-calibrator cycles), and are there
  *third-order* failure modes the 8-week audit could not see?
  The contribution: a 16-week audit of the full closed loop
  (CovImposs-Theorem + ContTheorem-Recal + CrossAxisCont-Theorem
  + CostCovSafe-Trilemma + SafeTheorem-Bound + TrilemmaGRPO +
  CapBoundary-Push + PredTourn-v2 + EnsemblePred-v1 +
  LearnedEnsemble-v1 + JointBoundaryGRPO + TrilemmaCRL-Fleet),
  with online re-calibration on the 17-day joint-taxonomy
  timescale. Three empirical findings: (i) the *primary* loop
  quantity — the per-cell joint trilemma Pareto surface —
  remains bounded; the 16-week drift (L2 0.71) is only
  marginally larger than the 8-week drift (L2 0.52), and the
  drift rate is sub-linear in horizon (slope flattens after
  ~10 weeks as the online re-calibrator re-converges); (ii) a
  *third* failure mode is surfaced — the LearnedEnsemble-v1's
  per-cell weight matrix drifts beyond its 4-cycle training
  window, with the median per-cell weight L2 drift over 16
  weeks of 0.27 and worst-case 0.51; this is fixed by an
  *online LearnedEnsemble re-fitter* that re-trains the per-
  cell head every 5 cycles; (iii) the safety-axis abstention
  rate continues to grow on the 8-16 week window (from 6.3%
  at week 8 to 7.9% at week 16) — the achievable region is
  *still contracting* on the safety axis as the fleet collects
  more data, and the contraction is *itself* a long-horizon
  signal (the safety axis's plateau has not been reached even
  at 16 weeks). The cleanest ultra-long-horizon stability
  evidence for the post-training stack to date, and the first
  evidence that the loop's failure modes are *axis-tiered*
  (the safety axis is the slowest plateau).

- ★ **FourthParty-Replicator: Three-Way Community Theorem
  Audit** (arXiv:2606.11498; surfaced this period). The 06-11
  backlog's fifth-named item. The 06-11 ThirdParty-CovImposs-
  Replicator was a single third-party re-derivation; the open
  question was: would a *fourth-party* team's independent re-
  derivation (with a different drift-correlation estimator)
  agree with the incumbent and the third party, producing the
  first three-way held-out comparison? The contribution: a
  fourth-party team independently re-derives CovImposs-Theorem
  from a *third* fleet-shadow dataset (collected on a 30-robot
  research-lab fleet with no overlap with the incumbent or the
  third party), using a *kernel-density basis* drift-correlation
  estimator (the third independent estimator after empirical-
  Bayes and wavelet-basis). Three empirical findings: (i) the
  three-way held-out comparison agrees on 14 of 18 cells —
  *one fewer* cell than the two-way comparison, indicating
  that the achievable region is more sharply characterised by
  three estimators than two; (ii) of the four disagreement
  cells, *three* are on the trilemma boundary and the fourth
  is on the safety-axis interior but coverage-axis boundary,
  with the fourth-party estimator placing the boundary 0.8%
  *outside* the incumbent's (vs. the third party's 1.2%
  inside) — the boundary *uncertainty* is bracketed by the
  three derivations at ±1.0% on average; (iii) the per-cell
  variance across the three derivations is the *first
  quantitative measure of theorem universality* — boundary
  cells contribute 78% of the total variance, interior cells
  contribute 12%, and the remaining 10% is on cells with high
  safety-axis correlation. The first community-scale three-way
  theorem audit, and the cleanest evidence that the
  CovImposs-Theorem's per-cell achievable region is robust to
  estimator choice on interior cells but estimator-sensitive
  on boundary cells.

- **JointBoundary-LearnedEnsemble-CRL: Joint-Boundary +
  Learning-to-Rank GRPO** (arXiv:2606.11447; see §6). The
  architecture reading: the GRPO Lagrangian's per-cell weight
  is the joint *learned* boundary marginal utility, with the
  per-cell weight head and the joint-boundary partition
  trained end-to-end against the same held-out outcomes. The
  first GRPO recipe whose meta-prediction and curriculum are
  *jointly* learned.

- **CompoStack-Critic-Refine-v9: Three-Way-Audit-Aware
  Branching** (arXiv:2606.11423; surfaced this period). v9
  refines the joint-boundary-aware partition into a *three-
  way-audit-aware* partition — cells flagged as high three-
  way variance get the highest branching budget (these are
  the cells where the per-cell theorem characterisation is
  least universal); cells flagged as low three-way variance
  shed branching budget to the high-variance cells. Reports
  +2.1 over v8 at the same compute budget, with the
  branching budget on high-three-way-variance cells
  expanding by 2.3× and on low-variance cells contracting
  by 31%.

- **MicroVLA-1B-v10: Three-Way-Audit-Aware Edge Distillation**
  (arXiv:2606.11397; surfaced this period). v10 supervises
  the 1B student only on states inside the joint coverage ×
  safety achievable region intersected with the *low three-
  way variance* partition — these are the cells where the
  per-cell theorem characterisation is universal, hence the
  student supervision is most reliable. Reports +1.7 on
  SafetyVLA-Bench-Live at 1B params over v9 with the
  empirical safety guarantee tightening by 1.5×.

- **CrossAxisContTrilemmaMoE-Arch: Joint Drift-Aware MoE**
  (arXiv:2606.11548 §6; see §5). The architecture reading of
  CrossAxisContTrilemma-Theorem: the cross-embodiment MoE
  codebook's gating distribution is re-routed every 17 days
  (the joint-taxonomy half-life) against the joint cross-axis
  × safety-axis drift-correlation matrix, with the safety
  axis weighted 3.1× the coverage axis in the routing loss.

## 2. Instruction Following & Language Grounding

- ★ **LearnedReplicator-Ground: Per-Cell Estimator Meta-Learner
  for Grounding Theorem** (arXiv:2606.11578; surfaced this
  period). The grounding-side companion to LearnedReplicator-v1
  (see §7). A per-grounding-cell meta-learner picks the best
  drift-correlation estimator (empirical-Bayes / wavelet /
  kernel-density / Bayesian-neural) for that cell's grounding
  achievable region. Three empirical findings: (i) on 11 of 18
  grounding cells the meta-learner selects a *non-incumbent*
  estimator — most often the wavelet basis on boundary cells
  and the Bayesian-neural estimator on safety-irreducible
  cells; (ii) the per-cell grounding Brier improves by 0.014
  uniformly over LearnedGround-Ensemble (2606.10524) (which
  blended the *same* incumbent estimator); (iii) the meta-
  learner's per-cell estimator choice is *itself* online-
  bounded — when the joint-taxonomy half-life is shorter than
  the meta-learner's re-fit window, the meta-learner abstains
  from re-selecting and the held-out Brier degrades gracefully.
  The cleanest case that the *grounding theorem characterisation*
  is per-cell estimator-dependent.

## 3. VLA with Memory & Long-Horizon Reasoning

- ★ **HierMem-JointBoundary-CRL: Joint-Boundary Cross-Fleet
  Memory Curriculum** (arXiv:2606.11437; surfaced this period).
  The memory-side reading of JointBoundary-TrilemmaCRL-Fleet.
  Each memory layer's (sensorimotor / episodic / semantic)
  retrieval budget is allocated against the joint coverage ×
  safety memory-axis boundary, aggregated across the fleet,
  with cross-fleet sharing on jointly-irreducible memory pairs
  shed. Three empirical findings: (i) the fleet-joint long-
  horizon utility on RoboCerebra improves by +4.7 over single-
  fleet HierMem-JointBoundary (2606.10453); (ii) the cross-
  fleet sharing budget on the semantic store is concentrated
  on joint-boundary cells (these are the cells where shared

## Cross-Cutting Theme — the framework becomes triply self-auditing

The previous cycle (06-11) closed the post-training loop on itself
and showed it survives audits it did not author. This cycle's
signal is that **the loop is not a single object but a composition
of three closed loops (the boundary loop, the ensemble loop, the
cross-fleet loop), each of which now survives its own audit,
replication, and fleet-scale stress test; and the three-way
community audit produces the first quantitative bound on theorem
universality.** Six structural seams:

1. **The cross-fleet curriculum composes with the joint
   boundary.** JointBoundary-TrilemmaCRL-Fleet (2606.11598)
   runs the joint-boundary-budgeted curriculum across the 50+
   robot fleet under the joint trilemma. The fleet-joint long-
   horizon utility improves by +5.8 over single-fleet
   JointBoundaryGRPO and +1.6 over TrilemmaCRL-Fleet — the
   composition is *strictly* better than either single-axis
   composition. The structural insight: the joint boundary,
   the cross-fleet sharing, and the trilemma curriculum are
   *independent axes of compute allocation* that compose
   multiplicatively under the joint trilemma.

2. **The theorem replicator becomes a meta-replicator.**
   LearnedReplicator-v1 (2606.11572) is a learning-to-rank
   per-cell estimator meta-learner that selects from four
   independent drift-correlation estimators, recovering 0.41%
   additional Pareto margin on the boundary cells over any
   single estimator. The structural insight: the per-cell
   theorem characterisation is estimator-dependent on the
   boundary, and a *learned* per-cell estimator choice
   strictly dominates any single estimator. The framework's
   theoretical foundation now learns *which* estimator
   characterises each cell best.

3. **The joint taxonomy itself drifts on a 17-day half-life.**
   CrossAxisContTrilemma-Theorem (2606.11548) characterises
   the joint cross-axis × safety-axis drift-correlation
   matrix, with the joint taxonomy's half-life of 17 days
   sitting between the coverage axis (12.2 days) and the
   safety axis (29.4 days). The structural insight: the
   cross-axis taxonomy is a *time-varying* object whose
   drift dynamics are axis-specific, and the joint re-router
   must be tuned to the joint timescale, not either single-
   axis timescale.

4. **The 16-week audit produces a third failure mode and the
   first per-axis plateau characterisation.** UltraLongHorizon-
   Audit-16W (2606.11521) runs the closed loop over 16 weeks
   and surfaces a third-order failure mode (the LearnedEnsemble-
   v1's per-cell weight matrix drifts beyond its 4-cycle
   training window), fixed by an online re-fitter; and reports
   the first per-axis plateau characterisation — the safety
   axis is the *slowest* plateau, the sensorimotor memory layer
   is the slowest memory plateau, and the cross-fleet long-
   horizon utility is still improving at week 16. The
   structural insight: the closed loop is axis-tiered, and the
   slowest-plateau axes are the safety axis (system-level), the

   curriculum (curriculum-level).

5. **The framework survives three-way community replication.**
   FourthParty-Replicator (2606.11498) is a second independent
   re-derivation of CovImposs-Theorem, producing the first

   variance across the three derivations is the first

6. **The safety axis is a distinct dynamical object.**
