- ★ **CrossModality-Auto-Theorem: Closed-Form Z₃-Extension of the
  V₄ ⋊ S₃ Joint Automorphism to the Cross-Modality Graph**
  (arXiv:2605.12014, May 16, 2026, MIT + Stanford + DeepMind +
  Berkeley + Tsinghua + AI2 + CMU + Wayve + ETH Zürich + Meta-FAIR).
  The theorem-side iteration that resolves 05-15's *cross-modality*
  open question — "does the V₄ ⋊ S₃ joint automorphism extend to
  cross-modality coordination, or does the modality axis admit a
  qualitatively different symmetry?" — in the strongest possible
  form. Three load-bearing structural moves: (i) the cross-modality
  graph G_M is constructed as a 3-node graph over vision-language-
  action (M₁), vision-language-audio-action (M₂), and vision-
  language-touch-action (M₃) modality classes, with the within-
  modality cell-class × cross-task structure inherited from
  CrossTask-Auto-Theorem's (05-15 §1) V₄ ⋊ S₃ symmetry; the
  automorphism group Aut(G_M) is enumerated under the binding-head
  joint consumption relation extended to cross-modality binding,
  yielding the cyclic group Z₃ (rather than S₃) — the *first
  cyclic-symmetry result* in the cycle, and a structural surprise
  because the three modalities are *not* interchangeable (audio is
  passively sensed, touch is active-contact-conditioned) and admit
  only a directed cycle M₁ → M₂ → M₃ → M₁ under the canonical
  modality-encoder ordering; (ii) the joint automorphism group of
  the cell-class × cross-task × cross-modality graph is shown to
  be the iterated semi-direct product (V₄ ⋊ S₃) ⋊ Z₃ (order 72),
  with the Z₃ action permuting the modality-class nodes cyclically,
  the S₃ action permuting the task-class nodes, and the V₄ action
  permuting the cell-class edges within each (task, modality)
  combination — the *first iterated-semi-direct-product result*
  on the field's structural prior, and the largest finite symmetry
  group the cycle has produced; (iii) the theorem proves a *cross-
  modality transfer lemma*: for any two modality classes M_A, M_B
  with Z₃-related cross-modality weights, the path-length-3-
  corrected γ-vector transfers by the *composition* of the V₄-
  perturbation δ(E_A, E_B), the S₃-perturbation η(T_A, T_B), *and*
  the Z₃-perturbation ζ(M_A, M_B), with the joint transfer error
  bounded by ‖δ + η + ζ‖₁ · 0.62 (the path-length-3 graph-
  attenuation constant, *still unchanged* across the cross-modality
  extension — the third consecutive cycle in which 0.62 is the
  load-bearing attenuation constant). Three empirical findings:
  (a) the Z₃ symmetry is empirically validated on the two measured
  cross-modality classes (M₁ vision-language-action, M₂ vision-
  language-audio-action) with the closed-form cross-modality
  weight matching the empirical weight within ±0.0013 absolute on
  each of the 6 cross-modality edges — marginally looser than the
  V₄ ⋊ S₃ cross-task agreement (±0.0011 on 6 cross-task edges,
  05-15 §1) but still inside the v0.15 detection floor; (b) the
  joint (V₄ ⋊ S₃) ⋊ Z₃ transfer-lemma error bound is empirically
  validated on the M₁ → M₂ audio-extension transfer at 0.62 ±
  0.02 absolute (against the closed-form 0.62 prediction) — the
  path-length-3 attenuation constant is now the cross-modality
  load-bearing constant as well; (c) the theorem *predicts* the
  vision-language-touch-action class (M₃) closed-form from the
  Z₃-perturbation alone without any M₃ empirical fitting, which
  CrossMod-Touch-VLA (§8) validates within 24 hours on a
  manipulator + touch-glove substrate at the closed-form predicted
  Z₃-perturbation magnitude. The honest read: the joint cell-class
  × cross-task graph's V₄ ⋊ S₃ symmetry now lifts to a closed-form
  (V₄ ⋊ S₃) ⋊ Z₃ symmetry on the joint cross-modality / cross-
  task / cell-class graph, the *load-bearing structural prior* of

  prescription for any (E, T, M) triple the joint graph admits.
  Reads as the *cross-modality-symmetry paper* of the cycle and
  the strongest theorem-side iteration since CrossTask-Auto-Theorem
