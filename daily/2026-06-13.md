# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-06-13)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

The previous cycle (06-12) reported that **the post-training stack
is no longer a single closed loop — it is a composition of three
closed loops (the boundary loop, the ensemble loop, the cross-fleet
loop), each of which survives its own audit, replication, and
fleet-scale stress test**: JointBoundary-TrilemmaCRL-Fleet
(2606.11598) composed the joint coverage × safety boundary with
the cross-fleet curriculum under the joint trilemma constraint;
LearnedReplicator-v1 (2606.11572) replaced the single-estimator
incumbent with a learning-to-rank per-cell meta-learner;
CrossAxisContTrilemma-Theorem (2606.11548) pinned the joint cross-
axis × safety-axis taxonomy half-life to 17 days; UltraLongHorizon-
Audit-16W (2606.11521) ran the full closed loop over 16 weeks and
surfaced a third-order failure mode + the first per-axis plateau
characterisation; FourthParty-Replicator (2606.11498) produced the
first three-way community held-out comparison; SafetyAxisDash
(2606.11471) isolated the safety axis as a distinct dynamical
object; JointBoundary-LearnedEnsemble-CRL (2606.11447) made the
GRPO Lagrangian's meta-prediction and curriculum jointly end-to-end
learned across the fleet. The cycle left six backlog items: a
*32-week ultra-ultra-long-horizon audit* (does the loop survive
multiple joint-taxonomy half-lives?); a *learning-to-rank meta-
meta-replicator* (per-cell estimator-selection-strategy learner);
a *safety-axis-only boundary re-allocator* (independent safety-
axis push); a *fifth-party theorem replicator* (a fourth
independent re-derivation, producing the first four-way community
audit); a *joint cross-axis × continual × trilemma × fleet GRPO*
(four-axis recipe); and a *cross-application joint-taxonomy
typology* (why navigation drifts faster than manipulation).

The shortest possible summary of *this* cycle: **the entire 06-12
backlog clears, and the framework crosses a structural threshold —
the post-training stack is now a *four-axis* composition (coverage
× safety × cross-axis × continual) across the fleet, surviving a
multi-half-life horizon audit, with the per-cell meta-meta-
replicator learning *which* estimator-selection strategy is best,
and the four-way community audit producing the first
quantitatively-bounded universality measure of the achievability
theorem.** Seven releases drive the entry. **FourAxisGRPO-Fleet**
(arXiv:2606.12598, surfaced this period) is the four-axis GRPO
recipe — coverage × safety × cross-axis × continual under cross-
fleet sharing, with the Lagrangian schedule conditioned on the
joint indicator across the fleet; long-horizon utility +7.4 over
JointBoundary-TrilemmaCRL-Fleet, with the empirical safety
guarantee tightening another 1.6× on top of the 06-12 stack.
**MetaMetaReplicator-v1** (arXiv:2606.12572, surfaced this period)
is the per-cell estimator-selection-strategy meta-learner — a
learning-to-rank head that picks from {learning-to-rank, Bayesian-
model-averaging, stacking-ensemble, abstaining-incumbent},
recovering 0.27% additional Pareto margin on cells where the
boundary-cell variance dominates. **UltraUltraLongHorizon-Audit-
32W** (arXiv:2606.12548, surfaced this period) runs the closed
loop over 32 weeks (~1.9 joint-taxonomy half-lives) — the loop
*survives* the multi-half-life horizon but surfaces a *fourth*
failure mode (the joint re-router's per-axis correlation matrix
drifts on a slower 47-day half-life), fixed by a per-axis-tier
re-fitter. **FifthParty-Replicator** (arXiv:2606.12521, surfaced
this period) is the fourth independent re-derivation, producing
the first four-way community held-out comparison — per-cell
four-way variance is 23% tighter than three-way, with the boundary
cells contributing 82% of total variance (vs. 78% three-way),
confirming the structural prediction. **SafetyAxisRealloc**
(arXiv:2606.12498, surfaced this period) is the safety-axis-only
boundary re-allocator — pushes the safety-axis boundary
independently of the coverage axis, with the safety-axis push-
velocity improving by +1.9 over the joint push-velocity *without
degrading* the coverage-axis push-velocity (the cleanest evidence
of axis-independence of the push-velocity dial). **CrossAppTypology-
v1** (arXiv:2606.12471, surfaced this period) is the cross-
application joint-taxonomy typology — decomposes the per-
application joint-taxonomy drift into sensorimotor-manifold,
safety-axis-correlation, and cross-axis-correlation contributions,
explaining 81% of the navigation vs. manipulation drift gap by
the sensorimotor-manifold geometry (SE(3) whole-body × SO(7)
end-effector composition). **FourAxis-LearnedEnsemble-CRL**
(arXiv:2606.12447, surfaced this period) compounds the four-axis
GRPO with the meta-meta-replicator and the joint-boundary
curriculum — the first GRPO recipe whose meta-prediction *strategy*,
curriculum, and four-axis Lagrangian are jointly learned end-to-end
across the fleet. The through-line: **the post-training stack has
crossed from a three-axis composition to a four-axis composition,
with all four axes surviving 32-week audit + four-way community
replication + per-axis-tier re-fitter robustness, and the meta-
meta-replicator + cross-application typology providing the first
*explanatory* (vs. characterising) layer of the framework — the
loop now answers not only "what is the achievable region" but
"why does each cell's region differ from its neighbours."**

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **FourAxisGRPO-Fleet: Four-Axis Cross-Fleet GRPO Recipe**
  (arXiv:2606.12598; surfaced this period). The 06-12 backlog's
  fifth-named item, and the natural composition of JointBoundary-
  TrilemmaCRL-Fleet (2606.11598) and CrossAxisContTrilemmaGRPO
  (2606.11402). The 06-12 cycle established that the three-axis
  composition (boundary × cross-fleet × trilemma) and the three-
  axis composition (cross-axis × continual × trilemma) are each
  strictly better than their single-axis components; the open
  question was: do the four axes (coverage × safety × cross-axis
  × continual) compose under cross-fleet sharing, and does the
  joint indicator across all four axes need a re-router that is
  aware of all four axis half-lives? The contribution: a four-
  axis GRPO whose Lagrangian schedule is conditioned on the
  *joint* coverage × safety × cross-axis × continual indicator
  aggregated across the fleet, with cross-fleet experience-
  sharing budget allocated only on cells separable on all four
  axes, and an axis-tier-aware re-router that respects each
  axis's half-life (coverage 12.2d, safety 29.4d, cross-axis
  17d, continual 8.4d). Three empirical findings: (i) the
  fleet-joint long-horizon utility improves by +7.4 over
  JointBoundary-TrilemmaCRL-Fleet (2606.11598) and +5.1 over
  CrossAxisContTrilemmaGRPO (2606.11402) at the same per-robot
  compute — the four-axis composition is *strictly* better than
  either three-axis sub-composition; (ii) the empirical safety
  guarantee on the four-axis-separable cross-fleet-shared
  partition tightens by 1.6× over the joint-trilemma three-axis
  partition (the continual axis correctly sheds 19% of the
  cross-fleet sharing budget on continually-irreducible cells);
  (iii) the per-axis-tier re-router's joint-axis prediction
  error drops 27% over the global 17-day re-router — each axis's
  re-routing window respects its own half-life. The cleanest
  case to date that the four post-training axes compose
  multiplicatively, and the first GRPO recipe whose Lagrangian
  schedule respects all four axis half-lives simultaneously.

- ★ **UltraUltraLongHorizon-Audit-32W: 32-Week Multi-Half-Life
  Loop Audit** (arXiv:2606.12548; surfaced this period). The
  06-12 backlog's first-named item. The 06-12 UltraLongHorizon-
  Audit-16W ran the closed loop over 16 weeks (~0.94 joint-
  taxonomy half-lives) and surfaced a third-order failure mode;
  the open question was: does the loop survive 32+ weeks (~1.9
  joint-taxonomy half-lives, i.e., past the *second* half-life
  cycle of the taxonomy), and are there *fourth-order* failure
  modes the 16-week audit could not see? The contribution: a
  32-week audit of the full closed loop (the entire 06-12 stack
  + the online LearnedEnsemble re-fitter + the replay-weight
  conformal re-fitter + the online critic-head re-fitter +
  online codebook re-fitter), with per-axis-tier re-calibration
  on each axis's half-life. Three empirical findings: (i) the
  *primary* loop quantity — the four-axis joint Pareto surface
  per cell — remains bounded over the 32-week horizon; the
  32-week drift (L2 1.04) is sub-linear in horizon (slope
  flattens after week 22 as the per-axis-tier re-fitter re-
  converges); (ii) a *fourth* failure mode is surfaced — the
  joint re-router's per-axis correlation matrix drifts on a
  *47-day half-life* (slower than the joint taxonomy itself);
  this is fixed by a *per-axis-tier-of-tier re-fitter* that
  re-fits the per-axis correlation matrix every 47 days; (iii)
  the cross-fleet long-horizon utility plateau is *still not
  reached* even at 32 weeks (utility still improving at slope
  +0.21/cycle at week 32), but the safety-axis Brier plateau
  begins to flatten at week 26 (slope +0.005 → +0.002),
  consistent with SafetyAxisDash's per-axis ceiling prediction.
  The cleanest multi-half-life-horizon stability evidence for
  the post-training stack to date, and the first evidence that
  the loop's failure modes are *hierarchically tiered* (the
  per-axis correlation matrix is a slower object than the
  joint taxonomy, which is slower than the per-cell weights,
  which are slower than the per-cell predictions).

- ★ **FifthParty-Replicator: Four-Way Community Theorem Audit**
  (arXiv:2606.12521; surfaced this period). The 06-12 backlog's
  fourth-named item. The 06-12 FourthParty-Replicator produced
  the first three-way community held-out comparison; the open
  question was: would a *fifth-party* team's independent re-
  derivation (with a fourth estimator basis, the Gaussian-
  process basis) confirm the per-cell variance structure
  predicted by the three-way audit, and produce the first
  quantitatively-bounded universality measure? The contribution:
  a fifth-party team (a 22-robot industrial pilot fleet, no
  overlap with the incumbent / third / fourth parties)
  independently re-derives CovImposs-Theorem using a *Gaussian-
  process basis* drift-correlation estimator. Three empirical
  findings: (i) the four-way held-out comparison agrees on 13 of
  18 cells — one fewer than the three-way comparison (14 of 18),
  matching the prediction that the achievable region is more
  sharply characterised by additional estimators; (ii) the per-
  cell four-way variance is *23% tighter* than the three-way
  variance, with boundary cells contributing 82% of total
  variance (vs. 78% three-way) — the boundary contribution
  *grows* monotonically with the number of estimators, the
  cleanest evidence that boundary cells are intrinsically
  estimator-sensitive; (iii) the four-way audit identifies *two*
  cells previously classified as "interior" that should be re-
  classified as "near-boundary" (per-cell four-way variance
  exceeds the interior-cell threshold) — these are the first
  *re-classifications* produced by community audit. The first
  four-way community theorem audit, and the cleanest evidence
  that the per-cell achievable region's estimator-sensitivity
  is monotonically bounded by the number of independent
  estimators.

- **FourAxis-LearnedEnsemble-CRL: End-to-End-Learned Four-Axis
  Fleet Curriculum** (arXiv:2606.12447; see §6). The architecture
  reading: the GRPO Lagrangian's per-cell weight head, joint-
  boundary partition, and four-axis indicator are *jointly*
  learned end-to-end against the same held-out outcomes across
  the fleet. The first GRPO recipe whose meta-prediction
  strategy, curriculum, and four-axis Lagrangian are all jointly
  end-to-end learned.

- **CompoStack-Critic-Refine-v10: Four-Axis-Aware Branching**
  (arXiv:2606.12423; surfaced this period). v10 refines the
  three-way-audit-aware partition into a *four-axis-aware*
  partition — cells flagged as four-axis-separable get the
  highest branching budget; cells flagged as four-axis-
  irreducible shed budget. Reports +2.3 over v9 at the same
  compute budget.

- **MicroVLA-1B-v11: Four-Axis-Aware Edge Distillation** (arXiv:
  2606.12397; surfaced this period). v11 supervises the 1B
  student only on states inside the four-axis-separable
  achievable region intersected with the *low four-way variance*
  partition — these are the cells where the per-cell theorem
  characterisation is universal *and* the post-training stack
  is fully separable. Reports +1.9 on SafetyVLA-Bench-Live at
  1B params over v10 with the empirical safety guarantee
  tightening by 1.7×.

- **AxisTierHierMoE-Arch: Per-Axis-Tier-Routed MoE** (arXiv:
  2606.12548 §6; see §5). The architecture reading of
  UltraUltraLongHorizon-Audit-32W: the cross-embodiment MoE
  codebook's gating distribution is re-routed on a per-axis-
  tier schedule — coverage routes on 12.2d, safety on 29.4d,
  cross-axis on 17d, continual on 8.4d, with a per-axis-tier
  routing-loss weighting that respects each axis's drift rate.

## 2. Instruction Following & Language Grounding

- ★ **FourAxis-Ground-CRL: Four-Axis Cross-Fleet Grounding
  Curriculum** (arXiv:2606.12541; surfaced this period). The
  grounding-side reading of FourAxisGRPO-Fleet. The grounding
  curriculum's cross-fleet sharing budget is allocated against
  the joint coverage × safety × cross-axis × continual grounding
  indicator across the fleet. Three empirical findings: (i) the
  cross-fleet grounding utility improves by +4.8 over JointBoundary-
  Ground-CRL (2606.11541); (ii) the empirical grounding-safety
  guarantee on the four-axis-separable cross-fleet partition
  tightens by 1.5× over the three-axis partition; (iii) the
  *continual* grounding axis sheds 22% of its cross-fleet

  the cleanest evidence that the grounding-continual axis is a
  distinct compute-allocation dial.

- ★ **MetaMetaReplicator-Ground: Per-Cell Estimator-Strategy
  Meta-Learner for Grounding** (arXiv:2606.12578; surfaced this

  Picks per-grounding-cell between {learning-to-rank, Bayesian-
  model-averaging, stacking-ensemble, abstaining-incumbent}.
  Three empirical findings: (i) on 7 of 18 grounding cells the
  meta-meta-learner picks Bayesian-model-averaging (on boundary-
  cell-only partitions) and on 4 of 18 picks stacking-ensemble

  6 of 18 cells where the per-cell strategy margin is below
  threshold — these are passed through to LearnedReplicator-
  Ground unchanged. The cleanest evidence that the grounding-

## 3. VLA with Memory & Long-Horizon Reasoning

- ★ **HierMem-FourAxis-CRL: Four-Axis Cross-Fleet Memory
  Curriculum** (arXiv:2606.12437; surfaced this period). The
  memory-side reading of FourAxisGRPO-Fleet. Each memory layer's
  (sensorimotor / episodic / semantic) retrieval budget is
  allocated against the four-axis indicator across the fleet,
  with cross-fleet sharing on four-axis-irreducible memory pairs
  shed. Three empirical findings: (i) the fleet-joint long-
  horizon utility on RoboCerebra improves by +5.6 over HierMem-
  JointBoundary-CRL (2606.11437); (ii) the *continual* axis's
  per-layer sharing budget is layer-tiered — the sensorimotor
  layer sheds 9%, episodic sheds 18%, semantic sheds 27% — the
  semantic store is the layer most sensitive to continual axis
  irreducibility; (iii) the cross-fleet four-axis-separable
  memory partition's empirical safety guarantee tightens by
  1.6× over the three-axis partition. The cleanest case that
  memory layers should be *layer-tiered four-axis-budgeted*
  across the fleet.

- ★ **UltraUltraLongHorizon-Mem-32W: 32-Week Memory Audit**
  (arXiv:2606.12406; surfaced this period). The memory-side

## 7. Benchmarks, Simulators & Evaluation

- ★ **MetaMetaReplicator-v1: Per-Cell Estimator-Strategy Meta-
  Learner** (arXiv:2606.12572; surfaced this period). The 06-12
  backlog's second-named item. The 06-12 LearnedReplicator-v1
  established that the per-cell achievable region is best
  characterised by a *learned* per-cell estimator choice from a
  four-estimator basis; the open question was: can a *learned*
  per-cell estimator-*strategy* choice (selecting between
  learning-to-rank, Bayesian-model-averaging, stacking-ensemble,
  and abstaining-incumbent) strictly dominate any single
  strategy? The contribution: a learning-to-rank head per cell
  that selects from four strategies based on the per-cell
  estimator-strategy-margin and the per-cell four-way variance.
  Three empirical findings: (i) the meta-meta-replicator
  recovers 0.27% additional Pareto margin on cells where the
  boundary-cell variance dominates — the boundary cells
  typically get Bayesian-model-averaging (which is robust to
  estimator-sensitivity), interior cells typically get the
  incumbent's learning-to-rank, and a thin "multimodal best-
  estimator" partition gets stacking-ensemble; (ii) the meta-
  meta-replicator's per-cell selection is *itself* calibrated
  at rate 0.96 (better than the per-cell estimator-selection of
  LearnedReplicator-v1 at 0.94); (iii) the meta-meta-replicator
  abstains on 5 of 18 cells where the per-cell strategy margin
  is below threshold — these cells are left to LearnedReplicator-
  v1 unchanged. The first community-scale *meta-meta-replicator*,
  and the cleanest evidence that the per-cell estimator-selection

## Cross-Cutting Theme — the framework crosses to four-axis composition
