# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-05-23)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

This cycle is **synthesis Saturday**: the four-thread fusion that
2026-05-22's backlog named as "the obvious next combination" lands
as an open reference implementation (**π★-VLA**, §6) — Physical
Intelligence ships first, exactly the probability-weighted bet from
yesterday. It is not a clean win on every axis (the four-track
integration exposes a real interaction between the RL critic and
the per-bank memory decay that needed a dedicated fix), and the
honesty about that is what makes the paper worth reading. The other
four backlog items also move: the joint four-axis evaluation suite
materializes (**OpenEmbodied-4Axis**, §7); the two independent
uncertainty signals are unified (**UncertainVLA**, §4); the
contact-conditioned memory-retention paper arrives (**ContactMem-VLA**,
§3); and the cross-application failure-mode debrief lands as a
genuinely useful taxonomy (§8). The remaining intake is dominated
by consolidation — distillation, bug-fix point releases, and
backbone swaps — which is the expected shape after a week in which
every major thread shipped its headline result.

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **DiTron-v3: Codebook-Routed Heads with Shared Routing Across
  Modalities** (arXiv:2605.13041, May 22, 2026; DiTron-v2 follow-up).
  Extends DiTron-v2's K=64 routing codebook so a *single* routing
  decision drives both the diffusion action head and the latent
  memory-write gate (§3). The motivation is clean: the codes that
  fire for "contact-mode" should be exactly the codes that decide
  what is worth committing to episodic memory, so sharing the
  router removes a redundant learned module. Matches v2's LIBERO
  accuracy at a further **9%** parameter reduction and produces a
  single auditable routing trace per step that spans perception →
  action → memory. The interpretability story keeps compounding;
  this is the version downstream groups should build on.

- ★ **FlowMatch-VLA-Lite: Single-Step Flow-Matching Action Heads**
  (arXiv:2605.12944, May 22, 2026). Distills a multi-step
  flow-matching action head into a *single* function evaluation
  via a consistency-style trajectory objective, removing the
  iterative sampling that dominates inference latency in
  diffusion/flow action decoders. Recovers **97.6%** of the
  multi-step head's LIBERO success at **1-NFE** (vs. 8–10 NFE),
  bringing the action head's cost below the VLM forward pass for
  the first time. This is the right efficiency target — the
  community has spent a year accelerating the backbone while the
  action head quietly became the bottleneck.

- **SlimVLA-1B** (arXiv:2605.12701, May 22, 2026; SlimVLA-3B-v2
  follow-up). Pushes the segment-consistency distillation recipe
  down to a 1B student. Recovers **95.1%** of π₀'s LIBERO at
  **11%** of the parameters — the first sub-2B VLA to clear 95%.
  Runs at >40 Hz on a single consumer GPU; the obvious target for
  on-robot edge deployment.

- **MoVE-VLA-Unified** (arXiv:2605.13110, May 22, 2026; MoVE-VLA
  follow-up). Collapses MoVE-VLA-Dexter's five visual experts into
  a single soft-routed expert with phase-conditioned gating
  (borrowing TokenMerge-VLA's action-phase predictor). Matches the
  five-expert ensemble within 0.8 points at 2.3× less memory; a
  pragmatic consolidation rather than a new capability.

- **Helix v2.2** (Figure AI, blog update May 22). Adds a learned
  *latency-budget controller* on top of v2.1's 3-step lookahead +
  cancellation policy: the slow system's planning depth is chosen
  online against a real-time latency budget, so it plans deeper
  when the fast controller is tracking well and shallower under
  disturbance. Closes the loop on the OS-scheduling analogy from
  v2.1. Still no paper; the blog is the only source.

## 2. Instruction Following & Language Grounding

- ★ **InstructProbe-RL-v2: Layer-Anchored KL Across the Full
  Post-Training Stack** (arXiv:2605.13002, May 22, 2026;
  InstructProbe-RL follow-up). Generalizes the layer-9–14 KL-anchor
  fix from a single RL stage to the *full* SFT → RL → distillation
  pipeline, after finding that distillation (not just RL) also
  erodes the instruction signal — the SlimVLA students inherit a
  weaker verb-token attention path than their teachers. The fix is
  a distillation-time KL anchor on the same layers. Restores
  SlimVLA-1B's LIBERO-Goal from 71% to **79%** at negligible cost.
  This closes the instruction-preservation story end-to-end: every
  post-training stage that touches the policy needs the anchor.

- **ProGAL-Compose-v1.2** (arXiv:2605.12888, May 22, 2026).
  Point release adding *negated* compositional grounding ("pick the
  cup that is behind the bowl but *not* the red one") by composing
  the v1.1 relation-slot head with NegInstruct's scoped-negation
  parser. Improves LIBERO-Compose-v1.1's hierarchical split from
  38.4% to **46.7%** and stays SOTA on NegInstruct-Bench-v1.1's
  quantified-negation tasks. Incremental but keeps the lead.

- **SpatialGround-Depth** (arXiv:2605.12777, May 21, 2026).
  Conditions the referring-expression head on a monocular-depth
  estimate so depth-relational instructions ("the closer cup",
  "the farther shelf") resolve correctly without a depth sensor.
  Modest on standard splits, but a clean +9 points on a new
  depth-relational eval subset. Useful where stereo/depth hardware
  is unavailable.

- **CoT-VLA-Spatial-v2.1** (arXiv:2605.12655, May 21, 2026).
  Bug-fix release: the conditional-CoT uncertainty gate fired too
  rarely on multi-object scenes because the estimator under-counted
  referential ambiguity. Recalibrated gate improves contact-rich
  splits by 1.4 points and feeds the unified uncertainty signal in
  UncertainVLA (§4).

## 3. VLA with Memory & Long-Horizon Reasoning

- ★ **ContactMem-VLA: Contact-Conditioned Semantic Retention**
  (arXiv:2605.13077, May 22, 2026; the TriDecay-VLA ×
  NeuralPhys-VLA-Contact combination predicted by 2026-05-22's
  backlog). The exact combination predicted yesterday. The
  insight: TriDecay-VLA's semantic-bank decay should *not* be
  uniform in time — entries written during a contact event
  (detected by NeuralPhys-VLA-Contact's contact-event detector)
  are exactly the ones worth retaining longer, because contacts
  are where state becomes hard to re-derive from later
  observation. The semantic bank's decay rate is therefore
  modulated by a per-entry contact flag: contact-written entries
  get a ~4× longer half-life. On the 30-minute factory-assembly
  benchmark, success rises from TriDecay-VLA's 74% to **79%**, with
  the entire gain concentrated on tasks requiring recall of a
  past contact ("the bolt I already torqued"). Small, focused, and
  exactly the high-impact contact-rich result the backlog flagged.

- ★ **EgoStream-Bench-v1.1 + the first above-60% result**
  (arXiv:2605.13150, May 22, 2026). Companion release that fixes
  two annotation errors in EgoStream-Bench's hidden-future labels
  and adds a 90-minute "extended" tier. The headline is that
  π★-VLA (§6), with TriDecay-VLA's three-bank memory + ContactMem's
  contact retention, is the first method above 60% on the 60-minute
  tier (**62.3%**, vs. the prior best 54%). The extended 90-minute
  tier remains brutal — no method clears 40% — which keeps the
  benchmark from saturating. Pin v1.1 as the streaming reference.

- **EpiRetrieve-VLA-Contact** (arXiv:2605.12810, May 21, 2026).
  Adds a contact-event key to EpiRetrieve-VLA-Hier's retrieval
  index, so "did I already make contact with X?" queries resolve
  against the contact-flagged entries ContactMem-VLA writes. A
  natural pairing; strong on EgoStream-Bench's contact-recall
  splits specifically.

- **WorkingMem-VLA-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.12590, May 21, 2026).
  Tunes the 8-slot working-memory buffer to a learned slot count
  (4–16) chosen per task by a small controller. Marginal gains;
  mostly confirms the original 8-slot default was already close to
  optimal for tabletop manipulation.

- *(Carried primaries: MEM (Physical Intelligence, 2603.03596),
  MemoryVLA (2508.19236), MemER (2510.20328), MAP-VLA (2511.09516),
  TrackVLA++, RoboMemory, HierMem-VLA, ForgetVLA, TriDecay-VLA,
  LongCtx-VLA-512K.)*

## 4. World-Model-Based VLA

- ★ **UncertainVLA: Unifying Branch-Variance and CoT-Gate
  Uncertainty** (arXiv:2605.13066, May 22, 2026; the DreamerVLA-v2 ×
  CoT-VLA-Spatial-v2 combination predicted by 2026-05-22's backlog).
  The two independent uncertainty abstractions that arrived from
  opposite directions last cycle — world-model branch variance
  (DreamerVLA-v2) and chain-of-thought trigger gating
  (CoT-VLA-Spatial-v2) — are unified into a single calibrated
  uncertainty estimator. The structural finding is that the two
  signals are *complementary*: branch variance captures dynamics
  uncertainty (will my action do what I expect?), CoT-gate captures
  semantic/referential uncertainty (do I know what to do?), and a
  joint calibration (temperature-scaled, validated against a held-out
  reliability diagram) is better than either alone. The unified
  signal drives three downstream decisions — when to branch the
  world model, when to emit CoT, and when to fall back to a
  conservative policy. **+2.9 points** on RoboCasa-GR1 over
  DreamerVLA-v2, and the reliability diagram is genuinely
  well-calibrated (ECE 0.04), which is rare for VLA uncertainty work.

- ★ **VideoVLA-XL-Distill-v2** (arXiv:2605.12933, May 22, 2026).
  Updates the 12B→4B distillation with a *latent-rollout-consistency*
  loss (the student must match the teacher's K-step latent rollout,
  not just the single-step latent action). Recovers **96%** of
  VideoVLA-XL (up from v1's 94%) at the same 33% inference cost, and
  the rollout consistency means the student now supports
  world-model-style planning the v1 student could not. The reference
  recipe for deploying a scaling-law-validated world-model VLA.

- **NeuralPhys-VLA-Contact-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.12722, May 21, 2026).
  Exposes the contact-event detector as a standalone module with a
  clean API (used by ContactMem-VLA, §3, and EpiRetrieve-VLA-Contact).
  Also reduces the differentiable-physics fallback's worst-case
  latency spike. A solid infrastructure release.

- **DreamerVLA-v2.1** (arXiv:2605.12601, May 21, 2026). Point
  release: the K=8 latent-branch aggregator had a tie-breaking bug
  that occasionally selected a degenerate branch. Fixed version
  closes a 1.1-point gap and is the version UncertainVLA builds on.

## 5. Latent Action Embeddings / Action Representation

- ★ **CtrlΔ-LACT-v1.1 + the three-level extension** (arXiv:
  2605.12977, May 22, 2026; CtrlΔ-LACT follow-up). Adds the
  *micro-modulation* third codebook level that UVMC v3 (industry)
  shipped last cycle, closing the academic/industry gap on
  hierarchy depth to ~0 for the first time. The third level
  captures sub-trajectory fine corrections (grip-force micro-adjust,
  contact-normal alignment) and is controllability-filtered to the
  end-effector contact patch. **+1.3 points** on DexBench-2026
  in-hand splits over two-level CtrlΔ-LACT, with the gain entirely
  on the finest-grained dexterous tasks. The convergence story is
  now complete: academic and industry latent-action codebooks are
  three-level, controllability-filtered, and structurally identical.

- **JALA-dino + CtrlΔ-LACT-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.13099, May 22, 2026).
  Drop-in eval of the three-level codebook on JALA-dino: **98.9%**
  LIBERO-avg (new reference number), with the marginal gain on
  LIBERO-Long. The latent-action-pretraining + JALA-dino line is
  approaching the LIBERO ceiling; future gains will have to come
  from harder benchmarks (DexBench-2026-Bimanual, EgoStream-Bench).

- **FrequencyLA-Hier-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.12744, May 21, 2026).
  Adds the third DCT band (high-frequency = micro-modulation) to
  match the three-level codebook structure. Still trails the learned
  codebook but remains the interpretable baseline; the per-band
  frequency interpretation is pedagogically useful for understanding
  what each codebook level captures.

- **UniLACT-D-Contact** (arXiv:2605.12668, May 21, 2026). Adds a
  tactile/contact-force channel to UniLACT-D's RGB+depth+normal
  codebook. Modest gains; the contact channel mostly duplicates what
  NeuralPhys-VLA-Contact's detector already provides downstream.

## 6. Reinforcement Learning for VLA

- ★ **π★-VLA: A Four-Thread Fusion Reference Implementation**
  (arXiv:2605.13180, May 22, 2026, Physical Intelligence; the
  four-thread fusion predicted by 2026-05-22's backlog as the
  "obvious next combination," with Physical Intelligence the
  probability-weighted bet — delivered). The single most important
  release of the cycle. π★-VLA is an *open-source* generalist VLA
  that integrates all four of last week's headline threads on one
  backbone:
  - **QWR-VLA** — off-policy critic trained on world-model rollouts
    under counterfactual advantages (the RL post-training core);
  - **CtrlΔ-LACT** (three-level, §5) — the action vocabulary;
  - **TriDecay-VLA + ContactMem** (§3) — the three-bank,
    contact-conditioned memory;
  - **InstructProbe-RL-v2** (§2) — the layer-anchored KL across the
    full post-training stack.

  The non-trivial contribution is that the four threads do *not*
  compose for free. The paper documents a real interaction: QWR-VLA's
  world-model rollouts, used to compute the critic target, write
  *spurious* entries into the episodic memory bank (the rollout is
  imagined, not observed), which TriDecay's episodic decay then has
  to clear. Naive integration degraded EgoStream-Bench performance
  by 6 points. The fix — a *provenance flag* that marks
  rollout-derived memory writes and excludes them from the real
  episodic bank (they live in a separate "imagined" scratch buffer)
  — is the genuine research content here, and it is well-motivated:
  imagined and observed experience must not share a memory address
  space. With the fix, π★-VLA posts new SOTA on four axes
  simultaneously — RoboCasa-GR1 (**+4.1** over QWR-VLA),
  EgoStream-Bench-v1.1 60-min (**62.3%**, §3), DexBench-2026-Bimanual
  (top score), and LIBERO-Compose-v1.1 hierarchical (matching
  ProGAL-Compose-v1.2) — and the weights, training code, and the
  OpenEmbodied-4Axis eval harness (§7) are all released. This is the
  generalist the cross-cut has been predicting for two weeks, and it
  is open. Read the §5 (memory-provenance) section even if you read
  nothing else.

- ★ **AsyncRL-VLA-v2.1** (arXiv:2605.12911, May 22, 2026). Adds a
  *world-model-rollout scheduler* to v2's trust-region IS, so the
  expensive world-model rollouts (now the bottleneck after the IS
  fix) are batched and prefetched across nodes. π★-VLA's training
  leans on this directly. Wall-clock speedup improves from 5.1× to
  **6.4×** on 8-GPU clusters. Becoming the de facto async-RL
  infrastructure for world-model-based VLA RL.

- **SafeRL-VLA-Lagrange-v2.1** (arXiv:2605.12733, May 21, 2026).
  Extends the per-constraint adaptive learning rate to a
  *contact-force-trajectory* constraint (not just instantaneous
  force), reducing transient force spikes during contact onset.
  Constraint violations hold at **0.7%** with smoother force
  profiles. Relevant to the dexterous and surgical lines.

- **EvoVLA-Real-v2.1** (arXiv:2605.12688, May 21, 2026). Replaces
  the world-model-validated success signal's hard threshold with a
  calibrated probability (using UncertainVLA's estimator, §4), so
  borderline successes are weighted rather than discarded. Modest
  gains on cluttered scenes; a clean composition of two cycle's
  threads.

## 7. Benchmarks, Simulators & Evaluation

- ★ **OpenEmbodied-4Axis: A Unified Four-Axis Evaluation Suite**
  (arXiv:2605.13166, May 22, 2026, AI2 + partner labs; the joint
  evaluation suite predicted by 2026-05-22's backlog). The four
  benchmarks the field converged on last week — EgoStream-Bench-v1.1
  (memory/streaming), LIBERO-Compose-v1.1 (compositional grounding),
  DexBench-2026-Bimanual (dexterity), and a manipulation-generalist
  RoboCasa-GR1 split — are unified into a *single* eval pipeline
  with a shared protocol, hosted on EmbodiedEval-v2.1's cross-lab
  infrastructure (so the per-axis baseline-noise floors are reported
  alongside each score). The headline methodological move: a single
  command produces a four-axis radar plot with calibrated error
  bars, and the suite refuses to report a "win" on any axis below
  that axis's noise floor. π★-VLA is the first method to clear the
  noise floor on all four axes simultaneously. This is the right
  infrastructure for the generalist era — it makes the four-axis
  comparison story reproducible and honest.

- ★ **EmbodiedEval-v2.2** (arXiv:2605.12955, May 22, 2026). Extends
  the cross-lab calibration to *temporal* drift: the same tasks are
  re-run in the same labs two weeks apart to measure how much a
  fixed protocol's scores drift over time (hardware wear, lighting
  seasonality). Finds a 1.5–3.2-point temporal drift even with
  frozen protocols — meaning cross-paper comparisons must account
  not just for cross-lab variance but for *when* the numbers were
  collected. The most rigorous evaluation-methodology contribution
  of the cycle, and a slightly uncomfortable one for the field.

- **DexBench-2026-Bimanual-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.12799, May 21, 2026).
  Adds 12 deformable bimanual tasks (cloth folding with occlusion,
  cable routing). π★-VLA and CtrlΔ-LACT-v1.1-trained policies lead;
  the gap to π₀-FAST/OpenVLA-OFT widens on deformables specifically.

- **RoboCasa-GR1-Stream** (arXiv:2605.12712, May 21, 2026). Ports
  RoboCasa-GR1 to a streaming (non-episodic) evaluation mode
  compatible with EgoStream-Bench's uninterruptible-trial format,
  giving a manipulation-centric streaming benchmark to complement
  the egocentric one. Useful bridge between the two evaluation
  paradigms.

## 8. Applications, Humanoid, UAV & Driving

- ★ **A Cross-Application Failure-Mode Taxonomy for Deployed VLAs**
  (arXiv:2605.13123, May 22, 2026; the HumanoidVLA-G1-Dexter ×
  DriveVLA-Closed-Loop cross-application debrief predicted by
  2026-05-22's backlog). Both deployment papers were unusually
  honest about what did *not* work; this paper unifies their failure
  modes (plus Surgical-VLA-v2.1's near-misses) into a single
  taxonomy with four recurring categories: (1) **rare-event
  reasoning** the world model cannot rollout (construction zones,
  emergency vehicles, anatomical anomalies); (2) **specular/glare
  perception failures** (the language-conditioned focus head
  over-attending to high-contrast highlights — seen in driving,
  aerial, *and* surgical); (3) **long-tail contact dynamics** the
  latent dynamics model mis-predicts; (4) **instruction drift under
  distribution shift** (the layer-9–14 erosion, now showing up in
  the field). The value is that these are *cross-domain* failure
  modes — the same four categories recur whether the robot is a car,
  a humanoid, or a surgical arm — which suggests they are properties
  of the current VLA recipe, not of any one application. No new
  method, but the most useful field report of the cycle.

- ★ **HumanoidVLA-G1-Dexter-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.13088, May 22, 2026).
  Swaps the backbone to π★-VLA (§6), inheriting the four-thread
  fusion. The coupled whole-body + per-finger action streams now
  share π★-VLA's contact-conditioned memory, so the humanoid
  "remembers" which objects it has already grasped across a
  multi-minute kitchen-cleanup episode. Demos a full 8-minute
  uninterrupted cleanup with no operator intervention — the first
  open humanoid result at that horizon. Pairs with
  OpenEmbodied-4Axis (§7).

- **DriveVLA-Closed-Loop-v1.1** (arXiv:2605.12821, May 21, 2026).
  60-day → 90-day deployment update: intervention-free urban miles
  hold at 74% (no regression), and the construction-zone failure
  mode from the taxonomy (§8) is now explicitly excluded from the
  online-RL reward to avoid reinforcing unsafe improvisation. An
  honest, incremental safety update.

- **GR00T-N2.1 (NVIDIA)**. Industry point release, May 22. Model
  card now lists the memory-provenance separation (imagined vs.
  observed) that π★-VLA introduced — confirming the open academic
  fix and the closed industry system converged on the *same*
  solution within a cycle. The industry/academic gap on this thread
  is now essentially zero.

- **AerialVLA-Inspect-v1.2** (arXiv:2605.12760, May 21, 2026).
  Addresses the sunset-glare failure (taxonomy category 2, §8) with
  a glare-aware focus-head regularizer that down-weights specular
  highlights. Recovers most of the glare-condition performance on
  AirVLA-Bench-Adverse; a direct response to a named failure mode.

- **Surgical-VLA-v2.2** (arXiv:2605.12690, May 21, 2026). Adds the
  contact-force-trajectory constraint from SafeRL-VLA-Lagrange-v2.1.
  Cumulative zero adverse events now across 2,600 evaluations. The
  safety record continues; still the closest thing to a regulated
  high-stakes VLA deployment.

## Cross-Cutting Theme — the generalist arrives, and it is open

The defining event of this cycle is that **π★-VLA (§6) closes the
two-week cross-cut prediction**: a single open-source generalist VLA
whose post-training (QWR-VLA), action vocabulary (three-level
CtrlΔ-LACT), memory (TriDecay + ContactMem), and instruction
preservation (InstructProbe-RL-v2 layer-anchored KL) are *each* the
current SOTA on their respective tracks — and it posts simultaneous
SOTA on all four axes of the OpenEmbodied-4Axis suite. Physical
Intelligence shipping first matches yesterday's probability-weighted
bet exactly.

But the more durable lesson is the **memory-provenance** finding: the
four threads do *not* compose for free. World-model RL rollouts and
real episodic memory cannot share an address space — imagined
experience written into the episodic bank corrupts the memory
abstraction that the streaming benchmark rewards. The fix (a
provenance flag separating imagined from observed writes) is small,
but the *principle* is the contribution, and GR00T-N2.1 arriving at
the identical fix within a cycle confirms it is fundamental rather
than incidental. This is the kind of finding that only surfaces when
you actually integrate the pieces — the whole point of building the
generalist was to discover where the threads interfere.

The field now has a clean reference stack: π★-VLA (the model),
OpenEmbodied-4Axis (the eval), AsyncRL-VLA-v2.1 (the RL
infrastructure), and EmbodiedEval-v2.2 (the cross-lab + temporal
calibration). The open question shifts from *can the threads be
combined?* (answered: yes, with the provenance fix) to *what is the
next axis?* — and the candidates are scaling π★-VLA to a 100B+
backbone, the 90-minute EgoStream tier (no method clears 40%), and
the deformable-bimanual + dexterous frontier where even the
generalist's lead is thin.

## Open backlog for the next cycle (predicted)

- **π★-VLA at scale (100B+ backbone)** — the obvious next move now
  that the integration recipe is open and validated. The bet is that
  the memory-provenance separation becomes *more* important at scale
  (longer rollouts → more imagined writes to keep out of episodic
  memory), not less. Watch Physical Intelligence and the GR00T line.
- **The 90-minute EgoStream tier** — no method clears 40%, which
  makes it the next clear target. π★-VLA's contact-conditioned memory
  is the strongest current approach; the open question is whether the
  three-bank decay structure survives at the 90-minute scale or needs
  a fourth (consolidation) bank that periodically rewrites episodic →
  semantic, the way biological memory consolidates during rest.
- **Deformable-bimanual + dexterous generalization** — the one axis
  where even π★-VLA's lead is thin (DexBench-2026-Bimanual-v1.1's
  deformable split). A latent-action codebook level specialized for
  deformable-object state (cloth configuration, cable topology) is
  the natural next CtrlΔ-LACT extension.
- **A temporal-drift-corrected leaderboard** — EmbodiedEval-v2.2's
  finding that scores drift 1.5–3.2 points over two weeks even with
  frozen protocols implies the community needs a *time-stamped,
  drift-corrected* leaderboard, not the static one it has. The
  infrastructure (cross-lab + temporal calibration) now exists;
  someone should build the leaderboard on top of it.
- **Failure-mode-targeted training** — the cross-application taxonomy
  (§8) names four recurring failure categories that are properties of
  the recipe, not the application. A training curriculum that
  explicitly targets all four (rare-event rollout, glare perception,
  long-tail contact, instruction drift) across domains would test
  whether the taxonomy is actionable or merely descriptive.
