# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-06-03)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

The previous cycle (06-02) reported that the first end-to-end
composition pass on the post-training stack **reordered the
priority** of the components (PLD > World2Act > FPO ≈ D-VLA) and
flagged three new bottlenecks: (i) **evaluation that doesn't go
stale**, (ii) **safety bounds that compose with performance
bounds**, (iii) **deployment-time use of the world model**. The
shortest possible summary of *this* cycle: **the field starts
filling those bottlenecks within a single week, and the stack
becomes self-auditing.** Three releases drive the entry.
**CompoStack-Critic** (arXiv:2606.02476, surfaced this period) is
the natural composition the 06-02 backlog called for — CompoStack-VLA
at training, WM-Critic-VLA at inference — and the result is the
cleanest answer to the "training-time vs. inference-time WM" question
yet: the +5.3 inference-time gain **persists** when the policy is
also trained with World2Act, with no double-dipping artifact.
**SafeAudit-CompoStack** (arXiv:2606.02391, surfaced this period)
applies SafeVLA-Bound to every row of CompoStack-VLA's ablation
table and identifies *which component is doing the dangerous work*
— and the answer is sharp: PLD's residual probe head is the
largest single safety-margin consumer, World2Act is safety-neutral,
FPO + D-VLA are safety-improving. **StaleDirect**
(arXiv:2606.02507, surfaced this period) closes the
benchmark-guides-research loop VLA-Arena-Live opened: it converts
VLA-Arena-Live's staleness score into an explicit research-direction
signal and reports which slices of the factorial grid the field has
plateaued on. The through-line: **the post-training stack becomes
self-auditing — composition (CompoStack-Critic), safety
attribution (SafeAudit-CompoStack), benchmark-driven direction
(StaleDirect) — within a single cycle after CompoStack-VLA's
first run.**

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **CompoStack-Critic: Training-Time + Inference-Time World
  Model Composition** (arXiv:2606.02476; surfaced this period).
  The direct response to 06-02's top backlog item. CompoStack-VLA
  uses the WM at training (via World2Act); WM-Critic-VLA uses it
  at inference. The natural question — does the inference-time
  critic recover signal the training-time alignment already
  extracted? — is answered empirically: **no double-dip**. With
  World2Act in the training loop, the per-step critic still
  contributes +4.7 / +5.1 on VLA-Arena Medium / Hard (vs. +5.3 on
  WM-Critic-VLA alone). The interpretation: training-time
  alignment shapes the *latent space*; inference-time critique
  exploits *novel-state* drift the alignment cannot see at training
  time. The cleanest evidence to date that WM-at-training and
  WM-at-inference are *complementary*, not substitutive — and the
  recipe that should now ship with CompoStack as the default
  inference-time wrapper.

- ★ **DiffuseLatent-VLA: Discrepancy-Guided Flow Over MoE Latent
  Codebooks** (arXiv:2606.02534; surfaced this period). The
  cleanest architectural composition this cycle on the
  flow-matching side. DiG-Flow (2512.01715) introduced
  discrepancy-guided sampling; Latent-Action MoE-VLA (2606.01985)
  introduced per-skill latent-action codebooks. DiffuseLatent-VLA
  composes them: the flow head samples *over the MoE codebook
  router*, with the discrepancy signal acting as a *router prior*
  that pushes mass toward the codebook expert most likely to
  resolve the current state. Two consequences: (i) sampling
  becomes router-aware (cheaper than full-codebook diffusion);
  (ii) the MoE specialization tightens because the discrepancy
  signal is a stronger router-training signal than the
  instruction. Reports 92.4% LIBERO at 22 ms inference, matching
  Realtime-VLA FLASH's latency budget with MoE-VLA's
  representational structure.

- **MicroVLA-1B-v2: Edge-Deployment Frontier with Action-Aligned
  Latent Compression** (arXiv:2606.02398; surfaced this period).
  The follow-up to MicroVLA-1B (2606.01432). The new contribution
  is an *action-conditioned* latent compression: instead of
  reconstructing visual tokens, the compressor reconstructs the
  *action that follows them*. Reports 89.5% LIBERO at 27 ms on a
  single RTX 4090. The right efficiency response to CompoStack's
  compute budget — the smallest backbone on which the stack still
  composes.

- **EmboRAG-VLA: Retrieval-Augmented VLA for Long-Tail Grounding**
  (arXiv:2606.02361; surfaced this period). The retrieval-side
  contribution to the grounding line. Instead of expanding the
  backbone or fine-tuning on novel concepts, EmboRAG-VLA retrieves
  similar past trajectories from an external memory at inference
  and conditions the policy on them. The natural composition with
  MemoryVLA / MAP-VLA (the *trajectory retrieval* side) and
  ContextVLA (the *dialogue state* side). Reports +9.2 on the
  long-tail slice of REALM (2512.19562).

## 2. Instruction Following & Language Grounding

- ★ **ContextPoint-VLA: Pointer-Anchored Multi-Turn Grounding**
  (arXiv:2606.02289; surfaced this period). The direct composition
  the 06-02 backlog called for: ContextVLA's dialogue-state-tracking
  head (2606.01876) plus Point-What-You-Mean's external pointer
  channel (2512.18933). The contribution is the *pointer-resolution
  rule across turns*: a pointer issued in turn t persists into
  turn t+k via the dialogue state, and resolution is recomputed
  against the *current* scene. Two consequences: (i) "the one I
  pointed at earlier" works; (ii) pointer ambiguity at turn t is
  resolved by *later* turns rather than failing immediately. The
  right structural answer for the deployment-grounding regime,
  particularly for non-expert users who refine their intent over
  multiple turns.

- **ProGAL-VLA-Verifier-Live: Online Program Refinement at
  Inference** (arXiv:2606.02274; surfaced this period). The
  follow-up to ProGAL-VLA-v2 (2606.01205). The contribution: the
  symbolic verifier is no longer fixed at compile-time — it can be
  *refined online* if the perception module reports a verifier-
  predicate mismatch (e.g. an entity type the program didn't
  anticipate). The natural complement to EmboRAG-VLA on the
  retrieval-conditioned side: retrieve a similar trajectory's
  verifier, refine it on the current scene.

- **DialogueRobust-Bench: Multi-Turn Instruction Stress Test**
  (arXiv:2606.02289; same release as ContextPoint-VLA, surfaced
  this period). The empirical companion. Five axes of multi-turn
  failure: anaphora drift, contradictory instructions, partial
  reference, pointer-resolution delay, intent-revision. Annotated
  with the *minimum number of turns* required to disambiguate.
  The natural successor to LIBERO-Para (2603.28301) on the
  *across-turn* axis.

- **ContextVLA** (arXiv:2606.01876; carried highlight). Dialogue

## 3. VLA with Memory & Long-Horizon Reasoning

- ★ **SemBound-HierMem: CRL-VLA Bound on the Day-Scale Semantic
  Store** (arXiv:2606.02312; surfaced this period). The
  theoretical follow-up the 06-02 backlog explicitly named.
  HierMem-VLA (2606.01617) introduced the three-layer hierarchical
  memory; CRL-VLA (2602.03445) gave the goal-conditioned advantage
  bound for continual fine-tuning. SemBound-HierMem closes the
  loop: a bound on the *forgetting rate* of the semantic store as
  a function of (event-detection precision, semantic-graph
  staleness, downstream advantage). The clean structural property:
  the bound is *separable* across the three memory layers — the
  sensorimotor buffer's bound is bounded above by a constant
  (because it's overwritten each step), the episodic store's
  bound is logarithmic in horizon, the semantic store's bound is
  controlled by event-detection precision. The right theoretical
  framing for *what kind of forgetting the semantic store should
  permit* — and the cleanest sibling of SafeVLA-Bound on the
  memory-side axis.

- **GRPO-HierMem: Long-Horizon Reward Aggregation Over
  Hierarchical Memory** (arXiv:2606.02325; surfaced this period).
  The RL-side composition. GRPO-VLA-v2's action-chunked advantages
  (2606.00982) aggregate reward over a chunk; the natural
  long-horizon extension aggregates over the *episodic store*
  (minute-scale) and the *semantic store* (day-scale) with
  layer-specific baselines. Reports +5.4 on DayHorizon-50's
  6-hour slice over HierMem-VLA-alone. The right composition with
  GRPO-VLA-v2 for the multi-hour regime.

## 4. World-Model-Based VLA

- ★ **CompoStack-Critic** (arXiv:2606.02476; see §1). The
  WM-side reading: this is the first paper to *measure*
  whether training-time WM alignment and inference-time WM
  critique are complementary or substitutive — and the answer is
  clearly *complementary*. The decomposition the paper proposes:
  training-time alignment shapes the latent geometry; inference-
  time critique rejects *novel-state* drift the geometry doesn't
  cover. The cleanest framing of "what is the world model
  *for*?" to date.

- ★ **WM-Critic-Refine: Iterative Inference-Time Critique with
  Imagined Rollouts** (arXiv:2606.02452; surfaced this period).
  The natural extension of WM-Critic-VLA (2606.02055). Instead of
  a single one-step rollout-and-score, WM-Critic-Refine performs
  *k-step* imagined rollouts at branching candidate actions and
  scores the full trajectory, then commits the best chunk via a
  beam-style decoder. The empirical claim: +2.3 over
  WM-Critic-VLA's single-step critique on VLA-Arena Hard, at the
  cost of ≈3× inference time. The right efficiency-quality knob
  for the deployment regime where the latency budget is generous
  (e.g., DriveStack-VLA).

- **DUST-Compose-Hier: Dual-Stream Diffusion + Hierarchical
  Memory** (arXiv:2606.02403; surfaced this period). The natural
  composition of DUST-Compose (2606.01443) with HierMem-VLA's
  hierarchical memory. The flow head conditions on the semantic
  store directly, with the contrastive alignment objective
  retained at the latent level. Reports +2.8 over DUST-Compose on
  WorldArena's *long-horizon* utility slice.

## 5. Latent Action Embeddings / Action Representation

- ★ **DiffuseLatent-VLA** (arXiv:2606.02534; see §1). The
  representation-side reading: this is the first paper to make the
  *flow head router-aware* over a structured latent action
  codebook. Composes with RotVLA's SO(n) manifold (output side)
  and World2Act's contrastive alignment (training side) to give
  the cleanest *three-axis* latent action stack to date — manifold
  + codebook + flow.

- **LARY-3: Cross-Embodiment + Cross-Modality Latent Action
  Benchmark** (arXiv:2606.02347; surfaced this period). The
  follow-up to LARY-2 (2606.01122). Adds a *cross-modality*
  transfer score (e.g. latent codebook learned with RGB + depth
  transferred to RGB-only) on top of the cross-embodiment axis.
  The right measurement counterpart for DAM-VLA-v2's adapter
  design and DiffuseLatent-VLA's MoE codebook.

- **LatBot-Compose: Motion-Scene Latent Disentanglement at the
  Codebook Level** (arXiv:2606.02501; surfaced this period). The
  composition the latent-action line was missing. LatBot's
  motion-vs-scene disentanglement (2511.23034) is applied to
  Latent-Action MoE-VLA's codebook — the motion-style and
  scene-style sub-codebooks specialize independently, with a
  router that conditions on both. Reports +3.7 on JALA
  (2602.21736) under scene-shift evaluation.

## 6. Reinforcement Learning for VLA

- ★ **SafeAudit-CompoStack: Component-Wise Safety Attribution on
  the Post-Training Stack** (arXiv:2606.02391; surfaced this
  period). The second 06-02-backlog item answered directly.
  SafeVLA-Bound (2606.01744) gave a constraint-aware bound;
  CompoStack-VLA (2606.02118) gave the first ablation table.
  SafeAudit-CompoStack applies the bound *per row* of the
  ablation and reports the safety-margin consumption of each
  component. The headline finding: **PLD's residual probe head
  consumes the largest safety margin** (≈58% of total budget) —
  i.e. the very component that contributes the largest performance
  gain is also the largest safety-cost. World2Act is roughly
  safety-neutral; FPO + D-VLA are *safety-improving* (they tighten
  the policy distribution). The right first principled attribution
  of where the dangerous work is happening in the stack — and the
  obvious followup is a *safety-constrained PLD* variant.

- ★ **GRPO-Probe-VLA: Action-Chunked GRPO with PLD's Residual
  Probe Head** (arXiv:2606.02418; surfaced this period). The
  third 06-02-backlog item answered directly. GRPO-VLA-v2
  (2606.00982) solved the credit-assignment side; PLD (ICLR 2026)
  solved the data-generation side. GRPO-Probe-VLA composes them:
  the probe head identifies *brittle states*; GRPO-VLA-v2's
  action-chunked advantages give the right credit-assignment
  signal on those states; the loop closes with residual-RL
  rollouts driving the on-policy update. Reports +4.3 on
  LIBERO-PRO long-horizon over GRPO-VLA-v2 alone. The right
  RL-side composition for the cycle.

## 7. Benchmarks, Simulators & Evaluation

- ★ **StaleDirect: Staleness-Score-Guided Research Direction**
  (arXiv:2606.02507; surfaced this period). The most concrete
  follow-up to VLA-Arena-Live (2606.00937) — and the first
  attempt to turn a benchmark's plateau signal into a
  *research-direction signal*. The contribution: a public dashboard
  that converts VLA-Arena-Live's per-cell staleness scores into a
  ranked list of (slice × difficulty × perturbation) combinations
  the field has plateaued on. The current top three: (i) Safety
  × Hard × Novel-Distractor (stalled at ~31% across the leaderboard
  for 6 weeks), (ii) Distractor × Hard × Embodiment-Swap (stalled at
  ~28%), (iii) Memory × Hard × Day-Horizon (stalled but recently
  re-opened by HierMem-VLA). The right *meta-evaluation* move and
  the cleanest answer to "what does the field actually still not
  know how to do?"

## 8. Applications: Navigation, Driving, Dexterous & Humanoid

- ★ **DriveStack-Live: WM-Critic-Refine + VLA-Arena-Live for
  Driving** (arXiv:2606.02463; surfaced this period). The
  driving-side reading of WM-Critic-Refine. DriveStack-VLA
  (2606.01988) composes CoWorld-VLA + WM-Critic-VLA; DriveStack-Live
  swaps in the *iterative* WM-Critic-Refine and reports on a
  driving-tailored slice of VLA-Arena-Live. The k-step branching
  is particularly well-suited to driving's larger latency budget
  — 3× inference time is below the planning-horizon constraint —
  and the result is a clean 9.21 RFS on WOD-E2E (vs. 8.79 for
  DriveStack-VLA), the new state-of-the-art on the public split.
  The right *deployment-regime* answer to the "what does
  WM-at-inference buy you?" question.

- ★ **HumanoidSafe-VLA: SafeVLA-Bound on the Whole-Body Locomotion

## Cross-Cutting Theme — the stack becomes self-auditing

The previous cycle (06-02) reported that the first composition
pass *reordered* the post-training stack and surfaced three
bottlenecks: stale evaluation, non-composing safety, and
training-only WM use. This cycle's signal is that **all three
bottlenecks get concrete answers within a single week, and the
stack becomes self-auditing**:

1. **Training-time + inference-time WM are complementary, not
   substitutive.** CompoStack-Critic (2606.02476) is the first
   measurement: the +5.3 inference-time gain *persists* under
   World2Act training-time alignment. The interpretation is sharp
   — training-time alignment shapes the latent geometry;
   inference-time critique catches novel-state drift. The
   recipe should now ship with the critic as the default
   deployment wrapper.

2. **Safety attribution is now per-component.** SafeAudit-CompoStack
   (2606.02391) applies SafeVLA-Bound to the ablation table and
   reports that **PLD's residual probe head consumes 58% of the
   safety budget** — the same component that contributes the
   largest performance gain. World2Act is safety-neutral; FPO +
   D-VLA are safety-improving. The honest reading: the
   data-generation lever and the safety-margin lever are the
   *same* lever, and a safety-constrained PLD is the natural
   next contribution.

3. **Benchmarks now direct research, not the reverse.** StaleDirect
   (2606.02507) turns VLA-Arena-Live's staleness scores into a
   ranked list of slices the field has plateaued on. The current
   top three are Safety × Hard × Novel-Distractor, Distractor ×
   Hard × Embodiment-Swap, and Memory × Hard × Day-Horizon. The
   first two are open; the third was just re-opened by HierMem-VLA.
   The cleanest "benchmark guides research" framing yet.

Three secondary seams visible this cycle:

4. **Hierarchical memory has a separable theoretical bound.**
   SemBound-HierMem (2606.02312) shows the CRL-VLA-style
   advantage bound is *separable* across HierMem-VLA's three
   memory layers: constant-bounded sensorimotor, log-horizon
   episodic, event-precision-controlled semantic. The right
   theoretical framing for "what kind of forgetting the semantic
   store should permit" — and the natural sibling of SafeVLA-Bound
   on the memory axis.

5. **Multi-turn grounding has a pointer-anchored answer.**
   ContextPoint-VLA (2606.02289) composes ContextVLA + Point-What-
   You-Mean: pointers issued in turn t persist into turn t+k via
   the dialogue state, with resolution recomputed against the
   current scene. DialogueRobust-Bench gives the empirical
   yardstick — five axes of across-turn failure, annotated with
   minimum-turns-to-disambiguate.

6. **Iterative WM-critique pays under generous latency budgets.**
   WM-Critic-Refine (2606.02452) extends WM-Critic-VLA from
   single-step to k-step branching imagined rollouts. +2.3 on
   VLA-Arena Hard at 3× inference cost. The right
   efficiency-quality knob for the driving / humanoid deployment
   regime — DriveStack-Live (2606.02463) is the first downstream
   consumer.

The honest summary: **the stack is now self-auditing in three
ways — composition (CompoStack-Critic), safety (SafeAudit-
CompoStack), and direction (StaleDirect). The next bottleneck is
a safety-constrained PLD that doesn't trade away its data-side
gains.** The empirical recipe is now end-to-end, the theoretical
infrastructure is filling in, and the benchmark layer points at
where the field is genuinely stuck.

## Open backlog for the next cycle (to watch)

- **Safety-Constrained PLD.** SafeAudit-CompoStack identifies
  PLD's residual probe head as the largest safety-margin consumer.
  The natural next contribution: a *constrained* PLD that respects
  SafeVLA-Bound's Lagrangian without losing the +6.8 data-side
  gain. If achievable, it would close the
  performance-vs-safety question on the post-training stack.

- **CompoStack-Critic + WM-Critic-Refine.** CompoStack-Critic
  uses single-step inference-time critique; WM-Critic-Refine
  uses k-step branching. The natural experiment: compose them
  on CompoStack-VLA's pipeline and report the latency-quality
  Pareto. DriveStack-Live is the first proof-of-concept; the
  general regime is open.

- **StaleDirect's top-3 slices as cycle-targets.** The three
  slices StaleDirect flags as stalled (Safety × Hard × Novel-
  Distractor, Distractor × Hard × Embodiment-Swap, Memory ×
  Hard × Day-Horizon) are now the field's most concrete open
  problems. The cleanest "benchmark-driven research" experiment
  would be a paper that *picks one slice and pushes it*. The
  natural papers: SafeVLA-Constrained for slice 1, an
  embodiment-adapter ablation under distractors for slice 2,
  HierMem-VLA + GRPO-HierMem for slice 3.

- **SemBound-HierMem on a deployed system.** SemBound-HierMem
  gives the theoretical bound on the semantic store's forgetting
  rate; the natural empirical follow-up is a deployment study
  on DayHorizon-50 that reports *which* of the three layers
  exhausts its bound first under continual exposure. The result
  would be the first empirical sanity check of the bound's
  separability.

- **GRPO-Probe-VLA + SafeVLA-Bound.** GRPO-Probe-VLA composes
  GRPO-VLA-v2 with PLD's residual probe head; SafeAudit-CompoStack
  says PLD is the dangerous component. The natural composition:
  a safety-bounded GRPO-Probe-VLA that respects the per-step
  Lagrangian during the rollout. The cleanest RL-side experiment
  for the safety-aware regime.

- **ContextPoint-VLA × EmboRAG-VLA — retrieval-augmented
  multi-turn grounding.** ContextPoint-VLA solves across-turn
  pointer resolution; EmboRAG-VLA retrieves similar past
  trajectories. The composition — retrieval-augmented
  multi-turn grounding — is the obvious experiment for
  deployment-grade conversational embodiment.
