A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction. Today is the
**Friday after 05-07's empirical Thursday**, and the arXiv intake
has the *closing-of-loops* character one would expect: the count
is roughly flat, but three of the day's primaries each close a
loop 05-07 named explicitly. **PrimVoc-VLA v2** is the
substrate-aware redesign of the K = 384 codebook that 05-07's
PrimCond-Suite cross-cut leaderboard predicted, with K lifted to
512 and the per-primitive frequency exponent α retuned to 1.32 to
compensate the static-scene × identity-reference trade-off the
suite surfaced as its first cross-axis cost. **CtxJudge-VLA** is
the *non-decomposable preference signal* 05-07 forecast as the
next meta-judge gain after the four-judge committee saturated the
decision-theoretic basis at rank 4: a context-conditional utility
that does not factor over (state, action, primitive) and lifts the
stable-iteration ceiling from ≥24 to ≥27 — a 1.13× ratio that
confirms the deceleration but extends the ladder one more layer.
**SubScale-Compensate** is the architectural-compensation paper
the smaller-substrate community has needed since SafetyVLA-Audit

four-judge configuration loses structural diversity. The day reads

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **PrimVoc-VLA v2: A Substrate-Aware Primitive Vocabulary with
  Joint K, α, and Per-Module Specialization Design**
  (arXiv:2605.03127, May 8, 2026, Stanford + Westlake + DeepMind +
  ETH Zürich + Toyota Research Institute). The redesigned primitive
  vocabulary 05-07 explicitly named, on schedule. The original
  PrimVoc-VLA (05-03 §5) was a discrete-codebook artefact whose
  K = 384 / α ≈ 1.4 were chosen for codebook-utilization reasons;
  v2 redesigns these jointly with PrimCond-Suite's per-module
  specialization profile (05-07 §1) and three concrete diagnostic
  signals: the static-scene × identity-reference trade-off cell
  (DreamConsistency-Bench v1.3, 05-07 §4), the *spatial × align ×
  identity-reference* failure cell (ProcessBench-Embodied v0.7,
  05-07 §7), and the J₃ ↔ J₄ closeness bound at 0.71
  (FourJudge-VLA, 05-07 §6). Three load-bearing design moves: (i)
  K is lifted to 512 with the additional 128 slots concentrated in
  the primitive-pair *carry-and-place*, *step-and-grab*, and
  *grasp-rotate-insert* compositions that the suite's
  primitive-coverage matrix flagged as field-wide deep-but-narrow
  cells, with the new slots' frequency profile derived from
  PrimMix-Dynamics's gradient-balance constraint (05-07 §5); (ii)
  α is retuned from 1.40 to 1.32 to flatten the head and broaden
  the tail in the narrow direction the static-scene cell needs,
  with the gradient-balance ratio held at 3.4 ± 0.4 by
  proportionally rescaling the head/tail loss weights — i.e., the
  3.5-fold balance condition is preserved by construction; (iii) a
  per-module specialization-profile descriptor is *baked into* the
  primitive descriptor's 64-dimensional payload, with 16 dimensions
  reserved for the consumer-module identity (grounding / memory /
  world-model / judge / bandwidth / driving / humanoid /
  reserved). Three empirical findings make this the substrate
  paper's *next-layer*: (a) the static-scene × identity-reference
  trade-off cell (05-07 §4) reverses sign — suite-aware policies
  now *gain* 0.4 absolute on it instead of losing 0.3, the only
  cell that flipped under v2; (b) the *spatial × align ×
  identity-reference* open cell on ProcessBench-Embodied v0.7
  shrinks from 9% of residual mass to 4%, with the residual
  9% → 4% change attributable in closed form to the new K = 512
  *align*-pair slots; (c) PrimMix-Theory's (1 − c)^0.7 scaling law
  is preserved exactly on v2's α = 1.32 codebook, with the new
  curve constant 0.71 (vs. 0.70 on v1) — i.e., the redesign
  preserves the data-side theory's structural form to within ±1%.
  Reads as the *substrate-redesign paper* of the cycle, and the
  first piece of vocabulary engineering that consumes the suite's
  cross-axis trade-off table as a *design constraint* rather than a
  diagnostic.

- **CrossCut-VLA v1.2 (PrimVoc-v2 integration)**
  (arXiv:2605.01612v3, May 8, 2026). The flagship cross-cut
  architecture re-trains its three coupled blocks on PrimVoc-VLA
  v2's K = 512 codebook. The interesting empirical detail: the
  +0.3 residual cross-cut coupling that v1.1 (05-07 §1) reported
  as the surviving non-decomposable gain over the
  primitive-descriptor channel rises to +0.5 under v2 — i.e., the
  broader α tail and the new primitive-pair slots open additional
  decomposable head-room that v1.1 was visibly compressing. The
  total cross-cut gain is now +1.6 absolute, decomposed as +1.1
  (primitive-descriptor sharing) + +0.5 (residual cross-cut
  coupling), with the residual coupling now interpretable as
  primitive-pair joint specialization that the K = 384 codebook
  lacked the resolution to express.

- **DualBand-VLA v2.3 (PrimVoc-v2 bandwidth controller)**
  (arXiv:2605.00271v6, May 8, 2026). The bandwidth controller now
  exploits PrimVoc-v2's 16-dimensional consumer-module identity
  channel as a *bandwidth-class* feature, which means the
  *insert*-pair (3× tail-latency tolerance vs. *grasp*-pair, see
  v2.2 (05-07 §1)) and the new *carry-and-place*-pair (1.4× tail
  tolerance) are routed through different bandwidth profiles by
  default. The LIBERO-Plus tail-latency slice gains a further
  4.1% relative on top of v2.2's 8.4%, with no regression on the
  corridor-speed track.

## 2. Instruction Following & Language Grounding

- **BindFlow-VLA v1.3 (PrimVoc-v2 binding head)**
  (arXiv:2605.01378v4, May 8, 2026). The binding head adopts
  PrimVoc-v2's 64-dimensional descriptor with the new
  primitive-pair slots, and the per-primitive contact prior is
  re-fit on the K = 512 codebook. Empirical detail: the
  primitive-pair binding cells (05-07 §2) gain a further +0.4
  absolute when the binders address the new *carry-and-place* and
  *grasp-rotate-insert* slots that v1 collapsed onto neighboring
  cells. The product-form posterior is now *exact* on the
  primitive-triple cells GroundFlow-Bayes v2.4 introduced
  (05-07 §2) — i.e., the v2 codebook resolves the triples that v1
  could only approximate.

- **NegBind-VLA v1.1 (suite-registered with v2 alignment)**
  (arXiv:2605.02103v2, May 8, 2026). Same-week v1.1 of yesterday's
  primitive-invariant negation head retunes its primitive-marginalized
  path on the K = 512 codebook; the *negation × hand-off ×
  spatial-relation* cell residual mass drops further from 38% to
  31%. The headline empirical observation is that the primitive-
  invariance of negation that v1 exploited (05-07 §2) holds under
  v2 unchanged — i.e., the asymmetry between binding (conditional)
  and negation (marginal) is robust to the substrate redesign,
  which is a useful invariance result.

- **SpatialAlign-VLA: Closing the Spatial × Align ×
  Identity-Reference Cell** (arXiv:2605.03245, May 8, 2026, AI2 +
  CMU + Princeton + Tsinghua). Picks up the last open cross-tab
  cell ProcessBench-Embodied v0.7 (05-07 §7) and v2 partially
  reduced (§1). The construction routes the *align* primitive's
  spatial-reference parsing through a separate *identity-channel*
  head that is conditioned on the per-frame identity-reference
  vocabulary rather than the trajectory-level spatial primitive,
  with explicit composition over the *align × identity-reference*
  cross-axis. The cell that v2 reduced from 9% to 4% drops to
  1.6% under the joint combination of v2 and SpatialAlign-VLA
  (each contributes ~half the gain). With this paper the
  ProcessBench-Embodied cross-tab open-cell mass is below 2% of
  the residual — the cleanest closing-out of a benchmark's open
  cells the field has produced this cycle.

- **GroundFlow-Bayes v2.5 (PrimVoc-v2 triple posteriors)**
  (arXiv:2605.00342v7, May 8, 2026). The compositional posterior

## 6. Reinforcement Learning for VLA

- ★ **CtxJudge-VLA: A Context-Conditional Utility Beyond the
  Decomposable Basis** (arXiv:2605.03388, May 8, 2026, DeepMind +
  Stanford + Tsinghua + EPFL + Toyota Research Institute). The
  *non-decomposable preference signal* paper 05-07 explicitly
  predicted as the next layer beyond the four-judge committee.
  FourJudge-VLA (05-07 §6) saturated the decision-theoretic
  basis at rank 4 with utilities factoring over (state, action,
  primitive); CtxJudge-VLA introduces a fifth judge (J₅) whose
  utility is an explicit *non-decomposable* function over
  (state, action, primitive, *trajectory-context*), where the
  trajectory-context channel reads the prior 32 steps' latent
  history through a low-rank attention block that is *not*
  factorizable into the canonical (s, a, p) basis. Three
  load-bearing design moves: (i) the trajectory-context channel
  is constructed from the last 32 latent states' Markov-blanket
  closure, which the paper proves is the minimal context that
  cannot be expressed as a low-rank function of (s, a, p) under
  the field's preference-signal decomposition theorem; (ii) the
  five-judge pairwise Jacobian-alignment table is reported as a
  5 × 5 matrix whose worst entry (J₃ ↔ J₄, the two
  implicit-reward heads) remains 0.71 and whose *best* entry
  (J₂ ↔ J₅) is 0.43 — i.e., the new context-conditional judge
  is *more* dissimilar from the generative judge than any prior
  pair, lifting the structural D bound from 0.63 to 0.69; (iii)
  the closed-form analysis decomposes J₅'s contribution into a
  *factorizable* component (which is bounded above by the
  four-judge ceiling) and a *non-decomposable* component (which
  contributes the strict gain), with the non-decomposable
  component accounting for 78% of the marginal gain. Three
  empirical findings make this the meta-judge axis's *fifth-layer*
  paper: (a) CtxJudge-VLA's stable-iteration ceiling rises from
  ≥24 to ≥27, a 1.13× ratio that confirms the deceleration
  05-07 named but extends the ladder one more layer; (b) the
  D bound is tight at 0.69 ± 0.02 across 28 self-play runs,
  with no run dropping below 0.66; (c) the non-decomposable
  component's largest marginal gain is on the
  *primitive-quadruple* preference cells (e.g.,
  *grasp-rotate-insert-release* sequences) at +2.1 absolute,
  which is the largest single-cell jump on this axis since
  HeteroJudge-VLA. Reads as the *fifth-layer* paper of the
  meta-judge axis, with the predicted ceiling extension and the
  predicted shift from a factorable to a non-decomposable
  preference signal.

- **JudgeEnsemble-VLA v1.4 (CtxJudge default, optional)**
  (arXiv:2605.01051v5, May 8, 2026). Same-day v1.4 ships the

  The LIBERO-Long number rises to 99.5 (vs. v1.3's 99.4) at

- **AcceRL v1.6 (CtxJudge dashboards)**

## 7. Benchmarks, Simulators & Evaluation

- **LBM Eval v3.3 — primitive-pair coverage axis**
  (leaderboard report, May 8, 2026). The v3.3 extends the
  coverage-vs-depth scatter (05-07 §7) to *primitive-pair*
  coverage, which on the K = 512 codebook is the more
  diagnostic axis: a policy that covers ≥ 320 primitives but only
  ≤ 80 primitive-pairs is now penalized in the joint ranking. The
  immediate consequence: the suite-aware composite that topped
  v3.2 falls to second, with PrimVoc-v2-aware policies (§1)
  taking the top spot at primitive-pair coverage ≥ 220 and

## 8. Applications, Humanoid & Safety

- ★ **SubScale-Compensate: Architectural Compensation for
  Sub-4B-Parameter Four-Judge Configurations**
  (arXiv:2605.03452, May 8, 2026, EPFL + AI2 + DeepMind +
  Westlake). The architectural-compensation paper the
  smaller-substrate community has needed since SafetyVLA-Audit
  v3.4 (05-07 §8) identified the 4B threshold below which the
  four-judge configuration loses structural diversity by
  iteration 12. The construction analyzes the 3 leaderboard
  policies that drifted past the 0.71 J₃ ↔ J₄ bound and finds a
  shared failure mode: the implicit-reward judges' bilinear and
  non-bilinear utilities collapse onto a low-rank manifold whose
  rank scales with the substrate's effective head dimension.
  The compensation has three parts: (i) the bilinear J₃ is
  re-parametrized as a *blocked-bilinear* utility that explicitly
  excludes the non-bilinear J₄'s most-aligned subspace (which the
  paper computes from a one-time 5-minute calibration run); (ii)
  a *diversity-floor* regularizer adds an explicit Jacobian-
  alignment penalty whenever the J₃ ↔ J₄ pair drifts past 0.65
  (i.e., conservatively below the 0.71 bound); (iii) a *judge
  rotation* protocol rotates which judge head receives the
  current trajectory's most-informative gradient, with the
  rotation schedule derived from the four pairwise alignment
  bounds. Three empirical findings make this load-bearing: (a)
  on a 1.3B-parameter substrate, the compensation holds the
  J₃ ↔ J₄ alignment at 0.68 ± 0.04 across 25 self-play runs (vs.
  the un-compensated 0.78 ± 0.06), with the stable-iteration
  ceiling rising from 14 to 22 — closing 80% of the gap to the
  ≥24 four-judge ceiling that 4B+ substrates achieve; (b) on
  a 600M-parameter substrate, the compensation closes only 50%
  of the gap (ceiling rises from 9 to 16, vs. the 4B-substrate
  ≥24), which suggests there is a *floor* below which
  architectural compensation cannot fully recover the structural
  diversity — this floor sits at roughly 1B parameters; (c) the
  CtxJudge-VLA five-judge configuration (§6) shows the same
  scale-dependent diversity-decay pattern but with the threshold
  shifted to 8B rather than 4B, which the paper conjectures will
  generalize: each new judge-layer raises the minimum
  architectural-compensation threshold by ~2×. A clean,
  prescriptive paper that closes a deployment-blocking gap and
  surfaces a new structural law at the same time.

## Cross-Cutting Observation — the substrate redesign loop closes one cell and opens another

PrimVoc-v2 (§1) ships exactly the redesign 05-07 named:
K = 512, α = 1.32, with a per-module specialization-profile
descriptor baked into the 64-dimensional payload. Three
consequences:

1. The static-scene × identity-reference trade-off
   DreamConsistency-Bench v1.3 surfaced (05-07 §4) reverses sign
   — suite-aware policies now *gain* 0.4 absolute on it under v2
   (§4). The substrate-design loop closes one cross-axis trade-off
   on schedule. But v1.4 also surfaces a new candidate open
   cell (*dynamic-object × multi-instance*) that the v1 codebook
   lacked the resolution to expose — i.e., the substrate-design
   loop is iterative, and each redesign closes prior open cells
   while exposing new ones. The honest read: the v2 redesign is
   a strict improvement on the v1 cells but does not eliminate
   the open-cell phenomenon.

2. The cross-cut coupling decomposition extends. CrossCut-VLA
   v1.2 (§1) reports the residual cross-cut coupling rises from
   +0.3 (v1.1) to +0.5 (v1.2 on v2's K = 512), with the additional
   +0.2 attributable to the new primitive-pair joint slots that
   v1's codebook lacked. The *primitive-pair* specialization is
   the new structural coupling channel — i.e., the primitive
   vocabulary's coupling channel has shifted from
   *single-primitive* (v1) to *primitive-pair* (v2), which the
   field will need to internalize before the next round of
   architectural designs.

3. The data-side theory is now visibly *fragile* on v2.
   DiscreteFlow-VLA-XL v2.6 (§5) reports that the α drift under
   naive single-axis fine-tuning is *worse* on v2 (1.32 → 1.08)
   than on v1 (1.40 → 1.21) — i.e., the broader-tail design is
   more sensitive to gradient imbalance, and the prescriptive
   regularizer (§5) is now load-bearing rather than helpful. The
   smaller-substrate community will need to budget for stricter
   gradient-balance maintenance, with PrimSeed-Data v2 (§5) the
   data-side prescription that holds α = 1.32 ± 0.03 even under
   single-axis fine-tuning.

The natural prediction for the next 7–14 days is a paper that
*adapts* the primitive vocabulary to the deployment substrate —
i.e., a substrate-conditional K(θ) and α(θ) where θ is the
deployment substrate's effective head dimension. The current v2
ships a single K = 512 / α = 1.32 design across all substrates;
the next iteration will likely select K and α per substrate to
hold the gradient-balance ratio at 3.4 across the 1B–32B
parameter range that the deployment community covers.

## Cross-Cutting Observation — the meta-judge ladder extends to layer five with a non-decomposable signal

CtxJudge-VLA (§6) lifts the stable-iteration ceiling from ≥24
to ≥27 with a closed-form D ≥ 0.69 from a five-judge committee
whose fifth judge is *non-decomposable* in the precise sense
05-07 named as the necessary condition for extending the basis
beyond rank 4. Three observations:

1. The doubling-per-layer pattern continues to decelerate:
   ≥9 → ≥13 → ≥18 → ≥24 → ≥27 is now 1.44× → 1.38× → 1.33× →
   1.13×, with the deceleration sharpening at the fifth layer.
   The information-theoretic reading is that each
   non-decomposable extension recovers roughly 50% of what the
   prior decomposable layer recovered — which would predict the
   sixth-layer ceiling at ≥29 if the deceleration holds at
   ~1.07×. The honest read: the meta-judge ladder is now in its
   late phase, and the field should expect at most one or two
   more layers before the structural ceiling stabilizes.

2. The non-decomposable component of J₅ accounts for 78% of the
   marginal gain (§6), with the factorizable component bounded
   above by the four-judge ceiling. This is the cleanest
   evidence to date that the *form* of the preference signal —
   not just the number of judges — is the rate-limiting factor.
   The next layer would need *another* non-decomposable
   extension (e.g., a bi-trajectory comparison signal or a
   counterfactual-conditional utility) rather than another
   judge in the same form class.

3. The scale-dependent diversity-decay threshold rises from 4B
   (four-judge) to 8B (five-judge), exactly the 2× pattern
   SubScale-Compensate (§8) conjectures will generalize. The
   smaller-substrate community will need explicit architectural
   compensation that scales with each new judge layer, and the
   deployment guidance is now to default to four-judge or
   three-judge configurations on sub-8B substrates and reserve
   the five-judge configuration for 8B+ deployments.

The honest read for the next two weeks: the meta-judge ladder
is in its terminal regime, the next gain will require a
non-decomposable extension orthogonal to the trajectory-context
axis, and the smaller-substrate community will need
SubScale-Compensate-style protocols to track each new layer.

## Cross-Cutting Observation — the open-cell taxonomy is now substrate-relative

ProcessBench-Embodied v0.8 (§7) closes three of the five v0.6
cross-tab cells (negation × hand-off × spatial-relation;
attribute × insert × negation; spatial × align ×
identity-reference) to <2% residual mass each, while opening
two new cells (primitive-quadruple × negation, 3.1%; step-and-
grab × dynamic-object, 2.4%) that v0.6 lacked the substrate
resolution to surface. Two observations:

1. The benchmark's open-cell taxonomy is *substrate-relative*:
   each substrate redesign reveals new failure modes that
   prior substrates could not express. This means the
   benchmark literature is now methodologically dependent on
   the substrate it evaluates — a point the field will need
   to address before the next round of cross-benchmark
   comparisons.

2. The closed cells (3) outnumber the newly opened cells (2),
   which means the substrate redesign is net-positive on the
   open-cell taxonomy. But the rate of closure is now
   *bounded* by the rate of substrate redesign, not by the
   rate of single-paper design choices. Future open-cell
   reductions will require either substrate redesigns
   (PrimVoc-v3) or *substrate-orthogonal* design moves
   (SpatialAlign-VLA, NegBind-VLA) — and the latter are
   becoming rarer.
