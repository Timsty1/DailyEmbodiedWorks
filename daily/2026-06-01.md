# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-06-01)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

The previous cycle (05-31) was about **probing the seams of the
consolidated stack** — DIAL (VLM↔action interface), AtomVLA
(post-training objective), VLA-Arena (factorial evaluation),
Embodied4C (cross-embodiment yardstick). The shortest possible
summary of *this* cycle: **the seams identified yesterday already
have concrete answers being submitted today.** Three releases drive
the entry. **World2Act** (arXiv:2603.10422, surfaced this period) is
the direct response to AtomVLA's open question — instead of refining
actions in pixel space, the policy's action features are aligned to
the world model's *video-dynamic latents* via a contrastive loss, so
WM rollout pixel hallucinations don't leak into the policy update.
That's the *right* version of "predictive latent world model as
post-training objective" and it composes cleanly with Qwen-VLA's
generalist backbone and D-VLA's distributed RL substrate. **CRL-VLA**
(arXiv:2602.03445, surfaced this period) is the *theory* the
continual-learning line was missing: a unified performance bound
linking the stability-plasticity trade-off to goal-conditioned
advantage magnitude. The bound is the first one that survives
contact with VLA-scale fine-tuning. **PLD — Probe-Learn-Distill /
Self-Improving VLAs via Residual RL** (ICLR 2026, surfaced this
period) closes the data-generation loop that AtomVLA / World2Act
opened: RL-generated, policy-aligned data surpasses teleoperation-only
demonstrations on the same backbone. The through-line: **post-training
moves from "open problem" to "stack" in a single cycle — objective
(World2Act), theory (CRL-VLA), data generation (PLD).**

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **World2Act: Latent Action Post-Training via Skill-Compositional
  World Models** (arXiv:2603.10422; surfaced this period). The
  cleanest answer to AtomVLA's open question. Existing world-model
  post-training pipelines refine actions in *pixel space*, which
  inherits every hallucination the WM rollout produces; World2Act
  aligns the policy's action features directly against the WM's
  *video-dynamic latents* via a contrastive loss. Two consequences:
  (i) the WM does not need to be a *perfect* generator — it only
  needs to produce a stable latent under the action it predicts; (ii)
  skills become *compositional* at the latent level rather than the
  pixel level. The natural pair with AtomVLA (2603.08519) and the
  obvious downstream consumer for Qwen-VLA (2605.30280): the
  generalist supplies the backbone, AtomVLA supplies the predictive
  latent, World2Act supplies the *alignment loss*. Reads as the
  third leg of the post-training stool.

- ★ **PLD: Probe-Learn-Distill — Self-Improving VLAs via Residual
  Reinforcement Learning** (ICLR 2026, openreview xqRD4LLY5M; surfaced
  this period). The data-generation half of the post-training stack.
  The framing is sharp: existing post-training is *bottlenecked by
  teleoperation*; PLD shows that *RL-generated, policy-aligned*
  trajectories not only match but *surpass* teleoperation-only
  demonstrations on the same backbone. The mechanism is a residual
  RL probe head bolted onto the frozen policy, trained to find the
  states where the policy is *almost* right — then distillation
  feeds those targeted corrections back into the base model. Pairs
  naturally with VLA-Arena's factorial difficulty axes (2512.22539,
  carried highlight) as a curriculum signal — the probe head will
  preferentially find Safety / Distractor / Extrapolation states
  where the policy is brittle.

- **AC²-VLA: Action-Context-Aware Adaptive Computation for VLA**
  (arXiv:2601.19634; surfaced this period). The right efficiency
  contribution at the same altitude as Mean-Flow VLA (2603.01469).
  Different framing: where Mean-Flow distills *flow steps*, AC²-VLA
  *adaptively allocates compute* per action chunk based on the
  estimated novelty of the action context. The natural counterpart
  for production deployments that span easy/hard execution states in
  the same trajectory.

- **DAM-VLA: Dynamic Action Model-Based VLA** (arXiv:2603.00926;
  surfaced this period). The cleaner statement of "VLM reasons,
  diffusion action model executes" — the diffusion head is treated
  as a *dynamic* component conditioned on VLM-derived task structure
  rather than a fixed decoder. Reads as the direct competitor to
  DIAL (2603.29844, 05-31 highlight): DIAL inserts a *latent visual
  foresight* between VLM and head; DAM-VLA inserts *structured task
  conditioning* — the same problem attacked from the conditioning
  side instead of the latent side.

- **PEAfowl: Perception-Enhanced Multi-View VLA for Bimanual
  Manipulation** (arXiv:2601.17885; surfaced this period). The
  multi-view fusion line gets a useful structural answer:
  geometry-guided multi-view fusion + Perceiver-style text-as-query
  readout over *frozen* CLIP features. The "frozen CLIP + Perceiver
  readout" recipe is the right efficiency move for bimanual systems
  that can't afford a per-view backbone — pairs cleanly with
  WholeBodyVLA (ICLR 2026, 2512.11047) as the architecture for the
  perception front-end.

## 2. Instruction Following & Language Grounding

- ★ **ActiveVLA: Injecting Active Perception into VLA for Precise 3D
  Manipulation** (arXiv:2601.08325; surfaced this period). The right
  active-perception complement to AVA-VLA (2511.18960). ActiveVLA's
  contribution is the *3D-precise* version: the policy emits not
  just where to look, but where to *reposition the camera-bearing
  end-effector* to disambiguate occlusions, with the active-perception
  loss aligned to the downstream manipulation objective. The
  cluttered-scene grounding line had two structural answers before
  this cycle — externalize the referent (Point-What-You-Mean,
  2512.18933) or internalize it (DIAL's latent foresight). ActiveVLA
  is the third: *acquire more views*. The right framing when the
  scene is genuinely ambiguous from any single viewpoint.

- ★ **Tactile-VLA: Deeply Fused Tactile-VLA Framework** (under review
  ICLR 2026; surfaced this period). Worth highlighting alongside AT-VLA
  (2605.07308) and HapticVLA (2603.15257) — together they bracket the
  tactile-grounding design space. AT-VLA: tactile at inference. HapticVLA:
  tactile at training, deployed without it. Tactile-VLA: tactile as a
  *first-class modality* fused at every layer. The right answer when
  tactile is genuinely the *contact-rich* signal and the deployment
  target *has* tactile hardware.

- **Point-VLA / Point-What-You-Mean — visually grounded instruction
  policy** (arXiv:2512.18933; carried highlight). Still the cycle's
  cleanest external-grounding contribution.

- **VLA-Forget — linguistic blindness as a grounding failure**
  (arXiv:2604.03956; carried highlight). The diagnostic frame still
  holds; ActiveVLA's third structural answer (acquire more views)
  is the cleanest sibling.

- **LIBERO-Para** (arXiv:2603.28301; carried highlight). Paraphrase
  robustness yardstick.

- **GesVLA** (arXiv:2605.22812; carried highlight). Non-language
  modality grounding.

- **Grounding via Explicit Language-Action Alignment** (arXiv:2604.05614;
  carried). Contrastive trajectory ranking.

- **ProGAL-VLA** (arXiv:2604.09824; carried). 3D entity-centric graph
  + symbolic sub-goal alignment.

- **Clutter-Robust VLAs** (arXiv:2512.22519; carried). Object-centric
  + geometry grounding for distractor-heavy scenes.

## 3. VLA with Memory & Long-Horizon Reasoning

- ★ **Efficient Long-Horizon VLA via Static-Dynamic Disentanglement
  (SD-VLA-2)** (arXiv:2602.03983; surfaced this period, updated). The
  KV-cache-aware long-horizon contribution the line needed. The
  observation is structural: across timesteps in a long-horizon
  task, the *static* tokens (scene layout, task instruction, robot
  morphology) don't move; the *dynamic* tokens (object state,
  end-effector pose) do. SD-VLA-2 keeps a *single* copy of static
  tokens across timesteps and reorders the attention input to put
  static-before-dynamic, enabling end-to-end KV-cache reuse over
  the full episode. The right efficiency answer for long-horizon
  deployment — pairs naturally with MEM (2603.03596) on the memory
  side and HiF-VLA (2512.09928) on the trace side.

- **MemoryVLA — Perceptual-Cognitive Memory Bank** (arXiv:2508.19236;
  carried highlight). Working memory + Perceptual-Cognitive Memory
  Bank with 71.9/72.7/96.5/41.2% success across the LIBERO/CALVIN
  suite.

- **LoHoVLA — Unified Long-Horizon VLA** (arXiv:2506.00411; surfaced
  this period). Joint high-level task planning + low-level motion
  control with linguistic sub-task inference. The natural pair with
  Mind-to-Hand (2512.08580, 05-31 carried) — Mind-to-Hand carries
  *purpose*; LoHoVLA carries *sub-tasks*; together they specify a
  two-level memory channel.

- **EchoVLA** (arXiv:2511.18112; carried highlight). Scene + episodic
  memory for mobile manipulation.

- **MEM — Multi-Scale Embodied Memory** (arXiv:2603.03596; carried
  highlight). Video short-horizon + text long-horizon, ~15-minute
  horizons.

- **MAP-VLA — Memory-Augmented Prompting** (arXiv:2511.09516; carried
  highlight). Trajectory-similarity retrieval, +7.0% sim / +25.0%
  real.

- **HiF-VLA's Hindsight stream** (arXiv:2512.09928; carried).
  Proprioception-trace episodic memory.

- **AtomVLA's predictive latent as memory** (arXiv:2603.08519; carried
  highlight). Predictive latent WM doubles as a learned compressed
  memory of the trajectory distribution.

## 4. World-Model-Based VLA

- ★ **World2Act — Skill-Compositional World Models for Latent Action
  Post-Training** (arXiv:2603.10422; see §1). Read here as the
  world-model contribution: World2Act's alignment loss is the
  cleanest answer to "what does the world model *actually* need to
  predict for post-training to work?" The answer turns out not to be
  "future pixels" but "future video-dynamic latents that the policy
  can be conditioned on contrastively." Pairs with AtomVLA on the
  *substrate* side and with DUST (2510.27607) on the *generator*
  side.

- ★ **World-Env: Leveraging World Model as a Virtual Environment for
  VLA Post-Training** (arXiv:2509.24948; surfaced this period). The
  *RL-environment-as-WM* framing. Where AtomVLA / World2Act use the
  WM as a *representation enricher*, World-Env uses it as a *training
  rollout substrate* — the policy executes in WM-imagined rollouts
  under safety constraints, with real-world execution reserved for
  validation. The composition with D-VLA (2605.13276) is obvious:
  D-VLA gives the distributed substrate; World-Env gives the
  *environment*. The natural complement to the FPO update rule
  (2510.09976).

- **DUST — Dual-Stream Diffusion for WM-Augmented VLA**
  (arXiv:2510.27607; carried highlight). Separate modality streams +
  decoupled flow-matching loss. The right architectural answer to
  modality collapse.

- **DiG-Flow — Discrepancy-Guided Flow Matching** (arXiv:2512.01715;
  carried highlight). Predicted-vs-observed dynamics gap as a
  training signal.

- **WorldArena — Unified Benchmark for Embodied WMs** (arXiv:2602.08971;
  surfaced this period). The *evaluation* counterpart to the WM-VLA
  line. WorldArena evaluates the WM not just on generation quality
  but on *functional utility*: can the model serve as a mental
  simulator for downstream action planning? The right yardstick to
  decouple "good video model" from "good VLA-WM."

- **OneWM-VLA — One Token Per Frame** (arXiv:2605.07931; carried
  highlight). Per-frame visual bandwidth ablation.

- **EA-WM — Event-Aware Generative World Model** (arXiv:2605.06192;
  carried highlight). Structured action-field WM.

- **MoLA — Mixture of Latent Actions** (arXiv:2605.12167; carried
  highlight). Mixture-IDM bridge from imagined futures to actions.

- **CoWorld-VLA** (arXiv:2605.10426; carried highlight). Multi-expert
  latent WM for driving.

- **DIAL's latent visual foresight as a one-step WM** (arXiv:2603.29844;
  carried highlight). The cleanest framing of why dual-system VLAs
  need *some* WM channel even when they don't need multi-step rollouts.

## 6. Reinforcement Learning for VLA

- ★ **CRL-VLA: Continual Vision-Language-Action Learning**
  (arXiv:2602.03445; surfaced this period). The theoretical
  contribution the continual-RL line was missing. CRL-VLA derives a
  *unified performance bound* linking the stability-plasticity
  trade-off directly to *goal-conditioned advantage magnitude* — i.e.
  the bound is in terms of quantities you can actually estimate
  from rollouts. The right answer to "why does continual RL fine-tuning
  work for VLAs but not for general policies?": the bound makes
  explicit that VLAs' goal-conditioning channel is what keeps the
  advantage magnitude bounded across tasks. Pairs naturally with
  LifeLong-RFT (2602.10503, carried) on the empirical side and with
  Simple-Recipe-Works (2603.11653, carried highlight) on the
  practical-claim side — together they specify continual VLA RL
  as a *bounded* problem.

- ★ **PLD — Probe-Learn-Distill / Self-Improving VLAs**
  (ICLR 2026; see §1). The data-generation half of post-training.
  RL-generated, policy-aligned trajectories surpass teleoperation-only
  demonstrations. The residual RL probe head + distillation back into
  the base model is the right framing for the data-scarcity bottleneck.

- ★ **World-Env — WM as a Virtual RL Environment** (arXiv:2509.24948;
  see §4). The "environment" piece of the RL stack. Pairs cleanly
  with D-VLA's distributed substrate and FPO's flow-policy update
  rule.

- **VLA-PPO-Chunked — Action-Chunked PPO + Self-BC** (arXiv:2509.25718;
  surfaced this period). The right combination of action-chunking
  (which makes PPO's credit assignment tractable for VLAs) and
  self-behavior-cloning (which prevents the chunked rollouts from
  drifting too far from the base policy). 93% success / 42.17 steps
  on MetaWorld. Reads as the production-grade pairing of FPO
  (2510.09976) and Adaptive Q-Chunking (2605.05544).

- **SA-VLA: Spatially-Aware Flow-Matching for VLA RL** (arXiv:2602.00743;
  surfaced this period). The spatial-grounding-preserving RL adaptation
  framework. The motivation is sharp: pure RL fine-tuning *erases*
  the spatial grounding that the VLM backbone provides; SA-VLA aligns
  the representation learning, reward design, and exploration with
  task *geometry*. The right complement to FPO's likelihood-free
  policy ratio.

- **ReinFlow — Fine-tuning Flow Policy with Online RL** (arXiv:2505.22094;
  carried). Learnable-noise injection converts a flow policy into a
  discrete-time Markov process for exact likelihood — 135.36% reward
  growth on legged locomotion.

- ★ **FPO: RL Fine-Tuning of Flow-Matching Policies** (arXiv:2510.09976;
  carried highlight). Likelihood-free policy ratio. The on-policy
  update rule for Qwen-VLA's DiT head.

- ★ **D-VLA — Distributed Asynchronous RL for VLAs** (arXiv:2605.13276;
  carried highlight). Plane decoupling + swimlane pipeline.

- ★ **AcceRL** (arXiv:2603.18464; carried highlight). Asynchronous
  worker topology — composes over D-VLA's plane decoupling.

- **AtomVLA — Predictive Latent WM as Post-Training Objective**
  (arXiv:2603.08519; carried highlight). 97.0% LIBERO, 48.0%
  LIBERO-PRO via GRPO over WM-predicted reward.

- **Simple-Recipe-Works — VLAs Are Natural Continual Learners with
  RL** (arXiv:2603.11653; carried highlight). RL sidesteps SFT
  forgetting.

## 7. Benchmarks, Simulators & Evaluation

- ★ **WorldArena: Unified Benchmark for Embodied World Models**
  (arXiv:2602.08971; surfaced this period). The right benchmark for
  the now-dense WM-VLA branch. WorldArena's design choice: separate
  *perception* and *functional utility* evaluation — the WM is
  scored not just on how well it predicts the next frame, but on
  whether the predicted latent supports downstream action selection.
  Reads as the WM counterpart to VLA-Arena's factorial difficulty
  (2512.22539, carried highlight): VLA-Arena tells you *which
  difficulty axis* the policy fails on; WorldArena tells you *which
  part of the WM* is doing the work in WM-augmented VLAs.

- **VLA-Survey: Datasets, Benchmarks, Data Engines** (arXiv:2604.23001;
  surfaced this period). The reference survey for the data-engine
  side of the field. Useful as a coordinate system for placing the
  AtomVLA / World2Act / PLD post-training stack.

- **VLATest — Testing and Evaluating VLA for Manipulation**
  (arXiv:2409.12894; surfaced this period). The *testing*-oriented
  evaluation framework, complementary to VLA-Arena's factorial axes
  and REALM's perturbation factors — VLATest treats the model as a
  software artifact to be tested rather than a policy to be
  benchmarked.

- **VLA-Arena** (arXiv:2512.22539; carried highlight). Factorial

- **REALM** (arXiv:2512.19562; carried highlight). 15 perturbation

## Cross-Cutting Theme — the post-training stack assembles

The previous cycle (05-31) named four open seams in the consolidated
VLA stack: the VLM↔action interface (DIAL), the post-training
objective (AtomVLA), the factorial evaluation (VLA-Arena), and the
cross-embodiment yardstick (Embodied4C). This cycle's signal is that
the **post-training seam is filling fastest** — the cycle closed with
three concrete answers to AtomVLA's open questions:

1. **The objective is contrastive, not generative.** World2Act
   (2603.10422) is the cleanest statement: do *not* refine actions in
   pixel space (where every WM hallucination leaks through); align
   the policy's action features against the WM's *video-dynamic
   latent* via a contrastive loss. The substrate-vs-objective split
   in §4 stops being a hypothetical.

2. **The data is RL-generated, not teleoperated.** PLD (ICLR 2026)
   shows residual-RL-generated, policy-aligned trajectories *surpass*
   teleoperation on the same backbone. The data bottleneck that has
   gated VLA scaling since π₀ may be soluble without more
   teleoperators.

3. **The training is bounded, not heuristic.** CRL-VLA (2602.03445)
   ties continual-learning stability to *goal-conditioned advantage
   magnitude* — the first VLA-continual-RL bound in terms of
   estimable quantities. The empirical claim of Simple-Recipe-Works
   (2603.11653) now has a theoretical companion.

Three secondary seams visible this cycle:

4. **Active perception is the third structural answer to grounding.**
   The cluttered-scene line had two before: externalize the referent
   (Point-What-You-Mean) or internalize it (DIAL). ActiveVLA
   (2601.08325) adds the third: *acquire more views*. The
   active-perception loss is the right structural complement when
   the scene is genuinely ambiguous from any single viewpoint.

5. **Long-horizon efficiency is a KV-cache problem, not a memory
   problem.** SD-VLA-2 (2602.03983) reframes the long-horizon
   efficiency question from "what should the memory module
   compress?" to "which tokens are static and can be cached?" The
   reframe is mechanical and the speedups are large — the right
   complement to MEM / EchoVLA / HiF-VLA on the *retrieval* side.

6. **The WM benchmark question gets a first-class answer.**
   WorldArena (2602.08971) separates perception from *functional
   utility* — the WM is scored on whether the predicted latent
   supports downstream action selection, not on next-frame fidelity.
   The right yardstick to keep the WM-VLA line from optimizing video
   metrics that don't transfer to policy gain.

The honest summary: **the post-training stack assembled in a single
cycle.** The combination World2Act × PLD × CRL-VLA × FPO × D-VLA ×
Qwen-VLA × AtomVLA × World-Env is now a concrete recipe with a
loss-side answer (World2Act), a data-side answer (PLD), a
theoretical bound (CRL-VLA), an update rule (FPO), a distributed
substrate (D-VLA), a backbone (Qwen-VLA), a representation enricher
(AtomVLA), and a rollout environment (World-Env). The remaining open
question is *not* "what's in the stack?" but *"what does it look like
end-to-end on VLA-Arena?"*

## Picks of the day (if you only read three)

1. **World2Act — Latent Action Post-Training via Skill-Compositional
   World Models (2603.10422)** — the cleanest answer to AtomVLA's
   open question. Contrastive alignment to the WM's video-dynamic
   latent (instead of pixel-space refinement) is the right loss-side
   contribution for the post-training stack. Composes with Qwen-VLA
   (backbone), AtomVLA (predictive latent), D-VLA (substrate), and
   FPO (update rule).

2. **PLD — Self-Improving VLAs via Residual RL (ICLR 2026)** — the
   data-side answer to the same post-training stack. RL-generated,
   policy-aligned trajectories surpass teleoperation-only
   demonstrations. The residual-RL probe head + distillation framing
   is the right structural answer to the data-scarcity bottleneck —
   and a natural curriculum partner for VLA-Arena's factorial
   difficulty axes.

3. **CRL-VLA — Continual Vision-Language-Action Learning (2602.03445)**
   — the theory. The first VLA-continual-RL performance bound in
   terms of *goal-conditioned advantage magnitude* — i.e. quantities
   you can actually estimate from rollouts. The bound makes explicit
   why VLAs work as continual learners where general policies don't:
   the goal-conditioning channel keeps the advantage magnitude
   bounded across tasks.

## Open backlog for the next cycle (to watch)

- **World2Act × AtomVLA × D-VLA × FPO × Qwen-VLA — the end-to-end
  post-training run.** Every component now exists separately; the
  first paper to actually compose them and report VLA-Arena numbers
  on the factorial grid will be the most informative empirical
  result of the year. The expected failure mode — and the one to
  watch — is the *composition cost*: each component adds a
  hyperparameter cliff, and the stack is now deep enough that the
  empirical recipe matters more than any single component.

- **CRL-VLA-style bounds for World2Act's contrastive objective.**
  The natural theoretical follow-up: World2Act's contrastive
  alignment loss admits a bound of the form CRL-VLA derives for
  goal-conditioned advantage. The bound would close the theory-side
  gap between supervised pre-train and contrastive post-train.

- **PLD's residual probe head as a VLA-Arena curriculum signal.**
  The probe head *naturally* finds the Safety / Distractor /
  Extrapolation / Long-Horizon states the policy is brittle on.
  Using the probe distribution to *schedule* further training would
  close the data-generation → curriculum → evaluation loop.

- **ActiveVLA × Point-What-You-Mean — the structured grounding
  combination.** ActiveVLA's view-acquisition channel and
  Point-What-You-Mean's external pointer are the two cleanest
  external-grounding answers. The combination — pointer-guided view
  acquisition — is the obvious experiment.

- **WorldArena as the WM-VLA training objective.** WorldArena scores
  the WM on *functional utility*. The first paper to use that score
  as a *training signal* (rather than just an evaluation) would
  finally answer "what does the WM need to be good at?" with a
  policy-aligned answer.

- **SD-VLA-2's static-dynamic disentanglement on Qwen-VLA's
  navigation channel.** Qwen-VLA carries navigation and manipulation
  in one backbone; SD-VLA-2's KV-cache reuse trick is most valuable
  in the long-horizon navigation regime. The composition is the
  obvious efficiency experiment for the generalist.
