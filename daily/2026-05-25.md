# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-05-25)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

This cycle is a **grounded roundup** pinned to actual arXiv releases
surfaced this period. The freshest items are **PointACT** (2605.21414,
a dual-system *3D-aware* VLA, May 20) and two world-model arrivals that
landed almost back-to-back — the **World Action Models** position
survey (2605.12090) and **Reinforcing VLAs in Task-Agnostic World
Models** (2605.12334). The dominant signal this cycle is a shift from
*2D token soup* toward *explicit structure in the action interface*:
(1) geometry is re-entering the loop — point clouds and 3D priors are
being fed straight into action decoding (PointACT) rather than left to
the VLM to infer; (2) the world-model line is consolidating into a
named research program ("World Action Models") while simultaneously
splitting hairs over *whether the world model should be task-agnostic*
(2605.12334) or co-trained with the policy (World-VLA-Loop, VLAW);
(3) the reasoning thread matured past "chain-of-thought as decoration"
into *action* chain-of-thought (ACoT-VLA) and an honest accounting of
its inference cost (VLA-Perf, DuoCore-FS). Memory also got a cleaner
benchmark story (VQ-Memory's non-Markovian suites, LongBench's
real-world long-horizon tasks).

---

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **PointACT: Vision-Language-Action Models with Multi-Scale
  Point-Action Interaction** (arXiv:2605.21414, May 20, 2026). The
  freshest design release. A **dual-system, 3D-aware** policy that
  injects *hierarchical 3D point-cloud representations* directly into
  the action-decoding pathway, rather than asking a 2D VLM backbone to
  recover geometry implicitly. The motivation is exactly right for the
  manipulation regime: contact and grasp are 3D problems, and a
  multi-scale point→action interaction gives the decoder explicit
  spatial structure that RGB tokens routinely blur. The most
  well-motivated architecture entry of the cycle — geometry belongs in
  the action interface, not just the perception front-end.

- ★ **ACoT-VLA: Action Chain-of-Thought for Vision-Language-Action
  Models** (arXiv:2601.11404, CVPR 2026). Reframes chain-of-thought so
  the intermediate "thoughts" are *coarse action intents* rather than
  free-text rationales. Pairs an **Explicit Action Reasoner** (proposes
  coarse reference trajectories as explicit action-level reasoning
  steps) with an **Implicit Action Reasoner** (extracts latent action
  priors from the model's internal representations). The right fix for
  the well-known failure where text-CoT helps the language head but
  never reaches the controller: keep the reasoning *in action space* so
  it actually conditions control.

- **MMaDA-VLA: Large Diffusion VLA with Unified Multimodal Instruction
  and Generation** (arXiv:2603.25406; carried). Single discrete
  token-denoising backbone that jointly generates a future goal
  observation and an action chunk; **98.0%** average LIBERO and
  **4.78** on CALVIN. Still the cleanest "discrete diffusion for both
  perception and control" baseline; reads naturally alongside the
  world-model section.

- **DeepThinkVLA: Enhancing Reasoning Capability of VLAs**
  (arXiv:2511.15669). SFT on embodied chain-of-thought datasets to make
  the policy "think before acting." A useful counterpoint to ACoT-VLA:
  DeepThinkVLA keeps the reasoning in text and shows the data recipe
  matters; ACoT-VLA argues the reasoning should be re-expressed in
  action space. The contrast is the interesting part.

- **LLaDA-VLA: Vision-Language-Diffusion-Action Models**
  (arXiv:2509.06932). Builds an action policy on a pretrained
  *discrete-diffusion* VLM (d-VLM) rather than an autoregressive one —
  the upstream of MMaDA-VLA's design lineage, worth citing for the
  d-VLM-as-backbone provenance.

- *(Carried efficiency / design anchors: Evo-1 (2511.04555, semantic-
  alignment preservation), SD-VLA (2602.03983, static/dynamic token
  disentanglement), VITA-VLA (2510.09607, action-expert distillation),
  TinyVLA, SmolVLA, π₀ / π₀-FAST, OpenVLA-OFT.)*

## 2. Instruction Following & Language Grounding

- ★ **When Vision Overrides Language: Counterfactual Failures in VLAs**
  (arXiv:2602.17659; carried highlight). Still the sharpest diagnostic
  on the books — the "illusion of instruction following" that collapses
  under counterfactual prompts. It remains the eval lens through which
  every instruction-following entry below should be read.

- **CAST: Counterfactual Labels Improve Instruction Following**
  (arXiv:2508.13446; carried). The constructive complement: VLM-driven
  counterfactual relabeling of existing demos so the policy can no
  longer shortcut through vision. Cheap, data-centric, root-cause.

- **Restoring Linguistic Grounding via Train-Free Attention
  Recalibration** (arXiv:2603.06001; carried). The no-budget,
  inference-time patch for already-deployed checkpoints; pairs with the
  two above as the diagnose → fix → retrofit pipeline.

- **Actions as Language: Fine-Tuning VLMs into VLAs Without
  Catastrophic Forgetting** (arXiv:2509.22195; carried). Treating
  actions as another language modality preserves core VLM capabilities
  and enables zero-shot, multilingual, open-world-semantic instruction
  generalization — the forgetting framing connects directly to Evo-1
  (§1).

- *(Carried grounding anchors: LangForce — Bayesian decomposition via
  latent action queries (2601.15197); Point What You Mean — visually
  grounded pointing policy (2512.18933).)*

## 3. VLA with Memory & Long-Horizon Reasoning

- ★ **Beyond Short-Horizon: VQ-Memory for Robust Long-Horizon
  Manipulation in Non-Markovian Simulation Benchmarks**
  (arXiv:2603.09513). Encodes past *proprioceptive* states into a
  compact, **vector-quantized** set of discrete latent tokens that drop
  cleanly into a VLA's token stream — and, crucially, frames the
  evaluation on *explicitly non-Markovian* benchmarks where memoryless
  policies must fail by construction. The discrete, proprioception-first
  memory is a different and complementary bet to the visual-token-memory
  line (SD-VLA, MemoryVLA); the non-Markovian framing is what makes the
  result interpretable.

- ★ **LongBench: Evaluating Robotic Manipulation Policies on Real-World
  Long-Horizon Tasks** (arXiv:2604.16788). A *real-world* long-horizon
  benchmark — the missing physical counterpart to the simulator-side
  memory suites (LIBERO-memory, RoboCerebra). The field has plenty of
  simulated long-horizon scores; a real-robot long-horizon yardstick is
  exactly what the memory line needed to validate against.

- **Long-Horizon Manipulation via Trace-Conditioned VLA Planning
  (LoHo-Manip)** (arXiv:2604.21924). A modular framework that scales a
  short-horizon VLA executor to long-horizon instruction following via a
  dedicated *task-management VLM* and trace conditioning. The clean
  separation of "what to do next" (slow VLM manager) from "how to do it"
  (fast VLA executor) is the practical, deployable take on long-horizon
  control.

- **RB-VLA: Recursive Belief Vision-Language-Action Models**
  (arXiv:2602.20659; carried highlight). The POMDP-correct answer to
  "what should a VLA remember" — a compact recursive belief state in
  place of raw history. +52.5% / +37.5% on multi-stage pick-and-place /
  stacking. Still the strongest *abstraction* in the memory line.

- *(Carried memory anchors: MemoryVLA — 84.0% on 12 real tasks, +26 on
  long-horizon (2508.19236); SD-VLA + LIBERO-memory (2602.03983);
  TempoFit — plug-and-play layer-wise temporal KV (2603.07647); MAP-VLA
  (2511.09516); RoboCerebra (2506.06677).)*

## 4. World-Model-Based VLA

- ★ **World Action Models: The Next Frontier in Embodied AI**
  (arXiv:2605.12090). A position/survey that gives the fast-fragmenting
  world-model line a *name and a definition*: World Action Models (WAMs)
  are embodied foundation models that unify predictive state modeling
  with action generation, targeting a **joint distribution over future
  states and actions**. Useful precisely because the subfield needed a
  shared vocabulary — it sets the axis on which GigaBrain, World-Gymnast,
  RISE, World-VLA-Loop, and VLAW can be compared. Read with the
  complementary "World Model for Robot Learning" survey (2605.00080).

- ★ **Reinforcing VLAs in Task-Agnostic World Models**
  (arXiv:2605.12334). Builds an action-conditioned world model on top of
  a video-generation backbone and optimizes the VLA via policy gradients
  guided by success rewards — with the key design choice being a
  *task-agnostic* world model (one model, many tasks) rather than a
  per-task simulator. The task-agnosticism is the well-motivated bet: if
  the imagined environment generalizes across tasks, you amortize the
  expensive world-model training across the whole skill repertoire.

- **World-VLA-Loop: Closed-Loop Learning of Video World Model and VLA
  Policy** (arXiv:2602.06508). Jointly refines a *state-aware* video
  world model (predicting future observations **and** reward) and the
  VLA policy in a closed loop, so the simulator improves as the policy
  does. The natural counter-position to the task-agnostic stance above —
  co-adaptation vs. a frozen general world model is the cycle's cleanest
  open question.

- **VLAW: Iterative Co-Improvement of VLA Policy and World Model**
  (arXiv:2602.12063). Learns an action-conditioned world model from
  *limited real online rollouts* and then generates large-scale
  synthetic data "in imagination." The data-efficiency angle — buy a
  little real interaction, manufacture the rest — is the practical
  argument for the co-improvement camp.

- *(Carried world-model anchors: GigaBrain-0.5M* / RAMP — ~30% on hard
  long-horizon tasks (2602.12099); World-Gymnast — reward-from-imagined-
  video (2602.02454); RISE — compositional self-improvement (2602.11075);
  VLA-JEPA — minimal JEPA+action-head recipe (2602.10098);
  Do-World-Action-Models-Generalize robustness study (2603.22078);
  World Model for Robot Learning survey (2605.00080).)*

## 5. Latent Action Embeddings / Action Representation

- ★ **ALAM: Algebraically Consistent Latent Action Model**
  (arXiv:2605.10819; carried highlight). Still the freshest and
  best-motivated latent-action result: algebraic-consistency constraints
  on how latent actions *compose*, lifting MetaWorld MT50 from
  **47.9% → 85.0%** and LIBERO **94.1% → 98.1%**. The lesson the cycle
  keeps reinforcing — structure, not codebook capacity, is the
  bottleneck.

- ★ **JALA: Joint-Aligned Latent Action — Scalable VLA Pretraining in
  the Wild** (arXiv:2602.21736; carried). Scales latent-action
  pretraining to a **7.5M-clip / >2,000-hour** corpus (UniHand-Mix)
  blending lab + in-the-wild footage, bypassing full visual
  reconstruction by aligning a predictive action embedding to both
  inverse dynamics and real actions. The data-scale proof the
  LAPA-lineage needed.

- **WholebodyVLA: Towards Unified Latent VLA for Whole-Body
  Loco-Manipulation Control** (ICLR 2026, OpenDriveLab). Pushes the
  latent-action interface from a tabletop arm to *whole-body*
  loco-manipulation, where the latent must coordinate locomotion and
  manipulation jointly. The body-scale generalization is the interesting
  stress test for whether latent-action abstractions survive a much
  larger action space.

- *(Carried latent-action anchors: UniLACT — depth-aware RGB latent
  actions (2602.20231); Hierarchical Latent Action Model (2603.05815);
  LAPA (2410.11758); Latent Action Pretraining Through World Modeling
  (2509.18428); UniVLA — task-centric latent actions (2505.06111).)*

## 6. Reinforcement Learning for VLA

- ★ **Simple Recipe Works: VLAs are Natural Continual Learners with
  Reinforcement Learning** (arXiv:2603.11653). Argues — and shows — that
  RL post-training lets a VLA absorb new skills *without* the
  catastrophic forgetting that plagues supervised fine-tuning, making
  the VLA a natural continual learner. The well-motivated reframing: the
  recurring "fine-tuning erodes the backbone" complaint (Evo-1,
  Actions-as-Language) is partly a *learning-objective* problem, and RL
  is a surprisingly clean lever on it.

- **VLA-RFT: VLA Reinforcement Fine-Tuning with Verified Rewards in
  World Simulators** (arXiv:2510.00406). RL fine-tuning where the reward
  is *verified* inside a world simulator — the bridge between the §4
  world-model substrate and a well-posed RL signal. The verified-reward
  framing is the part that keeps imagined-rollout RL honest.

- **Balancing Signal and Variance: Adaptive Offline RL Post-Training for
  VLA Flow Models** (arXiv:2509.04063). Tackles the specific instability
  of doing RL on *flow-matching* action heads via an adaptively scaled
  bias–variance trade-off in the flow loss. The practical fix the
  flow-policy crowd (π₀-style) needs to post-train without collapse.

- *(Carried RL anchors: GigaBrain / RAMP (2602.12099, see §4); VLA-RL —
  scalable RL on a VLA backbone (2505.18719); SimpleVLA-RL / PRIME-RL
  (ICLR 2026, GitHub); RLinf — embodied/agentic RL infrastructure now
  shipping world-model-based RL fine-tuning.)*

## 7. Benchmarks, Simulators & Evaluation

- ★ **RobotArena∞: Scalable Robot Benchmarking via Real-to-Sim
  Translation** (arXiv:2510.23571). Automatically converts video
  demonstrations from widely used robot datasets into *simulated*
  counterparts, then evaluates VLAs at scale with online human feedback.
  The well-motivated move: instead of hand-authoring sim tasks (which
  drift from reality), *translate real demos into sim* so the eval
  distribution inherits real-world coverage. Read alongside REALM
  (2512.19562) — the two real-to-sim efforts are converging on the same
  goal of sim-cheap-but-real-correlated evaluation.

- ★ **VLA-Perf: How Fast Can I Run My VLA?** (arXiv:2602.18397). A
  systematic measurement study of VLA *inference* performance across
  model scale, architecture, long-context video inputs, async inference,
  and dual-system pipelines. Underrated and badly needed — the field
  reports task success obsessively and latency almost never, even though
  control frequency is what decides whether a policy is deployable.

- **REALM: A Real-to-Sim Validated Benchmark** (arXiv:2512.19562;
  carried highlight). 15 perturbation factors, 7 skills, 3,500+ objects,
  with empirically validated sim↔real correlation. Still the cleanest
  statement of "make cheap sim evaluation trustworthy."

- **StaticEmbodiedBench: Static and Plugged — Make Embodied Evaluation
  Simple** (arXiv:2508.06553). A static, unified, plug-and-play benchmark
  (42 scenarios, 8 evaluation dimensions) that sidesteps the cost and
  flakiness of live rollouts. The pragmatic option for fast, reproducible
  comparison when you cannot stand up a simulator per paper.

- **VLNVerse: A Benchmark for Vision-Language Navigation with Versatile,
  Embodied, Realistic Simulation** (arXiv:2512.19021). Extends the
  rigorous-benchmark push into *navigation*, the action space the
  manipulation-heavy benchmark suite under-serves.

- *(Carried benchmark anchors: Rethinking-Practicality unified benchmark
  + baseline (2602.22663); WoW embodied world-model Turing test
  (2601.04137); VLABench (OpenMOSS); LIBERO / CALVIN / MetaWorld MT50 /
  RoboCasa; AGIBOT Genie Sim 3.0.)*

## 8. Applications: Navigation, Driving, Dexterous & Humanoid

- ★ **DuoCore-FS: Asynchronous Fast-Slow VLA Policies for Whole-Body
  Robotic Manipulation** (arXiv:2512.20188). A *truly asynchronous*
  fast-slow framework — a fast pathway for high-frequency action
  generation and a slow pathway for rich VLM reasoning that run at
  **independent frequencies** rather than locking the controller to the
  VLM's slow tick. The well-motivated systems insight: the bottleneck in
  dual-system VLAs is the synchronous coupling, and decoupling the clocks
  is what restores control stability. Pairs directly with VLA-Perf (§7)
  and Fast-in-Slow (2506.01953, 117.7 Hz).

- **Vision-Language-Action Models for Autonomous Driving: Past, Present,
  and Future** (arXiv:2512.16760). The reference survey for the driving
  branch of VLA, mapping HiST-VLA / OpenDriveVLA / ColaVLA-style motion
  planners. Useful for placing the manipulation-centric VLA literature
  next to the driving variant, where the action space and horizon differ
  sharply.

- **LeVERB: Humanoid Whole-Body Control with Latent Vision-Language
  Instruction** (arXiv:2506.13751). Latent VL-instruction conditioning
  for humanoid *whole-body* control — the humanoid counterpart to
  WholebodyVLA (§5), and evidence the latent-instruction interface
  transfers to high-DoF bodies.

- *(Carried application anchors: METIS — multi-source egocentric
  dexterous VLA (2511.17366); ABot-N0 navigation foundation model
  (2602.11598); AIR-VLA aerial manipulation (2601.21602); DexVLA
  (2502.05855).)*

## Cross-Cutting Theme — structure is moving into the action interface

Stepping back, the through-line this cycle is that *structure the field
used to leave implicit is being pushed into the action interface*:

1. **Geometry re-enters the loop.** PointACT (2605.21414) feeds
   multi-scale 3D point clouds straight into action decoding instead of
   trusting a 2D VLM to recover depth; WholebodyVLA and LeVERB push the
   *latent* action interface to whole-body, high-DoF control. The shared
   intuition: manipulation is a 3D, embodied problem, and the action head
   should see that structure explicitly.

2. **The world-model line acquires a name and a fault line.** "World
   Action Models" (2605.12090) gives the program a definition (a joint
   distribution over future states and actions), and almost in the same
   breath the field splits on *task-agnostic* world models (2605.12334)
   vs. *policy-co-trained* ones (World-VLA-Loop 2602.06508, VLAW
   2602.12063). That tension — general frozen simulator vs. co-adapting
   one — is the cleanest open question of the cycle.

3. **Reasoning grows up — and gets a latency bill.** ACoT-VLA
   (2601.11404) moves chain-of-thought *into action space* so it
   actually conditions control, while VLA-Perf (2602.18397) and DuoCore-FS
   (2512.20188) force an honest accounting of what all that reasoning
   costs at inference and how to decouple it from the control clock.

The natural next combinations: **PointACT-style 3D action decoding ×
a task-agnostic world-model RL substrate** (geometry-aware actions are
exactly what should make imagined contact-rich rollouts stay physical),
and **ACoT-style action chain-of-thought × DuoCore-FS async scheduling**
(put the slow action-reasoning on the slow clock, the chunk execution on
the fast one).

## Picks of the day (if you only read three)

1. **PointACT (2605.21414)** — the freshest release and the cleanest
   argument that geometry belongs *in the action interface*. A
   dual-system, 3D-aware policy that decodes from hierarchical
   point-cloud structure rather than re-deriving depth from RGB tokens.

2. **World Action Models survey (2605.12090) + Reinforcing VLAs in
   Task-Agnostic World Models (2605.12334)** — read together, they name
   the world-model program *and* stake out the task-agnostic position
   within it. The pairing tells you where the §4 surge is heading.

3. **Simple Recipe Works (2603.11653)** — the quietly important RL
   result: VLAs are natural *continual* learners under RL, reframing the
   "fine-tuning erodes the backbone" complaint as a learning-objective
   problem with a clean lever. If you fine-tune VLAs, this should change
   your default post-training recipe.

## Open backlog for the next cycle (to watch)

- **3D action interfaces × world-model RL** — PointACT-style geometric
  action decoding as the action space for task-agnostic in-world-model RL
  (2605.12334). Geometry-aware actions are precisely what should keep
  imagined contact-rich rollouts physically consistent.
- **Task-agnostic vs. co-trained world models** — a head-to-head between
  the frozen-general-simulator stance (2605.12334) and the co-improvement
  camp (World-VLA-Loop 2602.06508, VLAW 2602.12063) on the same task
  suite is the experiment the field now needs.
- **Latency-aware leaderboards** — VLA-Perf (2602.18397) makes the case;
  the benchmarks (REALM, RobotArena∞) should start reporting control
  frequency next to success rate so deployability is first-class.
- **Real-world memory benchmarks** — LongBench (2604.16788) is the
  real-robot start; an hour-scale, streaming, partially-observable suite
  remains the gap for the RB-VLA / VQ-Memory / MemoryVLA line to be
  compared on.
