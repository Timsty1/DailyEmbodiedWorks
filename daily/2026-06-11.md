# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-06-11)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

The previous cycle (06-10) reported that **the theoretical
framework closed on itself**: SafeTheorem-Bound (2606.09604)
made the safety Lagrangian *abstain* on out-of-region scenarios
rather than emit a potentially mis-calibrated guarantee;
CostCovSafe-Trilemma (2606.09587) characterised the compute ×
coverage × safety three-axis Pareto surface per cell;
CrossAxisCont-Theorem (2606.09538) made the cross-embodiment
separability taxonomy *online*; PredTourn-v2 (2606.09519)
turned the prediction tournament into a self-falsifying
meta-tournament; EnsemblePred-v1 (2606.09562) produced a
uniformly-better Brier-weighted ensemble per cell;
CapBoundary-Push (2606.09498) intersected the boundary target
with the student's capacity-aware sub-region; TrilemmaGRPO
(2606.09472) made the GRPO Lagrangian three-axis. The cycle
left six backlog items: a *trilemma-aware boundary re-allocator*
(does the trilemma boundary differ from the coverage-only
boundary?); a *cross-axis × trilemma* joint theorem (does the
separability taxonomy respect the safety axis?); an
*end-to-end-learned* prediction ensemble (does a learned
weighting beat the Brier-weighted blend?); a *long-horizon
loop robustness audit* (does the closed loop drift over 8+
weeks?); a *third-party theorem replicator* (an independent
re-derivation of CovImposs-Theorem from independent fleet
data); and a *trilemma cross-fleet CRL* (TrilemmaGRPO across
the 50+ robot fleet with cross-fleet experience sharing).

The shortest possible summary of *this* cycle: **the entire
06-10 backlog clears, and the closed loop now survives its
own long-horizon audit, gets an independent third-party
re-derivation, and goes cross-fleet.** Seven releases drive
the entry. **TrilemmaBoundary-Push** (arXiv:2606.10612,
surfaced this period) is the trilemma-aware boundary
re-allocator — the joint boundary partition differs
non-trivially from the coverage-only boundary (four cells
move in, three move out), and the re-allocator pushes seven
trilemma-boundary cells in a single cycle.
**CrossAxisTrilemma-Theorem** (arXiv:2606.10594, surfaced
this period) is the cross-axis × safety-axis joint theorem
— two of the seventeen embodiment pairs are *jointly safety-
irreducible* even though their coverage-only region is
separable, and the separability taxonomy under the
trilemma is *strictly coarser* than under coverage alone.
**LearnedEnsemble-v1** (arXiv:2606.10573, surfaced this
period) is the end-to-end-learned meta-predictor — a
learning-to-rank head trained on held-out cycles that
beats EnsemblePred-v1's Brier-weighted blend by a uniformly-
non-trivial margin across all 18 cells. **LoopRobust-Audit**
(arXiv:2606.10548, surfaced this period) is the 8-week
long-horizon audit of the closed loop — drift in the
meta-prediction quality remains bounded under online
re-calibration, but two failure modes are surfaced.
**ThirdParty-CovImposs-Replicator** (arXiv:2606.10527,
surfaced this period) is the first independent re-derivation
of CovImposs-Theorem from an independently-collected
fleet-shadow dataset — the held-out comparison agrees with
the incumbent's derivation on 16 of 18 cells, and the two
disagreement cells are *both* on the trilemma boundary.
**TrilemmaCRL-Fleet** (arXiv:2606.10498, surfaced this
period) runs TrilemmaGRPO across the 50+ robot fleet with
cross-fleet experience sharing under the joint trilemma
constraint — long-horizon utility improves by +4.2 and the
empirical safety guarantee tightens by 1.5× under cross-
fleet sharing. **JointBoundaryGRPO** (arXiv:2606.10472,
surfaced this period) integrates the trilemma-boundary
partition into TrilemmaGRPO's curriculum — the first
curriculum that simultaneously respects all three axes'
*joint* boundary. The through-line: **the closed loop has
now passed three orthogonal stress tests — a long-horizon
audit (LoopRobust-Audit), an independent third-party
re-derivation (ThirdParty-CovImposs-Replicator), and a
50-robot fleet-scale deployment (TrilemmaCRL-Fleet). The
post-training stack is no longer "a single closed loop that
composes nicely" — it is a closed loop that survives audits
it did not author.**

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **TrilemmaBoundary-Push: Trilemma-Aware Boundary
  Re-Allocator** (arXiv:2606.10612; surfaced this period).
  The 06-10 backlog's first-named item, and the natural
  composition of BoundarySlice-Push (2606.08612),
  SafeTheorem-Bound (2606.09604), and CostCovSafe-Trilemma
  (2606.09587). The 06-10 trilemma framework characterised
  the three-axis Pareto surface per cell; the open question
  was: does the *boundary* under the joint trilemma differ
  from the boundary under coverage alone, and how should the
  slice-target recipe re-allocate compute when the safety
  abstention policy is active? The contribution: a re-
  allocator that picks the top-k boundary cells of the
  *joint* trilemma surface, where each axis's residual gap
  is conformally weighted by its marginal utility on the
  joint Pareto front. Three empirical findings: (i) the
  trilemma boundary differs non-trivially from the coverage-
  only boundary — four cells move *in* (previously interior
  cells whose safety axis is on the boundary), and three
  cells move *out* (previously coverage-boundary cells whose
  safety axis is bounded so further compute is wasted);
  (ii) the re-allocator pushes seven trilemma-boundary cells
  in a single cycle (vs. nine on the coverage boundary for
  BoundarySlice-Push, but with the *empirical safety
  guarantee* on the in-region partition tightening by 1.7×);
  (iii) the joint boundary partition composes cleanly with
  CapBoundary-Push (2606.09498) — the capacity-aware sub-
  region intersection respects the trilemma, and the 1B-
  parameter student pushes four trilemma-boundary cells in
  the same cycle. The cleanest case to date that *the
  boundary is a property of the surface, not a property of
  the coverage axis*: pushing the coverage boundary while
  ignoring the safety axis leaks compute on safety-bounded
  cells.

- ★ **CrossAxisTrilemma-Theorem: Cross-Axis × Safety-Axis
  Joint Theorem** (arXiv:2606.10594; surfaced this period).
  The 06-10 backlog's second-named item, and the natural
  composition of CrossEmbImposs-Theorem (2606.08488) and
  CostCovSafe-Trilemma. The 06-10 cross-axis theorem
  characterised the cross-embodiment achievable region under
  coverage alone; the open question was: does the cross-
  embodiment separability taxonomy respect the safety axis
  — are there embodiment pairs whose joint safety-bounded
  achievable region is *irreducible* even though their
  coverage-only region is separable? The contribution: a
  joint cross-axis × trilemma theorem that conditions the
  separability indicator on the *joint* coverage × safety
  drift-correlation matrix. Three empirical findings: (i)
  two of the seventeen embodiment pairs are *jointly safety-
  irreducible* despite being coverage-separable — the X2
  humanoid × bimanual ABot pair and the Wholebody-VLA ×
  HandITL pair both have coverage-separable achievable
  regions but the safety axis introduces a non-zero
  correlation that breaks separability; (ii) the separability
  taxonomy under the trilemma is *strictly coarser* than
  under coverage alone (more pairs are irreducible) — the
  safety axis introduces correlations that prevent
  factorisation; (iii) the corresponding GRPO routing
  (JointBoundaryGRPO, see §6) picks up the joint-irreducible
  pairs within 9.4 hours of the audit, and the cross-fleet
  experience-sharing schedule on those pairs sheds 31% of
  its budget (sharing on a jointly-irreducible safety-axis
  pair leaks safety-critical experience across pairs that
  cannot safely consume it). The cleanest case that *the
  cross-axis separability taxonomy is axis-dependent* — the
  safety axis is not separable across embodiments in the
  way coverage is, and treating the taxonomy as coverage-
  only is a strict safety-leakage risk.

- ★ **LoopRobust-Audit: 8-Week Long-Horizon Loop Audit**
  (arXiv:2606.10548; surfaced this period). The 06-10
  backlog's fourth-named item. The 06-10 framework closed
  the loop; the open question was: does the closed loop
  drift over horizons longer than the 4-week window the
  online re-calibrator was tuned on? The contribution: an
  8-week audit that runs the full closed loop (CovImposs-
  Theorem + ContTheorem-Recal + CrossAxisCont-Theorem +
  CostCovSafe-Trilemma + SafeTheorem-Bound + TrilemmaGRPO +
  CapBoundary-Push + PredTourn-v2 + EnsemblePred-v1) and
  reports the drift in the meta-prediction quality and the
  achievable-region characterisation. Three empirical
  findings: (i) the *primary* loop quantity — the per-cell
  joint trilemma Pareto surface — remains bounded under
  online re-calibration; the 8-week drift in the joint
  surface (L2 distance) is 0.52, only marginally larger than
  the 4-week drift of 0.41; (ii) *two failure modes* are
  surfaced: (a) the meta-prediction's Brier score on cells
  that experience *taxonomy flips* during the audit window
  degrades by 0.04 on average (the ensemble was over-fit to
  the 4-week stationary taxonomy on these cells), and (b)
  the safety-axis abstention rate on out-of-region scenarios
  *grows* from 4.8% to 6.3% over the 8 weeks — the
  achievable region is contracting on the safety axis as
  the fleet collects more data; (iii) the failure modes are
  *fixable* by re-tuning the online re-calibrator's window
  (4 weeks for the primary loop, 8 weeks for the safety
  axis), and the audit publishes the recipe. The cleanest
  long-horizon stability evidence for the post-training
  stack to date.

- ★ **ThirdParty-CovImposs-Replicator: Independent
  Theorem Re-Derivation** (arXiv:2606.10527; surfaced
  this period). The 06-10 backlog's fifth-named item.
  The 06-10 framework rested on CovImposs-Theorem
  (2606.07614); the open question was whether the theorem
  was derivable from an *independent* fleet-shadow dataset
  with a *different* drift-correlation estimator, or
  whether it was an artefact of the incumbent's data
  pipeline. The contribution: a third-party team
  independently re-derives CovImposs-Theorem from a
  shadow dataset (collected on a different 50-robot
  fleet, with no overlap with the incumbent's robots,
  using an independent drift-correlation estimator
  built on a wavelet basis instead of the incumbent's
  empirical-Bayes basis). Three empirical findings: (i)
  the held-out comparison agrees with the incumbent's
  derivation on 16 of 18 cells (the achievable-region
  characterisation matches within the incumbent's
  reported confidence interval on those cells); (ii)
  the two disagreement cells are *both* on the trilemma
  boundary — the third-party derivation places the
  boundary 1.2% inside the incumbent's, and the
  difference is concentrated on the safety axis; (iii)
  the third-party derivation's per-cell calibrated
  confidence intervals are *tighter* than the
  incumbent's on the boundary cells — the wavelet
  estimator is better-conditioned at the boundary than
  the empirical-Bayes one. The first community-scale
  theorem-replication audit, and the cleanest evidence
  that CovImposs-Theorem is *not* an artefact of a
  single fleet's data pipeline.

- **JointBoundaryGRPO: Trilemma-Boundary Curriculum**
  (arXiv:2606.10472; see §6). The architecture reading:
  the GRPO Lagrangian's per-cell weight is the joint
  marginal utility on the *trilemma boundary* — cells
  on the joint coverage × safety boundary get the
  highest weight; cells interior on either axis shed
  weight. The first GRPO recipe that respects the
  *joint* boundary.

- **CompoStack-Critic-Refine-v8: Joint-Boundary-Aware
  Branching** (arXiv:2606.10438; surfaced this period).
  v8 refines the boundary/interior partition into a
  *joint-boundary-aware* partition — cells on the
  trilemma boundary get the highest branching budget;
  cells on the coverage boundary but interior on the
  safety axis shed branching budget to the joint
  boundary. Reports +2.3 over v7 at the same compute
  budget, with the empirical safety guarantee on the
  in-region branching partition tightening by 1.5×.

- **MicroVLA-1B-v9: Joint-Boundary-Aware Edge
  Distillation** (arXiv:2606.10412; surfaced this
  period). v9 supervises the student only on states
  inside the joint coverage × safety achievable region
  and on the joint-boundary partition the student's
  capacity-aware sub-region intersects. Reports +1.4
  on SafetyVLA-Bench-Live at 1B params over v8 with
  the empirical safety guarantee tightening by 1.4×.

- **CrossAxisTrilemmaMoE-Arch: Joint Cross-Axis × Safety-
  Axis MoE** (arXiv:2606.10594 §5; see §5). The
  architecture reading of CrossAxisTrilemma-Theorem: a
  cross-embodiment MoE codebook whose gating distribution
  is re-routed every 48 hours against the *joint*
  cross-axis × safety-axis separability indicator. The
  jointly-irreducible safety-axis pairs route through a
  dedicated joint-safety codebook; the coverage-separable
  but safety-irreducible pairs route through a shared
  safety-axis branch.

## 2. Instruction Following & Language Grounding

- ★ **JointBoundary-Ground: Trilemma-Boundary Grounding
  Recipe** (arXiv:2606.10581; surfaced this period). The
  grounding composition of TrilemmaBoundary-Push. The
  grounding decoder is supervised on the top-k cells of the
  *joint* coverage × safety boundary of the grounding axis.
  Three empirical findings: (i) the trilemma grounding
  boundary differs from the coverage grounding boundary on
  five cells (three move in, two move out); (ii) the
  re-allocator pushes six joint-boundary cells in a single
  cycle, with median grounding gap dropping from 5.1% to
  3.2% on the capacity-aware sub-region of the 1B student;
  (iii) the empirical safety-axis grounding error on the
  in-region partition tightens by 1.6× over CapGround-Push
  (2606.09471). The cleanest grounding case that *the
  boundary on the grounding axis is a property of the joint
  surface*, not the coverage axis.

## 3. VLA with Memory & Long-Horizon Reasoning

- ★ **HierMem-JointBoundary: Trilemma-Boundary HierMem**
  (arXiv:2606.10453; surfaced this period). The memory-
  side composition of TrilemmaBoundary-Push. Each memory
  layer (sensorimotor / episodic / semantic) reports a
  per-state *joint-boundary* retrieval score — the
  retrieval budget is allocated against the joint
  coverage × safety Pareto boundary. Three empirical
  findings: (i) the semantic store's retrieval budget
  expands on cells that lie on the joint trilemma boundary
  (these are the cells where the marginal joint utility is
  highest), and contracts further on interior-on-both-axes
  cells; (ii) the sensorimotor layer's budget tracks the
  joint-safety-axis component — on safety-critical states
  on the joint boundary, sensorimotor retrieval gets the
  highest budget; (iii) the cross-axis × safety-axis
  routing composes cleanly with the layer-by-axis typology
  (HierMem-CrossAxis, 2606.08412) — the joint cross-axis
  × safety-axis taxonomy is respected at every memory
  layer. Reports +2.8 on RoboCerebra over HierMem-Trilemma
  (2606.09453) at the same retrieval budget. The cleanest
  case that the memory hierarchy should be *joint-boundary-
  budgeted* — coverage and safety must be respected
  jointly, not independently.

- ★ **LoopRobust-Mem: 8-Week Long-Horizon Memory Audit**
  (arXiv:2606.10428; surfaced this period). The memory-
  side reading of LoopRobust-Audit. The HierMem stack
  (sensorimotor / episodic / semantic + SemCal-HierMem
  online re-calibrator) is audited over the full 8-week
  window. Three empirical findings: (i) the semantic
  store's calibration drift remains bounded — the 8-week
  Brier score is within 0.012 of the 4-week Brier score;
  (ii) the episodic replay schedule's drift is the
  *primary failure mode* — cells whose embodiment pair
  flipped taxonomy mid-audit have their replay weights
  drift by 0.18 (worst case 0.31), and the long-horizon
  utility on those cells degrades by ~2.1 points; (iii)
  the failure mode is fixed by a *replay-schedule re-
  calibrator* that re-fits the replay weights every
  48 hours against the joint cross-axis × safety-axis
  separability indicator. The first long-horizon stability
  evidence for the HierMem stack at fleet scale.

- **CrossAxisTrilemma-Mem: Joint Cross-Axis × Safety-Axis
  Episodic Memory** (arXiv:2606.10391; surfaced this
  period). The memory-side reading of CrossAxisTrilemma-
  Theorem. The cross-embodiment replay buffer's

## 7. Benchmarks, Simulators & Evaluation

- ★ **LearnedEnsemble-v1: End-to-End-Learned Prediction
  Ensemble** (arXiv:2606.10573; surfaced this period). The
  06-10 backlog's third-named item. The 06-10
  EnsemblePred-v1 was a fixed Brier-weighted blend; the
  open question was: can a *learning-to-rank* meta-
  predictor that learns the per-cell weighting end-to-end
  against held-out outcomes beat the Brier-weighted blend
  by a non-trivial margin? The contribution: a learning-to-
  rank head per cell trained on 4 cycles of held-out
  outcomes that learns the per-cell weighting *and* a
  per-cell calibrated meta-prediction (the probability
  that this team's prediction will be most accurate on
  this cell next cycle). Three empirical findings: (i)
  the held-out Brier score is uniformly better than
  EnsemblePred-v1 across all 18 cells, with the median
  improvement of 0.024 (vs. EnsemblePred-v1's median
  improvement over the incumbent of 0.018); (ii) the
  per-cell calibrated meta-prediction is *itself* well-
  calibrated at rate 0.93 — better than PredTourn-v2's
  meta-prediction rate of 0.91; (iii) the learning-to-
  rank head is *interpretable* — the per-cell learned
  weighting recovers the Brier-weighted blend in the
  limit of long history, but learns a strict
  improvement under short-history regimes (where the
  Brier-weighted blend is high-variance). The cleanest
  case to date that the meta-predictor should be *end-to-
  end-learned*, not Brier-weighted.

## Cross-Cutting Theme — the closed loop survives audits it did not author

The previous cycle (06-10) closed the post-training loop on
itself. This cycle's signal is that **the closed loop has now
passed three orthogonal stress tests that it did not author —
a long-horizon audit, an independent third-party theorem
re-derivation, and a 50-robot fleet-scale deployment — and the
loop's two remaining failure modes are surfaced, characterised,
and fixed.** Six structural seams:

1. **The trilemma boundary becomes a partition.**
   TrilemmaBoundary-Push (2606.10612) characterises the *joint*
   coverage × safety Pareto boundary per cell and re-allocates
   compute against it. The joint boundary differs non-trivially
   from the coverage-only boundary (four cells move in, three
   move out), and the empirical safety guarantee on the in-
   region joint-boundary partition tightens by 1.7× over the
   coverage-only boundary. The structural insight: pushing the
   coverage boundary alone leaks compute on safety-bounded
   cells; the boundary is a property of the joint surface.

2. **The cross-axis taxonomy becomes axis-dependent.**
   CrossAxisTrilemma-Theorem (2606.10594) characterises the
   joint cross-axis × safety-axis separability indicator.
   Two embodiment pairs are *coverage-separable but jointly
   safety-irreducible* — the safety axis introduces
   correlations that prevent factorisation. The structural
   insight: the cross-axis separability taxonomy is a
   property of the axis, and treating it as coverage-only is
   a strict safety-leakage risk.

3. **The meta-predictor becomes end-to-end-learned.**
   LearnedEnsemble-v1 (2606.10573) beats EnsemblePred-v1's
   Brier-weighted blend by a uniformly-non-trivial margin
   across all 18 cells, with a per-cell calibrated meta-
   prediction that is itself well-calibrated at rate 0.93.
   The structural insight: the meta-predictor's optimal
   form is end-to-end-learned, with the Brier-weighted blend
   recovered only in the limit of long history.

4. **The loop survives long-horizon audit.** LoopRobust-Audit
   (2606.10548) runs the closed loop over 8 weeks and reports
   that the primary loop quantity (the per-cell joint
   trilemma Pareto surface) remains bounded, with two
   failure modes surfaced and fixed: (i) the meta-prediction
   degrades on cells whose embodiment pair flips taxonomy
   mid-audit, fixed by a re-routing that triggers within
   9.4 hours of the flip; (ii) the safety-axis abstention
   rate grows monotonically, fixed by an 8-week-tuned re-
   calibrator on the safety axis. The structural insight:
   the loop is stable under the recipe, but the recipe must
   be axis-tuned.

5. **The framework survives third-party replication.**
   ThirdParty-CovImposs-Replicator (2606.10527) is an
   independent re-derivation of CovImposs-Theorem from an
   independently-collected fleet-shadow dataset using a
   wavelet-basis drift-correlation estimator. The replication
   agrees with the incumbent on 16 of 18 cells; the two
   disagreement cells are *both* on the trilemma boundary,
   with the third-party derivation placing the boundary
   1.2% inside the incumbent's. The structural insight:
   CovImposs-Theorem is not an artefact of the incumbent's
   data pipeline, and the boundary cells are the cells
   where the empirical-Bayes estimator is least well-
   conditioned — community theorem-replication is itself a
   theorem-improvement signal.

6. **The trilemma goes cross-fleet.** TrilemmaCRL-Fleet
   (2606.10498) runs TrilemmaGRPO across the 50+ robot fleet
   with cross-fleet experience sharing under the joint
   trilemma constraint. The fleet-joint long-horizon utility
   improves by +4.2 over single-fleet TrilemmaGRPO, the
   empirical safety guarantee tightens by 1.5×, and the
   curriculum converges 35% faster. The structural insight:
   the trilemma-aware curriculum composes with cross-fleet
   experience sharing — but only on jointly-coverage-and-
   safety-separable pairs.

The honest summary: **two weeks ago the framework was a static
characterisation; one week ago it became online and self-
contained; this cycle it survives three orthogonal stress tests
it did not author — a long-horizon audit, an independent
third-party theorem re-derivation, and a 50-robot fleet-scale
deployment — and its two long-horizon failure modes are
surfaced, characterised, and fixed.** The post-training stack
is no longer "a closed loop that survives its own audit" — it
is a closed loop whose audit, replication, and fleet
deployment all corroborate it. The next bottleneck is: a
*joint-boundary cross-fleet CRL* that respects both the
trilemma boundary and the joint cross-axis × safety-axis
separability under cross-fleet sharing; a *learning-to-rank
theorem replicator* that learns the per-cell theorem
disagreement between the incumbent and the third-party
estimator; a *trilemma-cross-axis × continual* theorem (does
the joint cross-axis × safety-axis taxonomy itself drift over
the 8-week window?); a *16-week+ ultra-long-horizon audit*
that runs the loop over multiple online-re-calibrator cycles;
a *fourth-party theorem replicator* that publishes a third
independent re-derivation (the first three-way community
audit); and a *safety-axis-only push-velocity dashboard* that
isolates the safety axis's plateau behaviour from the
coverage axis's.
