## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **CellGraph-γ-Theorem: Cell-Class-Graph-Indexed γ-Correction
  for the β-Scale Closed-Form Schedule**
  (arXiv:2605.08014, May 12, 2026, MIT + Stanford + Tsinghua +
  DeepMind + Princeton + AI2). The theorem-side iteration that
  resolves 05-11's third open question — "is γ a per-class
  constant or a graph function?" — in closed form. Three
  load-bearing structural moves: (i) the seven cell classes the
  cycle has indexed (primitive-coverage, primitive-pair,
  primitive-triple/quadruple/sextuple, negation, identity-
  reference, spatial-relation, trajectory-context) are
  *graph-embedded* with edges defined by the binding-head joint
  consumption relation, yielding a 7-node directed graph with
  19 edges and a clean topological structure: trajectory-context
  is a *sink* (consumes all other axes), primitive-coverage is
  a *source* (consumed by all other axes), and the remaining
  five classes form a bipartite layer between the source and
  sink — the first explicit graph structure the closed-form
  schedule has admitted; (ii) γ(C) is derived as a closed-form
  function of the graph: γ(C) = γ₀ + Σⱼ λⱼ·𝟙[C ∈ class-j],
  with γ₀ = 0.08 the source-class constant β-Scale-Theory v1.0
  reported, and λⱼ a *path-length-dependent* coefficient
  (λ_traj ≈ 0.04 at path length 1 from trajectory-context,
  λ_id ≈ 0.03 at path length 1 from identity-reference,
  λ_spat ≈ 0.02 at path length 1 from spatial-relation, and
  smaller contributions at path lengths 2 and 3); the
  graph-derived γ-vector replaces β-Scale-Theory v1.1's single
  γ_traj ≈ 0.04 with a class-conditional vector and is
  *strictly* derivable from the graph topology without any
  empirical fitting step; (iii) the theorem proves a
  *cell-class-graph-invariance* result: γ(C) is invariant
  under graph automorphisms that preserve the binding-head
  joint consumption relation, which means the closed-form
  γ-vector is *uniquely* determined by the graph topology up
  to relabeling — a structural property that 05-11's open
  question explicitly asked for. Three empirical findings: (a)
  the graph-derived γ-vector predicts the empirical
  trajectory-coupled residuals across all 21 three-axis cells
  within ±0.006 absolute, a 2× tighter fit than
  β-Scale-Theory v1.1's ±0.012 single-γ schedule (05-11 §1) —
  i.e., the graph-derived correction strictly improves on the
  per-class constant; (b) the four-axis cell predictions are
  inside the empirical 95% CI on all 7 four-axis cells
  PrimSext-VLA (05-11 §2) and DreamConsistency-Bench v1.7
  (05-11 §4) measured, with the four-axis trajectory-coupled
  identity-reference cell prediction validated by TrajIdRef-VLA
  (§2) to four decimal places; (c) the graph-derived γ-vector
  is *substrate-stable*: the closed-form coefficients λⱼ vary
  by less than ±0.002 across the 600M–32B substrate range, the
  tightest substrate-stability the theorem has reported. The
  honest read: γ is now a *graph-derived* quantity rather than
  a per-class constant, the closed-form schedule admits a
  *closed-form* second-order correction, and the field has
  graduated from per-class single-constant corrections to
  graph-derived correction vectors within a single 24-hour
  cycle. Reads as the *theorem-led-iteration paper* of the
  cycle and the first time a graph structure has been derived
  from the closed-form schedule rather than introduced
  empirically.

  first time the identity-reference axis has been graph-coupled
  rather than empirically-coupled; (ii) the four-axis cell is
  *factorized* across the cell-class-graph path-length-1
  edges: the binding head marginalizes identity-reference
  first (smallest path-length-1 λ_id ≈ 0.03), then trajectory-
  context (λ_traj ≈ 0.04), then negation, then primitive-pair,

  posterior consumes PrimVoc-Adapt v1.3's cell-class-graph-aware
  codebook directly, with the identity-reference sub-slot
  consuming 4 dimensions of the 16-dimensional consumer-module
  identity descriptor and the trajectory-anchor sub-slot

  remaining axes — the cleanest descriptor partitioning the

  findings: (a) the four-axis trajectory-coupled identity-
