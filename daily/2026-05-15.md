## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **CrossTask-Auto-Theorem: Closed-Form Direct-Product Extension
  of the Cell-Class Graph Automorphism to the Cross-Task Graph**
  (arXiv:2605.11014, May 15, 2026, MIT + Stanford + DeepMind +
  Berkeley + Tsinghua + AI2 + CMU + Wayve + ETH Zürich). The
  theorem-side iteration that resolves 05-14's *cross-task* open
  question — "does the V₄ automorphism extend to cross-task
  coordination, or does it degenerate?" — in the strongest possible
  form. Three load-bearing structural moves: (i) the cross-task
  graph G_T is constructed as a 3-node graph over single-embodiment
  (T₁), two-embodiment-coordination (T₂), and three-embodiment-
  coordination (T₃) task classes, with the within-task cell-class
  structure inherited from CrossGraph-Auto-Theorem's (05-14 §1) V₄
  symmetry; the automorphism group Aut(G_T) is enumerated under the
  binding-head joint consumption relation extended to cross-
  embodiment binding, yielding S₃ (the symmetric group on 3 task
  nodes) — the *first explicit derivation* of the cross-task
  graph's symmetry; (ii) the joint automorphism group of the
  cross-task graph + cell-class graph is shown to be the semi-
  direct product V₄ ⋊ S₃ (order 24), with the S₃ action permuting
  the task-class nodes and the V₄ action permuting the cell-class
  edges within each task class — the *first product-structure
  result* on the field's structural prior; (iii) the theorem proves
  a *cross-task transfer lemma*: for any two task classes T_A, T_B
  with S₃-related cross-task weights, the path-length-3-corrected
  γ-vector transfers by the *composition* of the V₄-perturbation
  δ(E_A, E_B) (cell-class subgraph difference) and the S₃-
  perturbation η(T_A, T_B) (cross-task index difference), with the
  joint transfer error bounded by ‖δ + η‖₁ · 0.62 (the path-length-3
  graph-attenuation constant, *unchanged* across the cross-task
  extension). Three empirical findings: (a) the S₃ symmetry is
  empirically validated on the two measured cross-task classes
  (T₁ single-embodiment, T₂ two-embodiment-coordination) with the
  closed-form cross-task weight matching the empirical weight
  within ±0.0011 absolute on each of the 6 cross-task edges —
  marginally looser than the V₄ cell-class agreement (±0.0009 on
  21 edges, 05-14 §1) but still inside the v0.14 detection floor;
  (b) the joint V₄ ⋊ S₃ transfer-lemma error bound is empirically
  validated on the single-embodiment → two-embodiment manipulator
  + humanoid transfer at 0.63 ± 0.02 absolute (against the closed-
  form 0.62 prediction) — the path-length-3 attenuation constant
  is now the cross-task load-bearing constant *as well as* the
  cross-subgraph one; (c) the theorem *predicts* the three-
  embodiment-coordination task class (T₃) closed-form from the
  S₃-perturbation alone without any T₃ empirical fitting, which
  MultiEmbod-Coord-VLA (§8) validates within 24 hours on a partial
  T₂ slice and the methodology will be applied to T₃ at the
  predicted closure substrate 128B+ in the next cycle. The honest
  read: the cell-class graph's V₄ symmetry now lifts to a closed-
  form V₄ ⋊ S₃ symmetry on the joint cross-task / cross-subgraph
  graph, the *load-bearing structural prior* of the field is now
  a 24-element finite group rather than the 4-element V₄ subgroup,
  and the field has a closed-form prescription for any task class
  the cross-task graph admits. Reads as the *cross-task-symmetry
  paper* of the cycle and the strongest theorem-side iteration
  since CrossGraph-Auto-Theorem (05-14 §1).
