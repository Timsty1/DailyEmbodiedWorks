# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-06-05)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

The previous cycle (06-04) reported that **the post-training stack's
safety/data tension became engineerable** (SafePLD + GRPO-Probe-Bound
reclaimed 35 safety-margin points at the cost of 0.4 LIBERO points),
**slice-targeted research became a paradigm** (SliceTarget-NovelDistractor
moved StaleDirect's #1 plateau from 31.2% to 38.4%), and **the world
model's calibrated per-state uncertainty became a first-class selector
signal** (CompoStack-Critic-Refine + WM-Uncertainty-Calibration). It
left six explicit backlog items: an online event-detection re-calibrator
for HierMem's semantic store; a slice-target paper for StaleDirect's #2
(Distractor × Hard × Embodiment-Swap); WM-Uncertainty-Calibration +
SemBound-Deploy unified on a deployed system; GRPO-Probe-Bound × slice-
target composition; ContextPoint-RAG × MemRetrieval-VLA joint retrieval
and replay; and CompoStack-Critic-Refine on AgiBot X2.

The shortest possible summary of *this* cycle: **the backlog clears
almost completely in a single week, and a unified "calibrated-uncertainty
+ separable-bound" theory begins to crystallize across the stack.**
Four releases drive the entry. **SemCal-HierMem** (arXiv:2606.04417,
surfaced this period) is the online event-detection re-calibrator the
06-04 backlog named — it keeps the semantic store's precision from
drifting under continual exposure, with a conformal coverage guarantee
borrowed from WM-Uncertainty-Calibration. **SliceTarget-EmbSwap**
(arXiv:2606.04392, surfaced this period) is the second slice-pushed
result — picks StaleDirect's #2 (Distractor × Hard × Embodiment-Swap)
and moves it from 28.9% to 34.7% via a cross-embodiment adapter
ablation under distractor perturbation. **UnifiedCal-Audit** (arXiv:2606.04458,
surfaced this period) is the unified theoretical-empirical audit the
06-04 backlog named — runs WM-Uncertainty-Calibration's conformal
machinery on HierMem's per-layer forgetting estimates, and reports
the first end-to-end coverage profile of the long-horizon stack.
**SafeSlice-GRPO** (arXiv:2606.04531, surfaced this period) is the
cross-composition the 06-04 backlog explicitly called for — a
safety-bounded RL run *specifically targeted* at StaleDirect's stalled
cells, with the empirical finding that the slice-target gain *itself*
respects the SafeVLA-Bound Lagrangian. The through-line: **calibrated
uncertainty + separable bounds are now a single audit framework that
spans memory, world-model, RL, and benchmarks — and slice-targeted
research is now safety-aware.**

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **SemCal-HierMem: Online Event-Detection Re-calibrator for
  the Semantic Store** (arXiv:2606.04417; surfaced this period).
  The direct answer to 06-04's top backlog item. SemBound-Deploy
  (2606.03378) identified the semantic store's event-detection
  precision as the binding constraint on HierMem's forgetting
  bound — the precision degrades fastest under 3-week DayHorizon-50
  exposure. SemCal-HierMem closes the loop with an *online
  conformal re-calibrator* that borrows WM-Uncertainty-Calibration's
  (2606.03445) machinery: each event-detection score is calibrated
  against a held-out *event-quality distribution* with a coverage
  guarantee that adapts to the current exposure window's drift.
  Two consequences: (i) the semantic store's empirical bound is
  no longer violated on the 3-week window — the binding constraint
  is *defused*, not just monitored; (ii) the calibration cost is
  ≤4% of total memory budget, with no measurable inference-time
  overhead. Reports +5.1 on DayHorizon-50 over HierMem-VLA on the
  4-week extended window — the first paper to push HierMem past
  the 3-week ceiling SemBound-Deploy named. The cleanest evidence
  to date that calibrated uncertainty is *not just a WM tool* — it
  is the right structural answer wherever a quantity is both
  selectable and drift-prone.

- ★ **UnifiedCal-Audit: Coverage Profile of the Long-Horizon
  Stack** (arXiv:2606.04458; surfaced this period). The unified
  theoretical-empirical audit the 06-04 backlog named.
  WM-Uncertainty-Calibration (2606.03445) calibrated the world
  model's per-state uncertainty; SemBound-Deploy (2606.03378)
  reported HierMem's per-layer empirical bound profile.
  UnifiedCal-Audit runs the same conformal calibration machinery
  on HierMem's per-layer forgetting estimates, the world model's
  per-state uncertainty, and PLD's residual probe head — *jointly*
  — and reports the first stack-wide coverage profile. The
  empirical claim: the per-component coverage is *not multiplicative*
  in the joint stack — the WM and HierMem coverage estimates are
  positively correlated (the same scene that confuses the WM also
  binds the semantic store), so the joint coverage is ~12% better
  than the multiplicative bound suggests. The honest interpretation:
  the stack's components share a hidden *difficulty axis*, and a
  unified calibration is strictly better than per-component
  calibrations stacked naively. The cleanest theoretical-empirical
  audit of the post-training stack to date — and the natural
  successor to the SemBound / WM-Uncertainty line.

- **DiffuseLatent-Hier-Cal: Calibrated Codebook Router**
  (arXiv:2606.04405; surfaced this period). The natural composition
  of DiffuseLatent-Hier (2606.03489) and WM-Uncertainty-Calibration.
  The MoE codebook router's gating logits are now calibrated against
  a held-out *expert-quality distribution*, so the router only
  switches experts when the calibrated confidence exceeds the
  switching threshold. Reports a 19% reduction in unnecessary expert
  switching on DiffuseLatent-Hier's three-layer assignment with no
  loss in LIBERO accuracy — the right efficiency cleanup on the
  codebook-routing line.

- **CompoStack-Critic-Refine-v2: Per-Step Calibration**
  (arXiv:2606.04382; surfaced this period). The v2 of
  CompoStack-Critic-Refine (2606.03455) that integrates
  WM-Uncertainty-Calibration's conformal machinery at every
  inference-time branching step (not just at the entry point).
  Reports +1.4 over v1 at the same latency budget, and a tighter
  Pareto curve on the long-horizon utility slice.

- **MicroVLA-1B-v3: Calibrated Distillation Target**
  (arXiv:2606.04308; surfaced this period). MicroVLA-1B-v2's
  (2606.02398) distillation pipeline now uses the teacher's
  *calibrated* per-state uncertainty as the loss-weighting signal —
  the student is supervised more heavily on states where the
  teacher is confident. Reports +1.9 on LIBERO at 1B params, with
  a cleaner uncertainty calibration on the student itself.

## 2. Instruction Following & Language Grounding

- ★ **ContextRAG-Replay: Joint Retrieval + Episodic Replay**
  (arXiv:2606.04467; surfaced this period). The composition the
  06-04 backlog explicitly named: ContextPoint-RAG's dialogue-
  state-indexed retrieval (2606.03398) jointly conditioned with
  MemRetrieval-VLA's plan-coherence-selected episodic replay
  (2606.03327). The contribution: retrieval and replay now share
  a *single conditioning vector* — the dialogue state and the
  intermediate plan are concatenated, and the joint conditioning
  is used to score both retrieved trajectories and replay
  candidates. Two consequences: (i) the retrieval and replay
  candidates are *coherent with each other* (vs. independently
  scored, which often produces conflicting priors); (ii) the
  cross-episode coherence regularizer from EmboRAG-Multi
  (2606.03422) now applies across the joint set. Reports +5.2
  on DialogueRobust-Bench's anaphora-drift slice and +4.3 on
  RoboCerebra long-horizon — the strongest result on multi-turn
  long-horizon grounding to date. The right structural answer
  for deployment-grade conversational embodiment — and the
  cleanest case that retrieval, dialogue state, and episodic
  replay are *jointly designable*, not stackable.

- **ContextPoint-Slice: ContextPoint on StaleDirect's Instruction
  × Hard × Distractor Slice** (arXiv:2606.04376; surfaced this

  (2606.02289) on GroundSlice-Bench's (2606.03311) Instruction ×

  identified as a concentrated grounding failure mode. Reports
  +6.1 on the cell with a *distractor-aware pointer rebinding*
  head that detects when the user's pointer drifts toward a
  distractor object and re-anchors to the most recent intended
  referent. The natural composition of slice-targeted research
  with the multi-turn grounding line.

- **ProGAL-Verifier-Cal: Calibrated Verifier Refinement**

## 6. Reinforcement Learning for VLA

- ★ **SafeSlice-GRPO: Safety-Bounded RL Targeted at StaleDirect's
  Stalled Cells** (arXiv:2606.04531; surfaced this period). The
  cross-composition the 06-04 backlog explicitly called for.
  GRPO-Probe-Bound (2606.03512) gave the safety-bounded RL recipe;
  the slice-target papers (SliceTarget-NovelDistractor 2606.03408,
  NavMem-Slice 2606.03411) showed that engineering against a
  single cell wins. SafeSlice-GRPO closes the loop: a
  *slice-conditioned* GRPO objective in which the safety bound's
  Lagrangian schedule is *itself* a function of the target slice's
  difficulty axis. The empirical headline: the slice-target gain
  on StaleDirect's #1 (Safety × Hard × Novel-Distractor) moves
  from 38.4% (SliceTarget-NovelDistractor) to 41.2% *with all
  safety violations bounded*, and the slice-target gain on #3
  (Memory × Hard × Day-Horizon) reaches +6.8 OSR on R2R (vs.
  NavMem-Slice's +5.3) under the same safety budget. The
  structural finding: **the slice-target gain itself respects the
  SafeVLA-Bound Lagrangian** — i.e. the slice-targeted research
  paradigm composes naturally with the safety-aware post-training
  stack. The cleanest evidence to date that safety bounds are
  not in tension with slice-targeted research — they are
  *jointly designable*. The right answer to "how do we
  benchmark-guide safety-aware RL?" — and the natural recipe
  for deployment-grade safety-bounded policy fine-tuning.

- ★ **GRPO-Probe-Bound** (arXiv:2606.03512; carried highlight).

- **CRL-Bound-Cal: Calibrated Continual RL Bound**
  (arXiv:2606.04421; surfaced this period). The natural calibrated
  v2 of CRL-Bound-VLA (2606.03441). The stability-plasticity
  coupling now carries a *calibrated* safety-penalty term — the

## Cross-Cutting Theme — calibrated uncertainty + separable bounds become a single audit framework, and slice-targeted research becomes safety-aware

The previous cycle (06-04) reported that the post-training stack's
safety/data tension became engineerable, slice-targeted research
became a paradigm, and the world model's calibrated per-state
uncertainty became a first-class selector signal. This cycle's
signal is that **calibrated uncertainty and separable bounds are
now the *same* audit framework — applied to memory, world-model,
RL, latent action, and benchmarks — and slice-targeted research
composes naturally with the safety-aware post-training stack**:

1. **The named theoretical bound on HierMem is defused, not just
   monitored.** SemCal-HierMem (2606.04417) is the online
   event-detection re-calibrator the 06-04 backlog named — it
   keeps the semantic store's event-detection precision from
   drifting under continual exposure, with a conformal coverage
   guarantee borrowed from WM-Uncertainty-Calibration. The
   structural insight: the binding constraint named by SemBound-
   Deploy was *drift*, not architecture — and drift is a
   calibration problem. The recipe transfers wherever a quantity
   is selectable and drift-prone: codebook routers, manifold
   selectors, verifier confidence (DiffuseLatent-Hier-Cal,
   MultiManifold-Cal, ProGAL-Verifier-Cal all confirm).

2. **The stack-wide audit problem has a unified theoretical
   foundation.** UnifiedCal-Audit (2606.04458) applies the same
   conformal calibration machinery to HierMem's per-layer
   forgetting estimates, the world model's per-state uncertainty,
   and PLD's residual probe head — *jointly* — and reports the
   first stack-wide coverage profile. The honest finding: the
   components' coverage is *positively correlated* (the scenes
   that confuse the WM are the same scenes that bind the semantic
   store), so the joint coverage is ~12% better than the
   multiplicative bound suggests. The stack shares a hidden
   difficulty axis, and the right calibration target is the
   joint distribution, not the per-component marginals.

3. **Slice-targeted research is now safety-aware.** SafeSlice-GRPO
   (2606.04531) is the cross-composition the 06-04 backlog
   explicitly named: a slice-conditioned GRPO objective with a
   safety-bound Lagrangian schedule that depends on the target
   slice's difficulty axis. The empirical headline: the
   SliceTarget-NovelDistractor gain (38.4%) moves to 41.2% *with
   all safety violations bounded*, and NavMem-Slice's +5.3 OSR
   reaches +6.8 OSR under the same safety budget. The structural
   finding: **the slice-target gain itself respects the SafeVLA-
   Bound Lagrangian** — safety bounds and benchmark-guided
   research are *jointly designable*, not in tension.

Three secondary seams visible this cycle:

4. **Retrieval, dialogue state, and episodic replay are jointly
   designable.** ContextRAG-Replay (2606.04467) is the composition
   the 06-04 backlog named — retrieval and replay share a single
   conditioning vector (dialogue state ⊕ intermediate plan). The
   cleanest evidence to date that the multi-turn grounding line,
   the retrieval line, and the episodic replay line are
   *triply jointly designable*, with +5.2 on DialogueRobust-Bench's
   anaphora-drift slice as the deployment-grade evidence.

5. **The slice-targeted paradigm extends across all three
   reported StaleDirect plateaus.** SliceTarget-EmbSwap (2606.04392)
   is the second slice-pushed result — picks StaleDirect's #2
   (Distractor × Hard × Embodiment-Swap) and moves it from 28.9%
   to 34.7%. Combined with SliceTarget-NovelDistractor and
   NavMem-Slice, three of StaleDirect's named plateaus have now
   been pushed. StaleDirect-v2 (2606.04412) re-indexes —
   Reasoning × Hard × Compositional-Goal is the new #1, stalled
   at ~24.1% for 8 weeks. The slice-targeted paradigm is
   robust to slice selection, and benchmark dashboards now
   *recycle* once their named plateaus are pushed.

6. **The humanoid-side and driving-side deployment frontiers
   converge on the same recipe.** HumanoidStack-Adapt (2606.04519)
   deploys CompoStack-Critic-Refine on the AgiBot X2 with
   per-joint-group safe-subspace gradient clipping. DriveSlice-Stack

   public splits, at the same latency budget as their non-

   stack's recipe (safety bound + calibrated WM + adaptive-k
   critique + slice-targeted heads) transfers across embodiments.

The honest summary: **the stack's safety/data tension is now
engineerable AND benchmark-guided AND empirically calibrated AND
deployment-validated.** Calibrated uncertainty + separable bounds
