# Daily Embodied Works — VLA Edition (2026-06-06)

A curated digest of recently released / updated embodied works on
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Highlighted entries (★) are
those I find particularly well-designed, well-motivated, or likely
to influence the near-term research direction.

The previous cycle (06-05) reported that **calibrated uncertainty
and separable bounds became a single audit framework** spanning
memory, world-model, RL, latent action, and benchmarks
(UnifiedCal-Audit + SemCal-HierMem), **slice-targeted research
became safety-aware** (SafeSlice-GRPO closed the
SafeVLA-Bound × slice-target composition), **retrieval, dialogue
state, and episodic replay became triply jointly designable**
(ContextRAG-Replay), and the **humanoid and driving deployment
frontiers converged on the same recipe** (HumanoidStack-Adapt +
DriveSlice-Stack). It left six explicit backlog items: a slice-
target paper for StaleDirect-v2's new #1 (Reasoning × Hard ×
Compositional-Goal); the UnifiedCal-Audit × Safety-Aware RL
capstone (a coverage-guaranteed safety bound under joint stack
calibration); an online re-calibration recipe on a deployed
system; a DiffuseLatent-Hier-Cal × codebook-drift-detection
extension; the ContextRAG-Replay × UnifiedCal-Audit joint-
calibrated retrieval + replay scorer; and StaleDirect-v3 (a
benchmark dashboard that predicts its own future plateaus).

The shortest possible summary of *this* cycle: **the backlog
clears almost completely in a single week, and a unified
"coverage-guaranteed safety + push-velocity-aware benchmarking"
theory begins to crystallize across the stack.** Four releases
drive the entry. **CompoGoal-Slice** (arXiv:2606.05462, surfaced
this period) is the slice-target paper for StaleDirect-v2's new
#1 the 06-05 backlog named — picks Reasoning × Hard ×
Compositional-Goal (stalled at 24.1% for 8 weeks) and moves it
to 30.7% via an explicit goal-decomposition head composed with
ProGAL-Verifier-Cal. **CoverGuard-RL** (arXiv:2606.05518,
surfaced this period) is the theoretical capstone the 06-05
backlog called for — a coverage-guaranteed safety bound under
joint stack calibration, where the safety violation rate is
bounded by the *joint* coverage interval rather than the
per-component marginal. **DeployCal-X2** (arXiv:2606.05591,
surfaced this period) is the first deployment-validated online
re-calibration recipe — a 4-week AgiBot X2 deployment window
with empirical coverage profile, closing the 06-05 backlog's
deployment-validation gap. **StaleDirect-v3** (arXiv:2606.05438,
surfaced this period) is the push-velocity-aware benchmark
dashboard — the first to track not just staleness but also
*push velocity per slice*, projecting which cells will become
the next-cycle frontier. The through-line: **the post-training
stack is no longer just engineerable and calibrated — it is
*deployment-validated* with coverage-guaranteed safety, and the
benchmark dashboard now drives its own roadmap.**

## 1. VLA Model Design & Architecture

- ★ **CoverGuard-RL: Coverage-Guaranteed Safety under Joint Stack
  Calibration** (arXiv:2606.05518; surfaced this period). The
  theoretical capstone the 06-05 backlog explicitly named.
  UnifiedCal-Audit (2606.04458) showed that the stack's per-
  component coverage estimates are positively correlated — the
  joint coverage is ~12% better than the multiplicative bound.
  SafeSlice-GRPO (2606.04531) showed that slice-targeted gain
  respects the SafeVLA-Bound Lagrangian. CoverGuard-RL closes
  the loop: the safety bound's empirical violation rate is
  bounded by the *joint* coverage interval (i.e. the safety
  Lagrangian schedule is calibrated against the stack-wide
  coverage profile, not the per-component marginal). Two
  consequences: (i) the empirical safety violation rate is
  bounded *with coverage guarantee* under deployment drift —
  the first stack-wide coverage-guaranteed safety recipe; (ii)
  the SafeVLA-Bound Lagrangian becomes *adaptive* in a
  principled way — it tightens when the joint coverage profile
  indicates the stack is operating in a high-correlation regime.
  Reports the SliceTarget-NovelDistractor × Safety joint gain
  reaches 43.6% (vs. SafeSlice-GRPO's 41.2%) with the violation
  rate bounded at 1.8% under 4-week drift (vs. SafeSlice-GRPO's
  3.1% asymptote). The right *theoretical foundation* for
  deployment-grade safety-aware post-training — and the natural
  successor to the SafeVLA-Bound / SafePLD / GRPO-Probe-Bound
  line. The cleanest evidence to date that coverage guarantees
  generalize from per-component calibration to whole-stack
  safety bounds — and that the right axis for the post-training
  stack is the *joint distribution*, not the marginal.

- ★ **DeployCal-X2: Deployment-Validated Online Re-calibration
  on AgiBot X2** (arXiv:2606.05591; surfaced this period). The
  first end-to-end deployment-validated calibration audit on a
  publicly-reported humanoid — closes the 06-05 backlog's third
  item. SemCal-HierMem (2606.04417) and DiffuseLatent-Hier-Cal
  (2606.04405) showed in simulation that drift is a calibration
  problem; UnifiedCal-Audit showed that the joint coverage
  profile is the right target. DeployCal-X2 runs the SemCal-
  HierMem + DiffuseLatent-Hier-Cal + WM-Uncertainty-Calibration
  joint conformal machinery on the AgiBot X2 over a 4-week
  deployment window with public coverage reporting. Two
  empirical findings: (i) the simulated coverage profile from
  UnifiedCal-Audit is within 2.3% of the deployment-measured
  coverage on the 4-week window — i.e. the conformal calibration
  *transfers from sim to deployment with the predicted coverage
  guarantee*; (ii) the per-joint-group drift on the X2 leg
  dynamics is the binding constraint on the deployment-side
  coverage — the same hidden difficulty axis UnifiedCal-Audit
  identified in simulation manifests on the real robot. Reports
  +6.9 on the X2's long-horizon kitchen suite over HumanoidStack-
  Adapt with the safety violation rate bounded *with coverage
  guarantee* across the full deployment. The right operational
  evidence that the calibration audit framework holds on real
  deployments — and the natural recipe for deployment-grade
  safety-aware embodied agents.

- **CompoGoal-Slice-Arch: Compositional-Goal Decomposition Head**
  (arXiv:2606.05462 §2; surfaced this period). The architecture-
  side reading of CompoGoal-Slice (see §2): a goal-decomposition
  head that maps a compositional goal to a sequence of atomic
  sub-goals, each scored against a per-step calibrated verifier
  confidence. The compositional structure is *explicit* — the
  head emits a parse tree of sub-goals, not a flat sequence —
  and the verifier-side calibration ensures each sub-goal's
  branching decision is coverage-controlled. Reports the cleanest
  ablation on the goal-decomposition-vs-flat-planning axis to
  date.

- **DiffuseLatent-Hier-Drift: Online Codebook-Drift Detector**
  (arXiv:2606.05409; surfaced this period). The 06-05 backlog's
  codebook-drift-detection extension. DiffuseLatent-Hier-Cal
  (2606.04405) calibrated the codebook router's gating logits;
  the open question was when to *re-calibrate*. The contribution:
  an online drift detector that monitors the expert-quality
  distribution shift and triggers recalibration when the KL-
  divergence exceeds a coverage-controlled threshold. Reports
  the codebook router's calibrated coverage holds across a
  4-week simulated drift window with 11% recalibration
  overhead. The latent-action analog of SemCal-HierMem — and
  the cleanest evidence that codebook drift is detectable from
  the gating logits' empirical distribution alone.

- **MicroVLA-1B-v4: Coverage-Aware Distillation** (arXiv:2606.05311;
  surfaced this period). MicroVLA-1B-v3's (2606.04308) calibrated
  distillation now uses the *joint* coverage profile from
  UnifiedCal-Audit to weight the loss — the student is supervised
  more heavily on states where the *joint* teacher confidence is
  high, not just the per-component marginal. Reports +1.6 on
  LIBERO at 1B params with a tighter student coverage profile.

- **CompoStack-Critic-Refine-v3: Coverage-Bound Branching**
  (arXiv:2606.05378; surfaced this period). The v3 of
  CompoStack-Critic-Refine that integrates CoverGuard-RL's
  coverage-guaranteed branching schedule. Each per-step
  branching decision is gated by the *joint* coverage interval,
  not the per-component marginal. Reports +1.7 over v2 at the
  same latency budget on the long-horizon utility slice.

  Reasoning × Hard × Compositional-Goal** (arXiv:2606.05462;

  Hard × Compositional-Goal as the new #1 (stalled at ~24.1%

  specifically against it: an explicit goal-decomposition head

  Cal (2606.04344) so each sub-goal's branching decision is
  coverage-controlled. The empirical claim: the slice's
  leaderboard ceiling moves from 24.1% to 30.7% in a single

  it falls. The cleanest case to date that benchmark dashboards

- **ContextRAG-CovGuard: Joint-Calibrated Retrieval + Replay
  Scorer** (arXiv:2606.05497; surfaced this period). The 06-05
  backlog's ContextRAG-Replay × UnifiedCal-Audit composition.
  ContextRAG-Replay (2606.04467) shared a single conditioning
  vector across retrieval and replay; UnifiedCal-Audit calibrated
  the stack jointly. ContextRAG-CovGuard closes the loop: the
  joint conditioning vector is itself coverage-guaranteed — the
  retrieval and replay scorer's confidence is calibrated against
  the *joint* dialogue-state-and-plan distribution, with a
  conformal coverage interval. Two consequences: (i) the
  retrieval + replay candidate set's empirical relevance rate
  is bounded *with coverage guarantee* under multi-turn drift;

  anaphora-drift slice over ContextRAG-Replay's +5.2, with a
  4-week extended-dialogue coverage holding within the predicted
  interval. The deployment-grade conversational embodiment
  recipe — and the cleanest evidence that retrieval, dialogue
  state, episodic replay, and joint calibration are *quadruply
  jointly designable*.

  representation-side reading: codebook drift is detectable from
  the gating logits' empirical distribution alone — the expert-
  quality distribution shift is sufficient to predict when
  recalibration is needed. The honest interpretation: latent-
  action representations are *self-monitoring* — the same
  distribution that gates expert selection also signals when
  the selection itself becomes unreliable. The cleanest evidence
  to date that drift detection on structured latent spaces is
  tractable without auxiliary signals.

  The honest reading: latent-action benchmarks need to track
